## Objectives

- Take **useful notes** in long training days without trying to write everything.
- Turn each day into **5 key ideas**, **questions** and **flashcards** in the app's diary.
- Use **spaced reviews** (+1 day, +7 days) so the academy content stays in your memory.

## Explanation

### During the session: the Cornell-style page

Divide the page (paper or tablet) into three parts:

1. **Main notes (right, big)** – key words, definitions, **numbers** (ages, hours, ranks), examples. Short phrases, not full sentences.
2. **Cue column (left, narrow)** – after the session, write **questions** whose answers are in the notes: *"Who authorises a superintendent's extension?"*
3. **Summary (bottom)** – 2–3 lines in **your own words**.

Write **English words you don't know** in a different colour – they become **flashcards**.

:::es
**Método Cornell**: notas principales a la derecha (palabras clave, definiciones, **números**, ejemplos), columna de **preguntas** a la izquierda (se rellena después) y un **resumen** de 2–3 líneas abajo. Las palabras inglesas que no conozcas, en otro color → **flashcards**.
:::

### After the session: 20 minutes in the app

Open **Hoy → Diario de academia** and fill in:

- **Module** and **topic** of the day.
- **My notes** – a clean version of the important points (not everything).
- **5 key ideas** – in your own words. If you can't write them, you didn't understand → that's a question for tomorrow.
- **Questions to ask** – write them down and ask the trainer.
- **Flashcards** – 3 to 8 per day: definitions, numbers, words.

When you save, the app schedules reviews at **+1 day** and **+7 days**, and on **Sundays** it shows the **weekly summary**.

:::es
Después de clase, **20 minutos** en la app: módulo, tema, apuntes limpios, **5 ideas clave** con tus palabras, **dudas** y **3–8 flashcards**. Al guardar se programan repasos a **+1 día** y **+7 días**, y el **domingo** ves el resumen semanal.
:::

### Language tips

- If the trainer speaks fast, write **key nouns and numbers** only and complete later.
- Ask colleagues: *"Sorry, what was the time limit for…?"* – it's normal.
- Record new **phrasal verbs** and **collocations** you hear (*to hand over*, *to carry out a search*).

:::es
Si hablan rápido: apunta **sustantivos y números** y completa después. Preguntar es normal. Apunta **phrasal verbs** y expresiones nuevas.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Day 3: a session on **custody time limits**. → Key ideas: relevant time; 24 hours; reviews (first at 6 h, then every 9 h ⚠ VERIFICAR in Code C/PACE s. 40); superintendent to 36 h; magistrates to 96 h. Flashcards for each number.
2. You didn't understand **"adverse inference"**. → Write it in **Questions to ask**; tomorrow's +1 review reminds you.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to write **everything** → you stop listening.
- Never reviewing → by Friday, Monday is gone.
- Writing notes only in Spanish → the exam and assessments are in English.

## Summary

1. Cornell page: notes, questions, summary.
2. New English words in another colour → flashcards.
3. 20 minutes daily in the diary: 5 key ideas + questions + flashcards.
4. Reviews at +1 and +7 days.
5. Sunday: weekly summary.
