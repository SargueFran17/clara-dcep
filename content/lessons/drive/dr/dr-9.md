## Objectives

- Know the **drink-drive limits** in England and Wales and how they compare with Spain.
- Understand **drug driving** rules (specified drugs and limits).
- Know the strict **mobile phone** law since 2022.

## Explanation

### Drink driving – limits (England, Wales and Northern Ireland)

| Sample | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Breath** | **35 micrograms** of alcohol per **100 ml** of breath |
| **Blood** | **80 milligrams** per **100 ml** of blood |
| **Urine** | **107 milligrams** per **100 ml** of urine |

**Scotland** has lower limits (22 µg breath / 50 mg blood). **Spain**: 0.25 mg/l breath (= 25 µg/100 ml) and 0.5 g/l blood for most drivers – so the English limit is **higher** than Spain's, but **any** alcohol affects driving. The only safe advice: **none if you drive**.

Penalties for driving over the limit include a **minimum 12-month disqualification**, an unlimited fine and possible prison (⚠ VERIFICAR detalles). Refusing to provide a specimen without reasonable excuse is also an offence.

:::es
**Límites (Inglaterra y Gales)**: **35 µg/100 ml en aire espirado**, **80 mg/100 ml en sangre**, **107 mg/100 ml en orina**. Escocia es más estricta. España: 0,25 mg/l aire (= 25 µg/100 ml), así que el límite inglés es **más alto**, pero lo seguro es **no beber nada** si conduces. Pena: **retirada mínima de 12 meses**, multa y posible prisión. **Negarse** a la prueba también es delito.
:::

### Drug driving

- It's an offence to drive while **unfit** through drugs (any drug, including medicines), and to drive with **certain specified drugs above set limits** in your blood (Road Traffic Act 1988, s. 5A) – e.g. cannabis (THC), cocaine, and some **prescription** medicines at higher limits.
- Police can use **roadside drug screening** (saliva) and require a **blood** sample.
- If you take prescribed medicine **as directed** and are not impaired, there's a **medical defence** for the specified-limit offence (⚠ VERIFICAR).

:::es
**Drogas**: delito conducir **no apta** por drogas (incluidas medicinas) y con **ciertas drogas por encima de límites** en sangre (RTA 1988 s. 5A), como cannabis o cocaína, y algunos medicamentos. Hay **test de saliva** en carretera y análisis de **sangre**. Si tomas un medicamento **según la receta** y no estás afectada, existe una **defensa médica** (⚠ verifica).
:::

### Mobile phones (since March 2022)

- It's **illegal to hold and use** a phone (or sat nav/tablet) **for any purpose** while driving – including **stopped at traffic lights** or **queuing in traffic**, taking photos, scrolling music.
- Penalty: **6 penalty points** and a **£200** fixed penalty (⚠ VERIFICAR); **new drivers** can lose their licence.
- **Hands-free** is allowed (cradle, voice), but you can still be prosecuted for **careless driving** if you're distracted.
- Exceptions: calling **999/112** in a genuine emergency when it's unsafe/impractical to stop; contactless payment while **stationary** at a till; remote-controlled parking.

:::es
**Móvil**: desde marzo de 2022 está **prohibido tenerlo en la mano y usarlo para cualquier cosa** al conducir, **también parada en un semáforo o en un atasco**. Sanción: **6 puntos y £200** (⚠ verifica). El **manos libres** está permitido, pero si te distrae puede ser **conducción descuidada**. Excepciones: llamar al **999** en emergencia, pago sin contacto **detenida** en una caja, aparcamiento remoto.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Two pints after work, then driving home. → Could be **over the limit** and impaired; don't.
2. You check a message at red lights. → **Offence** (6 points, £200).
3. A driver says she took prescribed codeine as directed. → Consider impairment; medical defence may apply to the specified-limit offence.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Thinking a "higher" UK limit means it's safe to drink.
- Holding the phone "just at the lights".

## Summary

1. Breath 35 µg / blood 80 mg / urine 107 mg (E&W); Scotland lower.
2. Over the limit: minimum 12-month ban.
3. Drug driving: unfit (any drug) or specified drugs over limits (s. 5A).
4. Phone in hand for any purpose = offence (6 points, £200).
5. Hands-free allowed, but distraction → careless driving.
