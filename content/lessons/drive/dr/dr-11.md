> 🔗 Relacionado con el capítulo NIE **2.14 (Offences involving standards of driving)**: obligación de parar y de informar tras un accidente. **Comprueba con tu edición 2027.**

## Objectives

- Know what to do at a **road traffic collision (RTC)** as a driver (and as an officer).
- Understand the legal **duty to stop and give details** and the **duty to report** (Road Traffic Act 1988, s. 170).
- Know when to call **999** and what to record.

## Explanation

### When does s. 170 apply?

When, owing to the presence of a **mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place**, an accident occurs causing:

- **personal injury** to a person **other than the driver**; or
- **damage** to **another vehicle** or to **property** (other than the driver's own vehicle/trailer) – including property on or near the road; or
- injury to an **animal** (horse, cattle, ass, mule, sheep, pig, goat or dog) not in the vehicle.

:::es
Se aplica cuando, por la presencia de un **vehículo de motor en una vía o lugar público**, hay un accidente con: **lesiones** a alguien **distinto del conductor**; **daños** a **otro vehículo** o a **propiedad** ajena; o lesiones a ciertos **animales** (caballo, ganado, burro, mula, oveja, cerdo, cabra o **perro**) que no iban en el vehículo.
:::

### The duties (s. 170)

1. The driver **must stop**.
2. If required by any person having **reasonable grounds**, give their **name and address**, the **vehicle owner's** name and address, and the **registration** number.
3. If they **don't give** their name and address at the scene, they must **report the accident** to the police **as soon as reasonably practicable** and **in any case within 24 hours**.
4. If **injury** is caused, the driver must produce **insurance** details (or report it and produce them later – ⚠ VERIFICAR plazos).

**Failing to stop** and **failing to report** are offences (they appear in NIE 2.14).

:::es
**Deberes**: **parar**; si alguien con **motivos razonables** lo pide, dar **nombre y dirección**, los del **propietario** y la **matrícula**; si no se dan en el lugar, **informar a la policía lo antes posible y como máximo en 24 horas**; si hay **heridos**, mostrar el **seguro**. **No parar** y **no informar** son delitos (capítulo NIE 2.14).
:::

### As a driver – step by step

1. **Stop** safely, hazard lights on, engine off.
2. **Check for injuries**; call **999** if anyone is hurt, the road is blocked, or there's danger (fuel, fire) or a suspected offence (e.g. drink driving).
3. Make the scene **safe**; don't move injured people unless in danger.
4. **Exchange details** (names, addresses, phone, insurance, registrations).
5. **Record**: photos, time, place, weather, damage, witnesses' details, what happened.
6. Tell your **insurer** (even if you don't claim).
7. Don't admit liability at the scene; just give facts.

:::es
Como conductora: **parar** con seguridad → **heridos** (999 si hay heridos, peligro o posible delito) → **asegurar** la zona → **intercambiar datos** → **fotos y notas** (hora, lugar, tiempo, daños, testigos) → avisar a la **aseguradora** → no reconocer culpa, solo hechos.
:::

### As an officer (overview)

Scene safety → casualties → **preserve evidence** (vehicle positions, debris, CCTV, dashcam) → **breath test** where the law allows → statements → consider **offences** (careless/dangerous driving, failing to stop, no insurance) → **RTC report**. Serious and fatal collisions are investigated by specialist **Roads Policing / Collision Investigation** teams.

## Scenarios

1. You scrape a parked car at night; owner not around. → **Stop**, leave details if possible, and **report to the police within 24 hours**.
2. You hit a **dog** that runs into the road. → s. 170 applies (dog is listed) – stop, give details, or report.
3. You hit a **cat**. → Cats are **not** listed in s. 170 (⚠ but you should still act responsibly).

## Common mistakes

- Leaving a note on a windscreen and thinking that's enough – you still have to **report** if you didn't give details to someone with reasonable grounds.
- Thinking 24 hours means "you can wait 24 hours" – it's **as soon as reasonably practicable**, and **in any case** within 24 h.

## Summary

1. s. 170 RTA 1988: injury to others, damage to other vehicle/property, listed animals.
2. Stop + give name/address/owner/registration if required.
3. Not given? Report ASAP, in any case within 24 h.
4. Injury: produce insurance.
5. Failing to stop/report = offences (NIE 2.14).
