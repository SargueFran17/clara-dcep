## Objectives

- Know **whether and for how long** you can drive in Great Britain with your **Spanish (EU) licence**.
- Know **how to exchange** it for a **GB licence** and what you need.
- Know what else you need to drive legally: **insurance, tax, MOT** (more in DR10).

## Explanation

### Can I drive with my Spanish licence?

According to gov.uk ("Driving in Great Britain on a non-GB licence"), if you have a **full licence from an EU/EEA country** (Spain), you can drive in Great Britain on that licence **until you are 70**, or for **3 years after becoming resident** if you are 67 or older (⚠ VERIFICAR la redacción exacta en gov.uk). You can drive the **same categories** of vehicle shown on your licence.

:::es
Según gov.uk, con un **carné completo de un país de la UE/EEE** (España) puedes conducir en Gran Bretaña **hasta los 70 años** (o durante **3 años desde que pasas a ser residente** si tienes 67 o más) ⚠ VERIFICAR el texto exacto. Puedes conducir las **mismas categorías** que tienes en tu carné.
:::

### Exchanging it for a GB licence

You can **exchange** your EU licence for a **GB licence** (you do not need to take a test). In general you need:

- to be **resident** in Great Britain;
- the application form (**D1**, available from the DVLA form ordering service or some Post Offices);
- your **original licence** (it is sent to the DVLA and returned to Spain);
- **identity documents** and a **passport-style photo** (or a digital photo if you apply online where available), and the **fee** (⚠ VERIFICAR el precio actual en gov.uk).

Why exchange? A GB licence is easier for **insurance**, **car hire**, ID checks, and you may need it if your Spanish licence **expires** while you live here (⚠ VERIFICAR qué pasa al caducar el carné español siendo residente).

:::es
Puedes **canjearlo** por un carné británico **sin examen**: ser **residente**, formulario **D1**, enviar el **carné original** a la DVLA, documentos de **identidad**, **foto** y la **tasa** (⚠ verifica el precio). Ventajas: seguro, alquiler de coches, identificación y evitar problemas si tu carné español caduca mientras vives aquí.
:::

### Also important

- You must **tell the DVLA** if you have certain **medical conditions** that affect driving.
- Your **insurance** must cover you with your current licence – tell the insurer you have a **Spanish licence**.
- Driving **without insurance** is a serious offence (and links to NIE 2.14 and road policing).

:::es
Informa a la **DVLA** si tienes alguna **condición médica** que afecte a la conducción, y dile a tu **aseguradora** que tienes carné **español**. Conducir **sin seguro** es un delito grave.
:::

## Scenarios

1. You start the academy on 29/12/2026 and want to drive to training. → You can drive with your Spanish licence (check age rule), but make sure the **insurance** knows it is a Spanish licence.
2. Your Spanish licence expires next year. → Check gov.uk for the rule for residents; consider exchanging it **before** it expires.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Assuming you **must** exchange immediately – check gov.uk; for EU licences it is generally not immediate.
- Forgetting to tell the insurer about the **foreign licence** – this can **invalidate** the insurance.
- Thinking a **provisional/learner** licence works the same – it doesn't (⚠ VERIFICAR).

## Summary

1. EU/EEA full licence → drive in GB until 70 (or 3 years after residence if 67+) ⚠.
2. Same categories as your licence.
3. Exchange (no test): D1 form, original licence, ID, photo, fee.
4. Tell the insurer you have a Spanish licence.
5. Tell the DVLA about relevant medical conditions.
