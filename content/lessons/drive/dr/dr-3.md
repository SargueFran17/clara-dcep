## Objectives

- Use **lanes** correctly on UK roads and motorways (keep left, overtake on the right).
- **Overtake** safely and know when it is not allowed.
- Understand **priorities at junctions** – very different from the Spanish "priority to the right".

## Explanation

### Lane discipline

- **Keep to the left** lane unless overtaking, turning right or following signs/markings. On motorways, return to the **left lane** after overtaking (Highway Code rules 264–265). "Middle-lane hogging" can be treated as **careless driving**.
- **Do not overtake on the left** (undertake), except in specific cases, e.g. when traffic is moving slowly in **queues** and the lane on your right is moving more slowly, or when the vehicle in front is signalling to turn right (rules 163, 268 – ⚠ consulta el texto exacto).
- **Bus lanes**: check the times on the blue sign; outside those times you can usually use them.

:::es
- **Circula por el carril izquierdo** salvo para adelantar, girar a la derecha o seguir señales. En autopista, vuelve a la izquierda tras adelantar; quedarse en el carril central sin motivo puede ser **conducción descuidada**.
- **No adelantes por la izquierda**, salvo excepciones (colas de tráfico, vehículo que gira a la derecha).
- **Carriles bus**: mira el horario de la señal azul.
:::

### Overtaking (rules 162–169)

Before overtaking: **mirrors – signal – manoeuvre**; make sure the road is clear far enough ahead; overtake **on the right**; give vulnerable road users plenty of room (e.g. at least **1.5 m** when overtaking **cyclists** at speeds up to 30 mph, more at higher speeds – rule 163). **Do not overtake** where you would cross or straddle **double white lines** with a **solid line nearest to you**, near **pedestrian crossings**, at **junctions**, approaching **bends** or **hump bridges**, or where the road narrows.

:::es
**Espejos → intermitente → maniobra**. Adelanta **por la derecha**, deja mucho espacio a ciclistas (al menos **1,5 m** a 30 mph o menos). **No adelantes** con **doble línea blanca continua por tu lado**, cerca de pasos de peatones, en cruces, curvas o estrechamientos.
:::

### Priority at junctions – no "prioridad a la derecha"

In the UK, priority is decided by **signs and road markings**, not by a general "give way to the right" rule:

- **Give Way**: broken double white lines across your lane (+ inverted triangle sign) → give way to traffic on the major road.
- **STOP**: solid white line + octagonal **STOP** sign → you must stop completely.
- **Unmarked crossroads** (no lines/signs): **nobody has priority** – proceed with great care (rule 146).
- **Turning right** across traffic: give way to **oncoming** vehicles.
- **Box junctions** (yellow criss-cross lines): **do not enter** unless your exit is clear (you may wait in the box when turning right if only oncoming traffic stops you) – rule 174.
- **Emergency vehicles**: let them pass safely, but don't break the law (e.g. don't jump a red light) to do so (rule 219).

:::es
En el Reino Unido **no existe la "prioridad a la derecha"** general:
- **Give Way** (líneas discontinuas dobles + triángulo invertido) → cede el paso.
- **STOP** → parada obligatoria.
- **Cruce sin señalizar** → **nadie tiene prioridad**: máxima precaución.
- Girar a la derecha → cede a los que vienen de frente.
- **Box junction** (cuadrícula amarilla) → no entres si tu salida no está libre.
- **Emergencias**: déjales pasar sin cometer infracciones.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Motorway, left lane empty, you stay in the middle lane for miles. → Move **left**; middle-lane hogging can be careless driving.
2. You approach an unmarked crossroads in a housing estate. → Slow down; **no one** has priority.
3. Yellow box junction, traffic queuing beyond it. → **Wait before the box**.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Assuming **priority to the right** at junctions.
- Exiting motorways from the middle lane at the last second.
- Not giving cyclists enough space.

## Summary

1. Keep left; overtake on the right; return left.
2. No undertaking except specific cases.
3. Give cyclists at least 1.5 m (up to 30 mph).
4. Priority comes from signs and markings; unmarked crossroads = no priority.
5. Don't enter a box junction unless your exit is clear.
