## Objectives

- Build safe habits for **driving on the left** after years on the right.
- Use **roundabouts** correctly: direction, priority, lanes and signals.
- Avoid the **typical mistakes** of drivers coming from Spain.

## Explanation

### Driving on the left – habits

- **Keep left** unless overtaking (Highway Code rule 160).
- The driver sits on the **right**; the **gear stick** is on your **left**. Many people start with an **automatic** car to reduce workload.
- At **junctions**, the danger comes from the **right** first.
- **Turning right** = crossing traffic (like turning left in Spain). **Turning left** = the short, easy turn.
- After stopping (petrol station, car park, quiet roads) say to yourself: **"Keep left."** Most mistakes happen when the road is **empty**.
- As a **pedestrian**: **look right** first.

:::es
- Circula **por la izquierda** salvo para adelantar.
- Conduces sentada a la **derecha**; la palanca de cambios queda a tu **izquierda** (un **automático** facilita mucho al principio).
- En cruces, el peligro viene primero **de la derecha**.
- **Girar a la derecha** = cruzar el tráfico (como girar a la izquierda en España).
- Al salir de una gasolinera o en carreteras vacías, repítete **"keep left"**: ahí es donde más errores hay.
- Como peatón: **mira a la derecha** primero.
:::

### Roundabouts (Highway Code rules 184–190)

- Traffic goes **clockwise** (in the opposite direction to Spain).
- **Give way to traffic coming from your right** (already on the roundabout), unless signs or markings say otherwise.
- **Lanes and signals** (general rule):
  - **Turning left / first exit**: signal **left**, use the **left** lane, keep signalling left.
  - **Straight ahead**: usually **no signal** on approach, **left** lane (unless markings say otherwise); signal **left** after passing the exit before yours.
  - **Turning right / going full circle**: signal **right**, use the **right** lane, keep to the right on the roundabout; signal **left** after passing the exit before yours.
- **Mini-roundabouts**: same priority rule; drive **around** the central marking if possible; **U-turns** on them are discouraged.
- Always follow **road markings** and **signs** – big roundabouts often have lane arrows.

:::es
- Se gira en el **sentido de las agujas del reloj**.
- **Cede el paso a los que vienen por tu derecha** (ya dentro de la rotonda).
- **Primera salida (izquierda)**: intermitente **izquierdo** y carril **izquierdo**.
- **Recto**: normalmente sin intermitente al entrar, carril **izquierdo**; intermitente **izquierdo** al pasar la salida anterior a la tuya.
- **Derecha / dar la vuelta**: intermitente **derecho**, carril **derecho**; intermitente **izquierdo** al pasar la salida anterior a la tuya.
- **Mini-rotondas**: misma prioridad; rodea el círculo pintado si puedes.
- Manda siempre lo que digan las **marcas viales y señales**.
:::

## Scenarios

1. You leave a supermarket car park onto an empty road. → Say **"keep left"** and position on the **left**.
2. Approaching a roundabout to take the **third exit** (right). → Signal **right**, **right** lane, give way to traffic from the **right**, signal **left** after the second exit.
3. You want to turn right into a side road. → Position near the centre line, give way to **oncoming** traffic, turn into the **left** side of the new road.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Turning right into the **wrong side** of the new road.
- Looking **left** first at roundabouts.
- Exiting a roundabout from the **outside lane** at every exit like in Spain – in the UK, lane use depends on your exit and the markings.
- Hitting the **kerb on the left** because you are not used to the car's width on that side.

## Summary

1. Keep left; danger comes from the right.
2. Right turns cross traffic.
3. Roundabouts: clockwise, give way to the right.
4. Left exit = left signal & lane; right exit = right signal & lane, then left signal.
5. Markings and signs always take priority.
