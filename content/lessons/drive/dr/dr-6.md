## Objectives

- Read UK traffic signs by **shape and colour** quickly.
- Recognise the signs that **differ from Spain** or catch foreign drivers out.
- Know the **national speed limit** sign and what it means for each road.

## Explanation

### Shape = function (same idea as Spain, with some differences)

- **Circles give orders**: **red ring** = prohibition (*no entry for…*, *speed limit*); **blue circle** = mandatory instruction (*turn left*, *mini-roundabout*).
- **Triangles warn** (red border).
- **Rectangles inform**: **blue** = motorways, **green** = primary routes, **white** = other routes, **brown** = tourist destinations.
- **STOP** (octagon) and **Give way** (inverted triangle).

:::es
- **Círculos = órdenes**: borde **rojo** = prohibición; círculo **azul** = obligación.
- **Triángulos = advertencia.**
- **Rectángulos = información**: **azul** autopistas, **verde** rutas principales, **blanco** otras rutas, **marrón** turismo.
- **STOP** (octógono) y **Give way** (triángulo invertido).
:::

### Signs that catch Spanish drivers out

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **White circle with a black diagonal stripe** | **National speed limit applies**: for cars **60 mph** on single carriageways, **70 mph** on dual carriageways and motorways |
| **Numbers in a red ring** | speed limit in **mph**, not km/h |
| **Red X / red ring on blue background** | **No stopping** (clearway) / **No waiting** (check times) |
| **Distances** | in **miles** and **yards** (e.g. "Services 1 m", "200 yds") |
| **Height limits** | in **feet and inches** (e.g. 12′6″) – check your van! |
| **"Zone ENDS"** | a 20 mph zone or controlled parking zone ends |
| **Blue sign with a white "P" and times** | parking allowed at those times |
| **Countdown markers** (3, 2, 1 bars) | approaching a motorway exit or a concealed junction |
| **"Except for access"** | only vehicles going to a property on that road |

:::es
- **Círculo blanco con raya diagonal negra** = **límite nacional**: coches **60 mph** en carretera de calzada única y **70 mph** en autovía/autopista.
- Límites en **mph**, distancias en **millas/yardas**, alturas en **pies y pulgadas**.
- **Fondo azul con X roja** = prohibido parar (*clearway*); con raya = prohibido esperar (mira horarios).
- **Except for access** = solo para acceder a propiedades de esa calle.
:::

### Variable and temporary signs

- **Smart motorway gantries**: speed in a red ring = **mandatory**; **red X** = lane closed (DR5).
- **Yellow temporary signs**: roadworks and diversions.

## Scenarios

1. You leave a 30 mph village and see the **white circle with a black stripe** on a single-carriageway A road. → Up to **60 mph** for a car (if safe).
2. A low bridge sign says **11′ 9″**. → About **3.58 m**.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Reading 60 as km/h – it's **mph** (≈ 97 km/h).
- Thinking the national speed limit sign means "no limit" (like the end-of-restrictions sign in some countries).
- Ignoring height limits in feet.

## Summary

1. Circles order, triangles warn, rectangles inform.
2. Blue motorway, green primary, white other, brown tourist.
3. National speed limit: 60 single / 70 dual & motorway (cars).
4. Distances in miles/yards; heights in feet/inches.
5. Clearway = no stopping.
