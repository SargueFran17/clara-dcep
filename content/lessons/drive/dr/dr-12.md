## Objectives

- Understand the difference between **PCN**, **FPN** and **private parking charges**.
- Park legally: yellow lines, bays, permits, night parking rules.
- Know how to **pay or challenge** a penalty.

## Explanation

### Three types of "parking ticket"

| Ticket | Issued by | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Penalty Charge Notice (PCN)** | **council** (civil enforcement officers) or Transport for London | **civil** penalty; usually **reduced by 50 %** if paid within **14 days** (⚠ VERIFICAR) |
| **Fixed Penalty Notice (FPN)** | **police** (or PCSOs) | **criminal** offence dealt with by a fixed penalty; may include **penalty points** for some motoring offences |
| **Parking Charge Notice** (private) | **private companies** (supermarkets, retail parks) | **contractual** charge – **not a fine**; appeal routes exist (e.g. POPLA/IAS) |

Watch the names: **Penalty Charge Notice** (council) vs **Parking Charge Notice** (private) – both "PCN"!

:::es
- **Penalty Charge Notice** (ayuntamiento): sanción **civil**; normalmente **50 % de descuento** si pagas en **14 días** (⚠ verifica).
- **Fixed Penalty Notice** (policía): infracción **penal** resuelta con sanción fija; a veces con **puntos**.
- **Parking Charge Notice** (empresas privadas): cobro **contractual**, **no es una multa**; se puede recurrir.
:::

### Parking rules to remember

- **Yellow lines** and **kerb blips** (DR7) – read the time plates.
- **Bays**: pay and display / pay by phone app, **permit holders only**, **disabled (Blue Badge)**, **loading only**, **taxi**, **doctor**.
- **At night** you **must not** park facing **against the direction of traffic** unless in a recognised parking space (rule 248).
- Don't park: on **zig-zags**, near junctions (within **10 m**), opposite or near junctions, at bus stops, in front of dropped kerbs/driveways, or where you'd **obstruct** (rules 242–243).
- **Pavement parking** is banned in **London** (and being reviewed elsewhere ⚠ VERIFICAR); elsewhere it can still be an **obstruction**.

:::es
- Mira las **placas horarias** de las líneas amarillas.
- Plazas: de pago, **solo residentes**, **Blue Badge** (discapacidad), **carga**, **taxi**.
- **De noche** no aparques **en sentido contrario** al tráfico salvo en plaza señalizada (regla 248).
- No aparques en zigzags, a menos de **10 m** de un cruce, en paradas de bus, delante de vados.
- **Aparcar en la acera** está prohibido en **Londres**; en otras zonas puede ser **obstrucción**.
:::

### Paying and challenging

- Pay within the **discount period** if you agree.
- If you disagree: **informal challenge** → **formal representation** → independent **tribunal** (for council PCNs). Keep **photos**, tickets, receipts.
- Never ignore a penalty: amounts **increase** and can go to enforcement.

## Scenarios

1. You park at a supermarket and overstay by 40 minutes. → **Private Parking Charge Notice** – contractual; check the signs, appeal if unfair.
2. A council officer tickets you on a single yellow line during restricted hours. → **PCN** – pay at discount or challenge with evidence.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Parking facing the "wrong" way at night.
- Ignoring time plates on single yellow lines.
- Paying a private "fine" without checking the signs/appeal process.

## Summary

1. Council PCN = civil; police FPN = criminal; private PCN = contractual.
2. Discount for early payment (council PCN).
3. Night: don't park against traffic (unless in a bay).
4. 10 m from junctions; no zig-zags; no blocking dropped kerbs.
5. Challenge with evidence; never ignore.
