## Objectives

- Know the **four legal basics** for keeping a car on UK roads: **insurance, MOT, vehicle tax (VED), V5C**.
- Understand **SORN** and **continuous insurance enforcement**.
- Know what to check when **buying** a used car.

## Explanation

### 1. Insurance

- You **must** have at least **third-party** insurance to drive on a road or public place (Road Traffic Act 1988, s. 143).
- Types: **third party** (others' damage), **third party, fire and theft**, **comprehensive**.
- If a vehicle is **registered** to you, it must be **insured** unless you've declared **SORN** (continuous insurance enforcement).
- Tell the insurer about your **Spanish licence**, convictions, address, and where the car is kept. Driving uninsured: unlimited fine, **6–8 points**, possible disqualification and the car can be **seized** (⚠ VERIFICAR).

:::es
**Seguro**: obligatorio, mínimo **a terceros** (RTA 1988 s. 143). Si el coche está a tu nombre, debe estar asegurado salvo **SORN**. Informa de tu **carné español**. Sin seguro: multa, puntos y posible **incautación** del coche.
:::

### 2. MOT

- Annual test of **roadworthiness** and **emissions** for most cars **3 years old or more** (in Great Britain).
- Without a valid MOT you generally **can't tax** the car and it's an offence to drive it (except to a pre-booked MOT/repair) – and insurance may be affected.
- Check MOT history online with the registration number.

:::es
**MOT** = ITV anual para coches de **3 años o más**. Sin MOT no puedes pagar el impuesto ni circular (salvo para ir a la cita). El historial se consulta online.
:::

### 3. Vehicle tax (VED)

- **Vehicle Excise Duty** – paid online/at the Post Office; there is **no paper tax disc** (since 2014).
- Tax **does not transfer** when you buy a car: the **new keeper must tax it** before driving.
- **SORN** (Statutory Off Road Notification) if the car is kept **off the road** and untaxed.

:::es
**VED** = impuesto de circulación, online; **no hay pegatina** desde 2014. **No se transfiere** al comprar: debes pagarlo antes de conducir. **SORN** si el coche está fuera de la vía pública.
:::

### 4. V5C (logbook)

- The **registration certificate**. It shows the **registered keeper** – **not necessarily the legal owner**.
- When you buy/sell, the keeper change is notified to the **DVLA** (online or by post).
- Update your **address** with the DVLA.

:::es
**V5C** ("logbook") = permiso de circulación. Indica el **keeper** (titular registrado), **no** prueba la propiedad. Al comprar o vender se notifica a la **DVLA**.
:::

### Buying a used car – checks

V5C details match (VIN, engine), **MOT history**, **vehicle history check** (outstanding finance, stolen, written off), service history, test drive, and **insure + tax before driving away**.

## Scenarios

1. You buy a car on Saturday and want to drive it home. → **Insure** and **tax** it first (tax doesn't come with the car).
2. A car is parked on the drive, unused and untaxed. → Declare **SORN**.

## Common mistakes (from Spain)

- Thinking tax comes with the car like in a Spanish transfer.
- Thinking the V5C proves ownership.
- Forgetting the MOT date (set a reminder – the DVLA offers free reminders).

## Summary

1. Insurance: at least third party; continuous unless SORN.
2. MOT yearly from 3 years old.
3. VED: online, no disc, doesn't transfer.
4. V5C = registered keeper, not owner.
5. Buying: checks + insure + tax before driving.
