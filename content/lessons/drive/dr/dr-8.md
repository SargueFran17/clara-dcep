## Objectives

- Know the **national speed limits** for cars (and the main other vehicles).
- Recognise **built-up areas** and **20 mph** limits.
- Understand the basic **consequences of speeding**.

## Explanation

### National speed limits (Highway Code rule 124)

| Vehicle | Built-up areas* | Single carriageway | Dual carriageway | Motorway |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cars, motorcycles, car-derived vans** | **30** | **60** | **70** | **70** |
| Cars **towing** caravans/trailers | 30 | 50 | 60 | 60 |
| Buses, coaches, minibuses (≤12 m) | 30 | 50 | 60 | 70 |
| Goods vehicles ≤7.5 t | 30 | 50 | 60 | 70 (60 if articulated/towing) |
| Goods vehicles >7.5 t (England & Wales) | 30 | 50 | 60 | 60 |

\*The 30 mph limit usually applies on roads with **street lights** (unless signs show otherwise). **In Wales**, the default on most such roads became **20 mph** in 2023 (⚠ VERIFICAR excepciones). Many English towns have **20 mph** zones too.

All figures in **mph**: 30 mph ≈ 48 km/h · 60 mph ≈ 97 km/h · 70 mph ≈ 113 km/h.

:::es
**Coches**: **30** mph en zona urbana con farolas, **60** en carretera de calzada única, **70** en autovía y autopista (≈ 48 / 97 / 113 km/h). Con remolque: 30/50/60/60. En **Gales** el límite urbano por defecto pasó a **20 mph** en 2023 (⚠ verifica). Muchas ciudades inglesas tienen zonas de **20 mph**.
:::

### Limits are a maximum, not a target

Drive **slower** in bad weather, near schools, in busy streets, at night. The Highway Code: drive at a speed that lets you **stop well within the distance you can see to be clear**.

**Stopping distances** (rule 126, dry road, typical car): 20 mph ≈ **12 m** · 30 mph ≈ **23 m** · 40 mph ≈ 36 m · 50 mph ≈ 53 m · 60 mph ≈ 73 m · 70 mph ≈ **96 m**. Double in the wet; up to ten times on ice.

:::es
El límite es un **máximo**. **Distancias de parada** (seco): 30 mph ≈ **23 m**, 70 mph ≈ **96 m**; el doble con lluvia y hasta 10 veces con hielo.
:::

### Consequences of speeding (general)

- Usually a **Fixed Penalty Notice**: minimum **£100** fine and **3 penalty points** (⚠ VERIFICAR importes vigentes), or a **speed awareness course** in some cases, or **court** for higher speeds.
- **12 points within 3 years** → usually a **disqualification** ("totting up").
- **New drivers** (first 2 years after passing a **GB** test): **6 points** → licence revoked (⚠ VERIFICAR cómo se aplica a carnés canjeados).

:::es
Exceso de velocidad: normalmente **multa fija (mínimo £100) + 3 puntos** (⚠ verifica), o **curso de concienciación**, o **juzgado** si es grave. **12 puntos en 3 años** → retirada del carné.
:::

## Scenarios

1. A road with street lights and no speed signs. → Assume **30 mph** (20 in Wales unless signed).
2. Dual carriageway with the national speed limit sign, you're towing a trailer. → **60 mph** max.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Reading limits as km/h.
- Assuming motorway limit is 120 km/h (it's **70 mph ≈ 113 km/h**).
- Not noticing that **street lights** usually mean 30.

## Summary

1. Cars: 30 / 60 / 70 / 70 mph.
2. Street lights usually = 30 mph (Wales default 20).
3. Limits are maximums; adapt to conditions.
4. Stopping distance at 30 mph ≈ 23 m; at 70 mph ≈ 96 m.
5. Speeding: points + fine; 12 points in 3 years = ban.
