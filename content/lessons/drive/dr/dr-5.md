## Objectives

- Recognise the UK **pedestrian crossings** and what drivers must do at each.
- Share the road safely with **cyclists** and **horse riders**.
- Drive on **motorways**, including **smart motorways** and what to do if you break down.

## Explanation

### Pedestrian crossings (rules 191–199)

| Crossing | How it works | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| **Zebra** | black and white stripes, **flashing yellow beacons** | **MUST give way** when someone has moved onto it; should give way to people waiting (H2) |
| **Pelican** | lights; **flashing amber** after red | on flashing amber, **give way** to pedestrians still crossing; go if clear |
| **Puffin** | lights with sensors; **no** flashing amber | normal light sequence |
| **Toucan** | for **pedestrians and cyclists** | normal light sequence |
| **Equestrian (Pegasus)** | for **horse riders** | normal light sequence |

**Zig-zag lines** each side of a crossing: **no parking, no overtaking** the leading vehicle.

:::es
- **Zebra**: rayas + **balizas amarillas intermitentes** → **cede** a quien ya está cruzando (y, según H2, a quien espera).
- **Pelican**: semáforo con **ámbar intermitente** → cede a quien sigue cruzando.
- **Puffin**: semáforo con sensores, sin ámbar intermitente.
- **Toucan**: peatones **y ciclistas**. **Pegasus**: jinetes.
- **Zigzags**: prohibido aparcar y adelantar.
:::

### Cyclists and horse riders

- Give at least **1.5 m** to cyclists at up to 30 mph (more at higher speeds), and at least **2 m** to horses at **10 mph or less** (rule 163).
- Cyclists may ride in the **centre of the lane** for their safety (e.g. on quiet roads, slower traffic, approaching junctions).
- **"Dutch reach"**: open your car door with the hand furthest from it so you look over your shoulder for cyclists (rule 239).

:::es
Ciclistas: **1,5 m** (≤30 mph); caballos: **2 m** a **≤10 mph**. Los ciclistas pueden ir por el **centro del carril**. **"Dutch reach"**: abre la puerta con la mano contraria para mirar atrás.
:::

### Motorways (rules 253–274)

- Speed limit **70 mph** for cars (unless signs say less).
- **Keep left**; overtake on the right; return left (DR3).
- **Smart motorways**: overhead signs show variable speed limits. A **red X** above a lane means it is **closed** – you **MUST NOT** drive in it.
- **Hard shoulder**: only for emergencies (unless signs open it as a running lane on some smart motorways).
- **Breakdown**: if possible leave at the next exit or stop in an **emergency area** (orange-marked lay-by). If you stop on the hard shoulder: hazard lights on, **get out on the left** (passenger side), wait **behind the barrier**, call for help.
- **Learner drivers** can use motorways only with an **approved driving instructor** in a **dual-control** car.

:::es
**Autopista**: **70 mph** (113 km/h), carril izquierdo. **Smart motorways**: límites variables; **X roja** = carril **cerrado** (prohibido circular). **Arcén** solo para emergencias. **Avería**: sal por la próxima salida o para en un **área de emergencia** (naranja); si paras en el arcén: warning, **sal por el lado izquierdo**, espera **detrás de la barrera** y pide ayuda.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Flashing amber at a **pelican** crossing, an older man is still crossing. → **Wait** until he's clear.
2. Overhead gantry shows a **red X** over lane 1. → Move out of that lane safely; driving in it is an **offence**.
3. Your car breaks down on the M27. → Emergency area or hard shoulder, exit **left**, behind the barrier, call.

## Common mistakes (drivers from Spain)

- Not stopping at **zebra** crossings when someone steps on.
- Ignoring the **red X**.
- Waiting inside the car on the hard shoulder.

## Summary

1. Zebra = give way; pelican flashing amber = give way to those crossing.
2. Puffin/toucan/pegasus = lights.
3. 1.5 m for cyclists; 2 m for horses (≤10 mph).
4. Motorway 70 mph; red X = lane closed (MUST NOT use).
5. Breakdown: exit left, behind barrier, call for help.
