> 🩺 Si tienes problemas de sueño persistentes o te sientes muy cansada o baja de ánimo durante semanas, habla con tu **GP**. Esto es información general.

## Objectives

- Build **simple daily habits** for water, sleep and training that survive a busy academy.
- Use the app's **habit tracking** and **streak** to stay consistent.
- Know the NHS guidance on **alcohol** and **sleep hygiene**.

## Explanation

### Make habits easy

- **Habit stacking**: attach a new habit to an existing one – *"After I make my morning coffee, I fill my water bottle."*
- **Make it visible**: water bottle on your desk; gym bag ready the night before.
- **Make it small**: *"10 minutes of mobility"* is better than nothing.
- **Track it**: tick **Hábitos** in **Hoy** every day (comidas, agua, sueño). The **streak** counts days you complete the plan.
- **Never miss twice**: one bad day is normal; two in a row starts a new (bad) habit.

:::es
- **Encadenar hábitos**: "después del café, lleno la botella".
- **Hacerlo visible**: botella en la mesa, bolsa del gym preparada.
- **Hacerlo pequeño**: 10 minutos es mejor que nada.
- **Registrarlo** en **Hoy → Hábitos**; la **racha** te motiva.
- **Nunca fallar dos veces seguidas.**
:::

### Water

Aim for **6–8 glasses** of fluid a day (more when training or hot). Pale-yellow urine is a simple sign of good hydration.

### Sleep hygiene (NHS general tips)

- Regular **bed and wake times** (also at weekends as far as possible).
- **Wind down**: no screens/study in bed for the last 30–60 min; dark, quiet, cool room.
- Avoid **caffeine late** and big meals just before bed.
- Exercise helps sleep – but not intense sessions right before bed.

:::es
**Sueño**: horarios **regulares**, **desconectar** 30–60 min antes (sin pantallas en la cama), habitación **oscura, silenciosa y fresca**, nada de **cafeína tarde** ni cenas copiosas, y ejercicio (no justo antes de dormir).
:::

### Alcohol

NHS low-risk guidance: **no more than 14 units a week** on a regular basis, **spread over 3 or more days**, with some **alcohol-free days**. A pint of normal-strength beer is about **2–3 units**; a 175 ml glass of wine about **2 units** (⚠ VERIFICAR según graduación). Alcohol also **worsens sleep** and recovery – and remember drink-driving (DR9).

:::es
**Alcohol (NHS)**: **máximo 14 unidades a la semana**, repartidas en **3 días o más**, con días sin alcohol. Una pinta de cerveza ≈ 2–3 unidades; una copa de vino de 175 ml ≈ 2 (⚠ depende de la graduación). Empeora el **sueño** y la recuperación.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Exam week at the academy and you skip training. → Keep a **mini version** (20-min walk + mobility) to maintain the habit.
2. You notice you only sleep 6 h on study nights. → Set a **fixed "screens off"** time and move study earlier.

## Common mistakes

- All-or-nothing thinking.
- Studying in bed until late.
- Using weekends to "catch up" sleep with very late nights (social jet lag).

## Summary

1. Habit stacking, visible cues, small steps.
2. Track habits daily; never miss twice.
3. 6–8 glasses of fluid.
4. Regular sleep, wind-down, no late caffeine.
5. ≤ 14 units/week, spread out, with alcohol-free days.
