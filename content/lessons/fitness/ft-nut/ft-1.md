> 🩺 **Consulta a tu GP** (médico de cabecera del NHS) antes de hacer cambios grandes en tu dieta o entrenamiento, sobre todo si tienes alguna condición de salud. Esta lección es información general, **no** consejo médico.

## Objectives

- Understand what the **NHS** recommends for **safe, sustainable** weight loss.
- Measure your **starting point** without obsessing over numbers.
- Set goals based on **habits and fitness**, not on fast results.

## Explanation

### What the NHS recommends (in general)

- Lose weight **gradually**: about **0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) a week** is the usual guidance for safe weight loss (NHS Better Health / NHS weight loss plan).
- Combine **healthy eating** with **physical activity**.
- Avoid **crash diets**: they are hard to keep, can make you lose muscle and energy, and weight usually comes back.
- Adults should do at least **150 minutes of moderate activity a week** plus **strength exercises on 2 or more days** (UK Chief Medical Officers' guidelines).

:::es
- Perder peso **poco a poco**: **0,5–1 kg por semana** es la recomendación general del NHS.
- **Comer bien + moverse**.
- **Nada de dietas milagro**: no se mantienen, pierdes músculo y energía, y el peso vuelve.
- Adultos: **150 minutos de actividad moderada a la semana** + **fuerza 2 o más días** (tú ya haces mucho más con gym, running y tenis).
:::

### Your starting point (in the app: Fitness)

- **Weight once a week**, same day, same time, same conditions (e.g. Monday morning). The app shows the **trend** and warns you if you go faster than 1 kg/week.
- **Bleep test** level (lesson FT2).
- **Habits**: meals plan, water, sleep.
- Optional: waist measurement and how your clothes fit – often better indicators than the scale when you train strength.

:::es
Punto de partida en la app (**Fitness**): **peso una vez por semana** (mismo día y hora), **nivel de bleep test**, **hábitos** (comidas, agua, sueño). Opcional: cintura y cómo te queda la ropa, que con entrenamiento de fuerza suelen decir más que la báscula.
:::

### Goals that work

- Process goals: *"5 training sessions a week"*, *"vegetables at lunch and dinner"*, *"7–8 hours of sleep"*.
- Performance goal: **bleep test 5.4** (and above, for a safety margin).
- Outcome goal: a **moderate** weight target agreed with your GP if needed.

:::es
Objetivos útiles: de **proceso** (entrenos, verdura, sueño), de **rendimiento** (**bleep 5.4** y algo más de margen) y de **resultado** moderado (si hace falta, hablado con tu GP).
:::

## Scenarios

1. Week 1: you lose 2 kg. → Normal at the start (water), but if the average stays above 1 kg/week, eat a bit more and talk to your GP.
2. Week 4: the scale doesn't move but your bleep level improves. → Progress! Strength training can change body composition without big changes in weight.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Weighing yourself every day and reacting to normal daily changes.
- Cutting meals on long academy days → low energy, poor learning and injury risk.
- Comparing yourself with others.

## Summary

1. Safe pace: about 0.5–1 kg per week.
2. Healthy eating + activity; no crash diets.
3. Weekly weigh-in, same conditions; watch the trend.
4. Process + performance goals (bleep 5.4).
5. GP before big changes.
