> 🩺 Información general. Si tienes necesidades especiales (alergias, condiciones médicas, embarazo…), habla con tu **GP** o un dietista registrado.

## Objectives

- Use the **Eatwell Guide** to build balanced meals without counting calories.
- Plan meals that give energy for **training and long study days**.
- Know simple **swaps** that help with healthy weight loss.

## Explanation

### The Eatwell Guide (Public Health England / NHS)

The Eatwell Guide shows the **proportions** of food groups for a healthy, balanced diet over a day or a week:

- **Fruit and vegetables** – about **a third** of what you eat; at least **5 portions a day**.
- **Starchy foods** (potatoes, bread, rice, pasta) – about **a third**; choose **wholegrain / higher-fibre** versions.
- **Beans, pulses, fish, eggs, meat and other proteins** – some every day; **2 portions of fish a week**, one of them oily (e.g. salmon, sardines).
- **Dairy and alternatives** – some, choosing lower-fat and lower-sugar options.
- **Oils and spreads** – small amounts, unsaturated.
- **Fluids**: **6–8 glasses** a day (water, lower-fat milk, sugar-free drinks).
- Foods high in fat, salt and sugar – **less often and in small amounts**.

:::es
**Eatwell Guide** (proporciones de un día o semana):
- **Fruta y verdura**: ~**un tercio**, mínimo **5 raciones al día**.
- **Hidratos** (patata, pan, arroz, pasta): ~**un tercio**, mejor **integrales**.
- **Proteínas** (legumbres, pescado, huevos, carne): algo cada día; **2 raciones de pescado a la semana** (una azul).
- **Lácteos** o alternativas: algo, mejor bajos en grasa/azúcar.
- **Aceites**: poca cantidad, insaturados.
- **Líquidos: 6–8 vasos al día.**
- Alimentos altos en grasa, sal y azúcar: **de vez en cuando y poco**.
:::

### The plate method (easy to remember)

For lunch and dinner: **½ plate vegetables/salad · ¼ protein · ¼ wholegrain carbs** + a little healthy fat. Around **training**: a bit more carbs; after strength sessions, include **protein**.

A Spanish-style example: *lentejas con verduras* + salad + fruit; *pescado a la plancha* with potatoes and vegetables; *tortilla* with a big salad and wholegrain bread.

:::es
**Método del plato**: ½ verdura, ¼ proteína, ¼ hidratos integrales + un poco de grasa buena. Los días de entreno, algo más de hidratos; tras fuerza, **proteína**. La comida española encaja muy bien: lentejas con verduras, pescado a la plancha con patata y verdura, tortilla con ensalada grande.
:::

### Simple swaps

- Sugary drinks → water, sparkling water, tea/coffee without sugar.
- Crisps/chocolate at the desk → fruit, yoghurt, a handful of nuts.
- Big takeaway → home-cooked batch meals (lesson FT6: meals on academy days).
- Reading labels: UK foods use **traffic-light labels** (red/amber/green for fat, saturates, sugars, salt).

:::es
Cambios fáciles: refrescos → agua/té/café sin azúcar; snacks → fruta, yogur, frutos secos; comida para llevar → **batch cooking**. En el Reino Unido las etiquetas usan el **semáforo** (rojo/ámbar/verde).
:::

## Scenarios

1. Long study Saturday (3.5 h) + long run. → Breakfast with oats and fruit, lunch with carbs and protein, water during the day.
2. You're hungry at 17:00 every day. → Plan a snack (fruit + yoghurt) so you don't arrive starving at dinner.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Cutting all carbs while training 8 sessions a week → no energy for the bleep test.
- Not drinking enough water.
- "All or nothing": one bad meal doesn't ruin the week.

## Summary

1. Eatwell: ⅓ fruit & veg, ⅓ starchy (wholegrain), protein daily, some dairy, little oil.
2. 5 a day; 2 fish a week (1 oily); 6–8 glasses of fluid.
3. Plate: ½ veg, ¼ protein, ¼ carbs.
4. Fuel your training; protein after strength.
5. Use traffic-light labels; no "all or nothing".
