> 🩺 Información general. Si tienes necesidades dietéticas concretas, habla con tu GP o un dietista registrado.

## Objectives

- Plan meals for **long academy days** (early starts, long sessions, study in the evening).
- Use **batch cooking** and **packed lunches** to eat well without spending hours.
- Manage **energy, hydration and caffeine** so you can learn and train.

## Explanation

### The weekly routine (1 hour on Sunday)

1. **Plan** 3–4 dinners for the week (repeat favourites).
2. **Batch cook** 2 big dishes (e.g. *lentejas*, chilli, curry with vegetables, *pisto* with chicken) → portions for lunches and dinners; freeze some.
3. **Prepare** snacks: fruit, yoghurts, nuts, boiled eggs, hummus + carrots.
4. **Shopping list** from the plan (fewer impulse buys).

:::es
**Domingo, 1 hora**: planifica 3–4 cenas, **cocina dos platos grandes** (lentejas, chili, curry con verduras, pisto con pollo…) en raciones para comidas y cenas (congela algunas), prepara **snacks** y haz la **lista de la compra**.
:::

### A typical academy day

| Time | What | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | protein + slow carbs | porridge with fruit and yoghurt; wholegrain toast with eggs |
| Mid-morning | snack | fruit + handful of nuts |
| Lunch (packed) | ½ veg · ¼ protein · ¼ carbs | batch-cooked dish; big salad with tuna/chickpeas + wholegrain bread |
| Afternoon | snack before training/study | yoghurt, banana, oatcakes |
| Dinner | balanced, not too late | fish/chicken/legumes with vegetables and potatoes/rice |

Carry a **water bottle**; aim for regular drinks through the day (6–8 glasses, more if you train).

:::es
**Desayuno**: proteína + hidratos lentos (avena con fruta y yogur). **Media mañana**: fruta y frutos secos. **Comida** (tupper): ½ verdura, ¼ proteína, ¼ hidratos. **Merienda** antes de entrenar o estudiar. **Cena**: equilibrada y no muy tarde. **Botella de agua** siempre.
:::

### Caffeine and sleep

- Caffeine can help concentration, but avoid it in the **6 hours before bed** if it affects your sleep.
- Sleep is when **memory consolidates**: protect **7–9 hours**.
- Energy drinks: high in caffeine and often sugar – not a meal replacement.

:::es
**Cafeína**: evita tomarla en las **6 horas previas a dormir** si te afecta. **Dormir 7–9 horas**: es cuando se consolida la memoria. Las bebidas energéticas no sustituyen comidas.
:::

## Scenarios

1. 07:00 start, 17:00 finish, then gym. → Big breakfast, packed lunch, snack at 16:30, dinner after gym.
2. Canteen only has chips and pies. → Bring your **own lunch**; choose the best option on the menu (jacket potato with beans, salad).

## Common mistakes

- Skipping breakfast and overeating at night.
- Living on coffee and biscuits during study weeks.
- Not planning → takeaways every night.

## Summary

1. Sunday plan + batch cooking.
2. Packed lunch with the plate method.
3. Snacks ready for training days.
4. Water bottle; caffeine not late.
5. Sleep 7–9 hours to learn better.
