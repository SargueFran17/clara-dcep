> 🩺 Si un dolor es fuerte, aparece hinchazón importante, no puedes apoyar o no mejora en unos días, **busca atención** (NHS 111, tu GP o un fisioterapeuta). Esto es información general.

## Objectives

- Use a proper **warm-up** and **cool-down**.
- Apply **progression** and **recovery** to avoid overuse injuries (shins, knees, Achilles).
- Know what to do with a **minor sprain** and when to get help.

## Explanation

### Warm-up (8–10 minutes) – "RAMP"

1. **Raise** – easy jog/bike to raise heart rate and temperature.
2. **Activate & Mobilise** – leg swings, hip circles, ankle mobility, glute bridges.
3. **Potentiate** – a few short accelerations or practice turns (before bleep/tennis).

### Cool-down

Easy jogging/walking 5 min + gentle stretches for calves, hamstrings, quads and hips.

:::es
**Calentamiento RAMP (8–10 min)**: subir pulsaciones → activar y movilizar (balanceos de pierna, caderas, tobillos, puentes de glúteo) → unas aceleraciones o giros de práctica. **Vuelta a la calma**: 5 min suave + estiramientos.
:::

### The main causes of running/tennis injuries

- **Too much, too soon** (sudden increases in distance or intensity).
- **Not enough recovery** (sleep, rest days).
- **Weak muscles** around hips, knees and ankles → your **gym A/B/C** helps.
- **Wrong or worn-out shoes** (replace running shoes roughly every 500–800 km – ⚠ guía general).

### Warning signs

- Pain that **changes how you move** (limping).
- Pain that **gets worse during** a session.
- **Localised bone pain** (e.g. one point on the shin) – could be a stress injury.
→ Stop, rest, get advice.

:::es
**Causas**: hacer **demasiado y demasiado pronto**, poca **recuperación**, **debilidad muscular** (el gym ayuda) y **calzado** inadecuado o gastado. **Señales de alarma**: dolor que te hace **cojear**, que **empeora** durante la sesión, o dolor **óseo localizado** → para y consulta.
:::

### Minor sprains and strains (NHS general advice)

For the **first couple of days**: **R**est, **I**ce (wrapped, ~20 min every 2–3 hours), **C**ompression (a bandage), **E**levation. Then gentle movement as pain allows. Get help if you **can't bear weight**, there's severe pain/swelling, or it doesn't improve (⚠ VERIFICAR guía NHS vigente).

:::es
**Esguince leve** (consejo general del NHS): los primeros días, **reposo, hielo envuelto (~20 min cada 2–3 h), compresión y elevación**; después, movimiento suave. Busca ayuda si **no puedes apoyar**, hay mucho dolor o hinchazón, o no mejora.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Shin pain after increasing runs from 2 to 5 per week. → Reduce, cross-train (bike), build up gradually; seek advice if bone pain persists.
2. Rolled ankle in tennis, can walk with mild pain. → RICE for 2 days, then gentle mobility; return gradually.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the warm-up before the bleep test or tennis.
- Training through sharp pain.
- Not counting tennis as training load.

## Summary

1. RAMP warm-up; easy cool-down.
2. Avoid too much, too soon; recover well.
3. Strength training protects joints.
4. Warning signs: limping, worsening pain, bone pain.
5. Minor sprain: RICE first days; get help if severe.
