title: "Combinar running y tenis"
---

> 🩺 Si tienes molestias que no mejoran en unos días, para y consulta a un profesional (fisioterapeuta o tu GP).

## Objectives

- Plan **running and tennis** in the same week without overloading your legs.
- Use tennis as **interval training** for the bleep test.
- Recognise signs of **too much load**.

## Explanation

### Your week (from the plan)

| Day | Session | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Gym A | moderate |
| **Tue** | **Intervals / bleep + tennis** | **high** |
| Wed | Gym B | moderate |
| **Thu** | **Tennis** | moderate–high |
| Fri | Gym C + easy jog | moderate / easy |
| **Sat** | **Long run + tennis** | moderate + moderate |
| Sun | Active rest | easy |

Tuesday is your **hardest day**: do **intervals first** (fresh legs) and play tennis **later** or at a lower intensity. On Saturday, do the **long run easy** – conversational pace – so you still enjoy tennis.

:::es
El **martes** es el día más duro: **intervalos primero** y tenis después (o más suave). El **sábado**, rodaje largo **a ritmo cómodo** (puedes hablar) para llegar bien al tenis.
:::

### Why tennis helps the bleep test

Tennis is full of **short sprints, quick stops and changes of direction** – very similar to the **turns** of the 15 m shuttle. Count it as part of your conditioning, especially singles.

### The hard/easy principle

- Don't put two **very hard** sessions back to back.
- **10 % rule** (prudent guide): don't increase weekly running time/distance by much more than about 10 % per week.
- If you feel **very tired**, sleep badly, or your resting heart rate is unusually high for several days, take an **easier day**.

:::es
**Principio duro/suave**: no encadenes dos sesiones muy duras; sube el volumen poco a poco (**~10 % semanal**); si notas **mucho cansancio** o duermes mal varios días, haz un día **suave**.
:::

### Footwear and surfaces

- Use **running shoes** for running and **tennis shoes** for tennis (lateral support).
- Mix surfaces if possible (park paths) to reduce impact.

## Scenarios

1. Tuesday: intervals at 18:00 and doubles at 20:00. → Fine. If legs are very heavy, play gently.
2. Saturday long run felt very hard and tennis was poor. → Run the long run **slower** next week.

## Common mistakes

- Running every session fast.
- Playing tennis in running shoes (ankle injuries).
- Ignoring pain that changes how you move.

## Summary

1. Tuesday = hardest: intervals first, then tennis.
2. Long run easy (conversational).
3. Tennis = sprints + direction changes = good for the bleep test.
4. Hard/easy principle; ~10 % increases.
5. Right shoes for each sport.
