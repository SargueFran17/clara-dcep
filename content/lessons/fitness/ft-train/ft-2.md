> ⚠ El estándar **nacional** es el **nivel 5.4** en el **15 m multi-stage fitness test**; **confirma con Hampshire** cuándo y cómo te lo harán. 🩺 Consulta a tu GP antes de empezar si tienes alguna condición de salud.

## Objectives

- Understand how the **15 m multi-stage fitness test (MSFT, "bleep test")** works.
- Train with **intervals and shuttle practice** to reach **level 5.4** with a margin.
- Track your progress in the app (**Fitness → Bleep test**).

## Explanation

### How the test works

- You run **back and forth** between two lines **15 metres** apart.
- You must reach the line **before the beep**. Each **level** gets slightly **faster**; each level has several **shuttles**.
- **Level 5.4** = level 5, shuttle 4. For the 15 m test this takes around **3½ minutes** of running (⚠ VERIFICAR la duración exacta).
- Usually you get a **warning** if you miss a line, and you are out if you miss again (⚠ VERIFICAR las reglas con Hampshire).

:::es
- Corres **ida y vuelta entre dos líneas a 15 m**.
- Tienes que llegar a la línea **antes del pitido**; cada **nivel** es algo más **rápido** y tiene varios **shuttles**.
- **Nivel 5.4** = nivel 5, shuttle 4: unos **3 minutos y medio** de carrera (⚠ verifica).
- Normalmente hay un **aviso** si no llegas y quedas fuera si fallas otra vez (⚠ confirma las reglas con Hampshire).
:::

### What to train

The test is **short and with turns**, so you need:

1. **Aerobic base** – your weekly **long run** (Saturday) and easy jogs (Friday).
2. **Intervals** – Tuesday: e.g. **6 × 1 minute fast / 1 minute walk**, building to **8 × 90 s**.
3. **Turning technique** – practise **shuttles over 15 m**: turn on the **same foot**, low centre of gravity, touch the line, don't over-run.
4. **Practice tests** – every **2–3 weeks** do a bleep test (free apps/audio exist) and record the level.
5. **Tennis** helps: lots of short sprints and changes of direction.

:::es
Necesitas: **base aeróbica** (rodaje largo del sábado y trote del viernes), **intervalos** (martes: 6 × 1 min rápido / 1 min andando, hasta 8 × 90 s), **técnica de giro** (giros cortos sobre el mismo pie, tocar la línea sin pasarte), **tests de prueba** cada 2–3 semanas (y registrar el nivel) y el **tenis**, que entrena sprints y cambios de dirección.
:::

### Sample progression (8 weeks, adapt to you)

| Weeks | Tuesday intervals | Saturday long run | Shuttle practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 6 × 1′ fast / 1′ walk | 20–25 min easy | 2 × 3 min at a comfortable rhythm |
| 3–4 | 8 × 1′ / 1′ | 25–30 min | test to find your level |
| 5–6 | 6 × 90″ / 90″ | 30–35 min | 3 × 3 min near test speed |
| 7–8 | 8 × 90″ / 60″ | 35–40 min | full practice test |

Increase **gradually** (about 10 % more volume per week is a common, prudent guide).

:::es
Progresión orientativa de 8 semanas; **sube poco a poco** (más o menos un 10 % semanal). Si algo duele, para y consulta.
:::

## Scenarios

1. First practice test: level 4.6. → You are close. Intervals + turning practice; retest in 3 weeks.
2. Practice test: 5.4 reached but exhausted. → Aim for **6.x** to have a margin on test day (nerves, a bad night…).

## Common mistakes & traps

- Starting too fast at the low levels – the early levels are **slow**; relax.
- Bad turns (wide turns, stopping completely).
- Only running long, slow distances and never practising turns.

## Summary

1. 15 m shuttles; reach the line before the beep.
2. Target: level 5.4 (confirm with Hampshire); aim higher for margin.
3. Train: aerobic base + intervals + turning technique.
4. Practice test every 2–3 weeks and record it in the app.
5. Progress gradually; GP if you have health concerns.
