> 🩺 Si eres nueva en el gym o tienes alguna lesión, pide a un **entrenador cualificado** que revise tu técnica y consulta a tu **GP** si tienes dudas de salud.

## Objectives

- Organise the **three gym sessions** of your plan (Monday A, Wednesday B, Friday C).
- Use **safe technique** and a simple **progression**.
- Support running, tennis and police work (lifting, restraint, injury prevention).

## Explanation

### Why strength matters for you

Strength training helps **body composition** (more muscle, healthier weight loss), **prevents injuries** in running and tennis, and prepares you for the **physical side of policing** (e.g. the **Job Related Fitness Test** includes other elements in some forces ⚠ VERIFICAR; officer safety training).

:::es
La fuerza mejora la **composición corporal**, **previene lesiones** (running y tenis) y te prepara para la parte **física del trabajo policial** (formación de seguridad personal).
:::

### The three sessions (full body, 45–60 min)

Warm-up 8–10 min: easy cardio + mobility (hips, ankles, shoulders).

| Session | Main exercises (3 sets × 8–12 reps) | Accessory (2–3 sets) |
|---|---|---|
| **A – Monday** | Goblet squat · Dumbbell bench press · Seated row | Plank · Glute bridge |
| **B – Wednesday** | Romanian deadlift (dumbbells) · Lat pulldown · Split squat | Side plank · Calf raises |
| **C – Friday** | Leg press or step-ups · Overhead press (dumbbells) · Single-arm row | Dead bug · Farmer's carry |

**Progression**: when you can do **all sets with 12 good reps**, increase the weight a little and go back to 8 reps. Leave **1–2 reps in reserve** (don't go to failure).

:::es
Tres sesiones de **cuerpo completo** (45–60 min) con calentamiento. **3 series de 8–12 repeticiones** en los ejercicios principales. **Progresión**: cuando hagas todas las series con 12 buenas repeticiones, sube un poco el peso y vuelve a 8. Deja **1–2 repeticiones en reserva**.
:::

### Fitting it with running and tennis

- **Friday** gym C + easy jog: do the **gym first**, jog after (short and easy).
- Avoid **heavy leg work** the day before your **interval session** or a **practice bleep test**.
- **Sunday** is active rest: walk, mobility, stretching.

:::es
Viernes: primero gym y luego trote suave. Evita pierna muy pesada el día antes de intervalos o de un bleep test de prueba. Domingo: **descanso activo**.
:::

## Scenarios

1. Monday after a long academy day and you're very tired. → Do a **shorter** session (main exercises only, 2 sets) instead of skipping completely.
2. Knee pain in split squats. → Stop that exercise, try a pain-free variation, and get advice if it continues.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Lifting too heavy with poor technique.
- Never increasing the weight (no progression).
- Skipping the warm-up.
- Doing only cardio for weight loss.

## Summary

1. Three full-body sessions: A (Mon), B (Wed), C (Fri).
2. 3 × 8–12 reps; increase weight when 12 reps are easy.
3. Warm-up always; technique before weight.
4. Plan around intervals and practice tests.
5. Tired? Shorter session, not zero.
