## Explanation

### PIP – four levels

| Level | Who | Typical investigations |
|---|---|---|
| **PIP 1** | patrol officers, investigators of volume crime | priority and volume crime (e.g. thefts, minor assaults) |
| **PIP 2** | **detectives (DCs, DSs)** and specialist investigators | **serious and complex** crime (e.g. serious assaults, sexual offences, robbery, fraud) |
| **PIP 3** | **Senior Investigating Officers (SIOs)** | **major crime** (e.g. homicide, kidnap) |
| **PIP 4** | strategic leads | **strategic oversight** of major/complex investigations |

Each level involves **training**, **workplace assessment** (a portfolio of evidence from real investigations) and **accreditation**, then **continuing professional development (CPD)**.

:::es
**PIP** tiene 4 niveles: **1** (delitos comunes), **2** (**detectives**: delitos graves y complejos), **3** (**SIO**: delitos mayores como homicidios) y **4** (supervisión estratégica). Cada nivel: **formación + evaluación en el puesto (portafolio) + acreditación** y luego desarrollo profesional continuo.
:::

### Your route: DCEP → PIP 2

1. **Initial training** at the academy (law, procedure, investigation skills).
2. **NIE** – you must **pass** it (80 questions, 70 scored, 39/70) as part of becoming a PIP 2 investigator.
3. **Workplace** – working on real cases with a tutor/assessor, building your **portfolio**.
4. **Accreditation** as a **PIP 2 detective**.

## Police scenarios

1. A colleague says "she's PIP 2 accredited". → She is an accredited **detective** for serious and complex investigations.
2. An SIO leads a murder investigation. → **PIP 3**.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Thinking passing the NIE alone makes you a detective – you also need **workplace assessment** and **accreditation**.
- Confusing **PIP levels** with **ranks** – a DS and a DC can both be PIP 2.

## Summary

1. PIP 1 volume · PIP 2 serious/complex (detectives) · PIP 3 major (SIO) · PIP 4 strategic.
2. Training + workplace assessment + accreditation + CPD.
3. NIE is required for PIP 2.
4. DCEP route: academy → NIE → portfolio → accreditation.
5. PIP level ≠ rank.
