## Objectives

- Know what **PACE 1984** is and why it matters to every investigation.
- Remember **what each Code of Practice (A–H)** covers.
- Know the main **sections** you will hear about (s. 1, 17, 18, 19, 24, 32, 37, 40–44, 76, 78).

## Explanation

### PACE 1984

The **Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984** sets out the main **police powers** (stop and search, entry, search, seizure, arrest, detention) and the **safeguards** for suspects. Its **Codes of Practice** give detailed rules. A breach of a Code is **not an offence** in itself, but it can lead to **evidence being excluded** (ss. 76, 78) and to **misconduct** proceedings. The Codes must be **available** to detainees and the public.

:::es
**PACE 1984** regula los **poderes** policiales (registro, entrada, incautación, detención, custodia) y las **garantías** del sospechoso. Sus **Códigos** detallan cómo hacerlo. Incumplirlos **no es delito**, pero puede llevar a **excluir pruebas** (ss. 76, 78) y a expedientes disciplinarios.
:::

### The Codes – memorise this table

| Code | Covers | NIE chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Stop and search** (and recording) | – (initial training) |
| **B** | **Search of premises** and **seizure** of property | **1.6** |
| **C** | **Detention, treatment and questioning** (non-terrorism) | **1.7** |
| **D** | **Identification** (ID procedures, fingerprints, DNA, photos) | **1.8** |
| **E** | **Audio recording** of interviews | 1.9 |
| **F** | **Visual (video) recording** of interviews | 1.9 |
| **G** | **Statutory power of arrest** (s. 24, necessity) | – |
| **H** | **Detention, treatment and questioning** under the **Terrorism Act 2000** | 2.4 |

:::es
**A** registro de personas · **B** registro de inmuebles e incautación · **C** detención, trato e interrogatorio · **D** identificación · **E** grabación de **audio** de entrevistas · **F** grabación de **vídeo** · **G** poder de **detención** · **H** detención por **terrorismo**. Truco: *All Buildings Custody Display Ears Film Grab Hazard*.

### Key sections (overview)

- **s. 1** stop and search · **s. 8** search warrants · **s. 17** entry without warrant (e.g. to arrest, save life) · **s. 18** search of premises after arrest (indictable offence) · **s. 19** general power of seizure · **s. 24** arrest without warrant · **s. 32** search upon arrest.
- **s. 37** detention before charge · **s. 40** reviews · **s. 41** 24-hour limit · **s. 42** superintendent's extension (to 36 h, indictable offences) · **ss. 43–44** warrants of further detention (max 96 h).
- **s. 76** confessions (oppression/unreliability) · **s. 78** exclusion of unfair evidence.

(⚠ Estudiarás cada una a fondo en los capítulos NIE 1.6–1.9; aquí solo es el mapa.)

:::es
Secciones clave (solo el mapa): s. 1 registro; s. 8 órdenes; s. 17 entrada sin orden; s. 18 registro tras detención; s. 19 incautación; s. 24 detención; s. 32 registro al detener; s. 37 detención antes de acusar; s. 40 revisiones; s. 41 límite de 24 h; s. 42 prórroga a 36 h; ss. 43–44 hasta 96 h; s. 76 confesiones; s. 78 exclusión de pruebas injustas.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. A colleague asks "which Code covers ID parades?" → **Code D**.
2. Defence argues your interview was unfair because the suspect wasn't given legal advice. → Possible **s. 78** exclusion; relevant **Code C**.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Mixing **E** (audio) and **F** (video).
- Thinking Code **G** is about detention – it's **arrest**.
- Thinking Code **H** is for all detainees – only **terrorism** detentions.

## Summary

1. PACE 1984 = powers + safeguards; Codes = detail.
2. A stop & search · B premises · C custody · D ID · E audio · F video · G arrest · H terrorism.
3. Breach of a Code ≠ offence, but evidence may be excluded (ss. 76, 78).
4. Detention: 24 h → 36 h (supt) → 96 h (magistrates).
5. Codes link to NIE chapters 1.6–1.9.
