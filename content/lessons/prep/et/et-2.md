> ⚠ El **Code of Ethics** se renovó en **2024**. Lo que sigue es un resumen con mis palabras; consulta la versión oficial del College of Policing (enlace abajo) y lo que te enseñen en la academia.

## Objectives

- Know what the **Code of Ethics** is, who it applies to and why it exists.
- Remember the **three ethical policing principles** of the 2024 Code: **courage, respect and empathy, public service** (⚠ VERIFICAR).
- Link the Code to the **National Decision Model (NDM)** and the **Standards of Professional Behaviour**.

## Explanation

### What it is

The **Code of Ethics** is published by the **College of Policing**. It describes the **values and behaviours** expected of **everyone in policing** – officers, staff, volunteers and contractors – on and off duty. It helps you **make good decisions**, especially when the law or procedures don't give a clear answer.

The 2024 version is built around **ethical policing principles** (⚠ VERIFICAR wording):

- **Courage** – doing the right thing even when it is difficult, challenging poor behaviour, being honest about mistakes.
- **Respect and empathy** – treating everyone with dignity, understanding other people's situation, valuing difference.
- **Public service** – acting in the public interest, being accountable and transparent.

Alongside it there is a **Code of Practice for Ethical Policing** (statutory, aimed at chief officers and organisations).

:::es
El **Código Ético** (College of Policing) describe los **valores y conductas** esperados de **todas las personas** que trabajan en la policía, de servicio o fuera de él. La versión **2024** se organiza en principios (⚠ verifica la redacción exacta):
- **Courage** (valentía): hacer lo correcto aunque cueste, denunciar malas conductas, reconocer errores.
- **Respect and empathy** (respeto y empatía): dignidad para todos, entender a los demás.
- **Public service** (servicio público): interés público, rendir cuentas, transparencia.
:::

### The Code and decisions: the NDM

The **National Decision Model** has the **Code of Ethics at its centre**, surrounded by five stages:

1. **Gather** information and intelligence.
2. **Assess** threat and risk and develop a working strategy.
3. **Consider** powers and policy.
4. **Identify** options and contingencies.
5. **Take action** and review what happened.

At every stage ask: *"Is this consistent with the Code of Ethics?"*

:::es
El **National Decision Model (NDM)** pone el **Código Ético en el centro** y tiene cinco fases: **recoger información → valorar amenaza y riesgo → considerar poderes y normas → identificar opciones → actuar y revisar**. En cada fase: *¿es coherente con el Código Ético?*
:::

### The Code and misconduct

The **Standards of Professional Behaviour** (in the Police (Conduct) Regulations 2020) set the **standards that can lead to misconduct proceedings** if breached (honesty and integrity; authority, respect and courtesy; equality and diversity; use of force; orders and instructions; duties and responsibilities; confidentiality; fitness for work; discreditable conduct; challenging and reporting improper conduct). You will study them in lesson **ET3**.

:::es
Las **Standards of Professional Behaviour** (Reglamento de Conducta 2020) son las normas cuyo incumplimiento puede llevar a un **expediente disciplinario**. Las verás en **ET3**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. A colleague makes a joke about a victim's accent in the custody suite. → **Courage**: challenge it (calmly) and, if needed, report it. The duty to **challenge and report improper conduct** is a professional standard.
2. You make an error in your notebook. → Be honest: correct it in the proper way (no erasing) and tell your supervisor if it matters. **Honesty and integrity**.
3. A distressed victim is angry with you. → **Respect and empathy**: stay calm, listen, explain what will happen next.

:::es
1. Broma ofensiva de un compañero → **valentía**: cuestionarla y, si hace falta, informar.
2. Error en la libreta → **honestidad**: corregir correctamente, sin borrar.
3. Víctima enfadada → **respeto y empatía**: calma, escuchar, explicar los siguientes pasos.
:::

## Common mistakes & traps

- Thinking the Code applies only **on duty** – it applies **off duty** too (especially social media).
- Thinking it is only for **officers** – it applies to **everyone in policing**.
- Confusing the **Code of Ethics** (values and decision-making) with the **Standards of Professional Behaviour** (basis for misconduct).

## Summary

1. Code of Ethics = values and behaviours for everyone in policing, on and off duty.
2. 2024 principles: courage, respect and empathy, public service (⚠ VERIFICAR).
3. Code of Ethics is at the centre of the NDM.
4. NDM: gather → assess → powers/policy → options → act & review.
5. Standards of Professional Behaviour = misconduct framework (ET3).
