---

## Objectives

- Use **accurate, respectful terms** for ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation and age.
- Know the **monitored strands of hate crime** in England and Wales.
- Avoid outdated or offensive words that may still appear in older documents.

## Explanation

### Ethnicity and race

- Be **specific** where relevant: *Black Caribbean*, *Pakistani*, *Polish*, *Gypsy or Irish Traveller* – usually the person's **self-defined ethnicity** (DE3).
- The UK government recommends **not using "BAME"** (Black, Asian and Minority Ethnic) as a catch-all; prefer "**ethnic minorities**" or specific groups (⚠ VERIFICAR la política de tu fuerza).
- Avoid: *coloured*, *half-caste*, *oriental* (for people), and any slurs. Use **"mixed ethnicity"** or the person's own term.
- Nationality ≠ ethnicity ≠ religion.

:::es
Sé **específica** (*Black Caribbean*, *Pakistani*…), idealmente con la **etnia autodefinida**. El Gobierno recomienda **no usar "BAME"** como etiqueta general. Evita *coloured*, *half-caste*, *oriental* (para personas). Nacionalidad ≠ etnia ≠ religión.
:::

### Religion and belief

- Use correct names: **Muslim** (not *Mohammedan*), **Jewish person** (prefer to *a Jew* in some contexts), **Sikh**, **Hindu**, **Buddhist**, **Christian**, **atheist / non-religious**.
- Places of worship: **mosque**, **synagogue**, **gurdwara** (Sikh), **mandir/temple** (Hindu), **church**.
- Respect practices during searches and in custody (e.g. **religious items**, **head coverings**, **dietary needs**, **prayer times**) – PACE Codes and force policy guide this.

:::es
Nombres correctos (Muslim, Jewish, Sikh, Hindu…) y lugares de culto (**mosque**, **synagogue**, **gurdwara**, **mandir**). Respeta prácticas religiosas en registros y custodia (objetos religiosos, cubrirse la cabeza, dieta, oración).
:::

### Sexual orientation and gender identity

- **gay**, **lesbian**, **bisexual**, **straight/heterosexual**; **LGBT+**.
- Avoid **"homosexual"** as a noun in everyday speech (sounds clinical/old-fashioned) and never use slurs.
- Use **"partner"** if you don't know the gender of someone's partner.
- **trans**, **transgender**, **non-binary**; respect names and pronouns (ET1).

:::es
**gay, lesbian, bisexual**; evita *homosexual* como sustantivo en el día a día; usa **partner** si no sabes el género de la pareja; **trans, non-binary**: respeta nombre y pronombres.
:::

### Age

- **older people / older adults** rather than *the elderly*, *OAPs*, *pensioners* (in police reports, age + facts).
- **young people**, **children** (under 18 in law); avoid *kids*, *yobs*, *hoodies* in reports.

### Hate crime – monitored strands

Police record hate crime where the victim **or any other person** perceives it to be motivated by **hostility or prejudice** based on: **race**, **religion**, **sexual orientation**, **disability**, **transgender identity**. (Other hostility – e.g. against sex workers or alternative subcultures – may be recorded locally.)

:::es
**Delitos de odio**: se registran si la víctima **o cualquier otra persona** percibe que hubo **hostilidad o prejuicio** por **raza, religión, orientación sexual, discapacidad o identidad trans**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. A victim says the attacker shouted a homophobic slur. → Record it as a **hate crime (sexual orientation)**; record the exact words in direct speech.
2. You need to search a Sikh man wearing a turban. → Follow PACE/force guidance: explain, offer privacy, ask him to remove it himself where possible (⚠ VERIFICAR procedimiento).

## Common mistakes

- Using outdated terms you learned in older textbooks or films.
- Guessing someone's religion from appearance.
- Not recording a hate crime because "the victim didn't say so" – **anyone** can perceive it.

## Summary

1. Specific ethnic terms; avoid BAME as a label and old offensive words.
2. Correct religious terms and respect for practices.
3. gay/lesbian/bisexual; partner; respect pronouns.
4. older people; young people.
5. Hate crime strands: race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, transgender identity – perception of victim or anyone.
