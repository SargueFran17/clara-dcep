## Objectives

- Use **at / on / in** for **places** in UK addresses and locations.
- Describe where people and objects were in a scene.
- Write a complete **"when + where"** sentence for a report.

## Explanation

| Preposition | Use it with | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | a point / a specific address / a building as a place of activity | **at** 12 Elm Road · **at** the junction **of** X and Y · **at** the station · **at** the bus stop · **at** the front door · **at** work |
| **ON** | surfaces, roads/streets (no number), floors, transport you can stand in | **on** Elm Road · **on** the A27 · **on** the M3 · **on** the second floor · **on** the bus/train · **on** the pavement · **on** the left |
| **IN** | inside an area or space; towns, cities, countries; cars | **in** Southsea · **in** Hampshire · **in** the kitchen · **in** the car · **in** a park · **in** the garden · **in** the queue |

Key UK details:

- With a **house number** use **at**: *at 12 Elm Road*. Without a number use **on**: *on Elm Road* (also *in* Elm Road is heard in the UK).
- Vehicles: **in** a car / taxi / van, but **on** a bus / train / bike / motorbike.
- Junctions: *at the junction **of** Elm Road **and** High Street*.
- Direction: *towards* / *away from* / *along* / *into* / *out of*.

:::es
- **AT** = punto concreto / dirección con número (*at 12 Elm Road*), *at the junction of X and Y*, *at the station*, *at the bus stop*.
- **ON** = calle o carretera **sin número** (*on Elm Road*, *on the A27*), superficies, plantas (*on the second floor*), transporte "en el que vas de pie": *on the bus / train / bike*.
- **IN** = dentro de un espacio, ciudades, condados, países, y **coches**: *in the car*.
- Dirección: *towards* (hacia), *away from* (alejándose de), *along* (a lo largo de), *into* (entrar en), *out of* (salir de).
:::

## Police scenarios

> **At** 14:30 **on** Monday 3 March 2026 I was **on** foot patrol **on** Palmerston Road, Southsea. I saw a male standing **at** the bus stop **outside** number 45. He got **into** a silver Ford Focus parked **on** the opposite side of the road and drove **towards** Clarence Parade. I followed **in** the marked police vehicle and stopped the car **at** the junction **of** Clarence Parade **and** Southsea Terrace.

:::es
Fíjate: *on foot patrol*, *on Palmerston Road* (sin número), *at the bus stop*, *outside number 45*, *got into* (subió a un coche), *in the police vehicle*, *at the junction of… and…*.
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *in the bus* → ✅ *on the bus*
- ❌ *on the car* → ✅ *in the car*
- ❌ *in 12 Elm Road* → ✅ *at 12 Elm Road*
- ❌ *in the junction* → ✅ *at the junction*
- ❌ *in the first floor* → ✅ *on the first floor* (UK first floor = Spanish *primera planta*; *ground floor* = *planta baja*)
- ❌ *entered in the house* → ✅ *entered the house* / *went into the house*

## Summary

1. AT + address with number, junctions, points.
2. ON + road without number, floors, buses/trains/bikes.
3. IN + towns/areas, rooms, cars.
4. Get **into/out of** a car; get **on/off** a bus.
5. Report formula: at [time] on [date] I was on/at/in [place].
