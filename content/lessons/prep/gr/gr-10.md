## Objectives

- Stop making the **most dangerous false-friend mistakes** for Spanish speakers in police work.
- Learn the **right English word** for what you mean in Spanish.
- Recognise false friends in the **manual and exam** questions.

## Explanation

### The big list

| English word | Means (Spanish) | NOT | For that, say… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **actually** | en realidad | actualmente | **currently / at the moment** |
| **eventually** | finalmente, al final | eventualmente | **possibly / occasionally** |
| **sensible** | sensato | sensible | **sensitive** |
| **assist** | ayudar | asistir (ir a) | **attend** |
| **attend** | asistir / acudir | atender | **deal with / serve / look after** |
| **discuss** | hablar de, tratar | discutir (pelear) | **argue** |
| **argument** | discusión, pelea; argumento (razonamiento) | – | – |
| **realise** | darse cuenta | realizar | **carry out / do** |
| **pretend** | fingir | pretender | **intend / aim** |
| **demand** | exigir | demandar (judicial) | **sue / take legal action** |
| **injury** | lesión | injuria | **insult / defamation** |
| **molest** | abusar sexualmente (¡grave!) | molestar | **bother / annoy / disturb** |
| **embarrassed** | avergonzado | embarazada | **pregnant** |
| **casualty** | víctima, herido (accidentes) | casualidad | **coincidence / chance** |
| **deception** | engaño (¡delito de fraude!) | decepción | **disappointment** |
| **sympathetic** | comprensivo | simpático | **nice / friendly** |
| **record** | registro, antecedentes, grabar | recordar | **remember / remind** |
| **exit** | salida | éxito | **success** |
| **library** | biblioteca | librería | **bookshop** |
| **constipated** | estreñido | constipado | **have a cold** |
| **prevent** | impedir, evitar | prevenir (avisar) | **warn** |
| **comprehensive** | completo, exhaustivo | comprensivo | **understanding** |
| **agenda** | orden del día | agenda (libreta) | **diary / planner** |
| **resume** | reanudar | resumir | **summarise** |
| **sentence** | pena; frase | sentencia (resolución) | **judgment** |
| **conviction** | condena | convicción | **belief** |

:::es
Las más peligrosas en tu trabajo: **molest** (= abusar sexualmente, no "molestar"), **deception** (= engaño, delito), **injury** (= lesión), **casualty** (= herido), **assist/attend**, **actually**, **eventually**, **sentence/conviction**.
:::

## Police scenarios

- ❌ *"The man was molesting the neighbours with loud music."* → this means **sexual abuse**! ✅ *"…was **disturbing / annoying** the neighbours."*
- ❌ *"I assisted to the scene."* → ✅ *"I **attended** the scene."*
- ❌ *"Actually he lives in Southsea."* (= currently) → ✅ *"He **currently** lives in Southsea."*
- ❌ *"There were no casualties, it was a casualty."* → ✅ *"There were no **casualties** (injured people); it was a **coincidence**."*

## Common exam traps

- *"obtaining by **deception**"* (old offences) and *"**deception**"* in fraud contexts = **engaño**.
- *"**actual** bodily harm"* = **real** (not "current").
- *"**sentence**"* = **pena**.

## Summary

1. actually = en realidad; currently = actualmente.
2. assist = ayudar; attend = acudir.
3. molest = abuso sexual – never for "molestar".
4. injury = lesión; casualty = herido; deception = engaño.
5. sentence = pena; conviction = condena.
