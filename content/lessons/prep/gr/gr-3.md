## Objectives

- Choose between **past simple** and **past continuous** when describing what happened.
- Use **when / while / as** to connect a background action with a short event.
- Write clear, chronological sentences for **witness statements** and **notebooks**.

## Explanation

### Past simple – completed actions, the story line

Form: verb + **-ed** (regular) or irregular form (*saw, went, ran, hit, took*).
Use it for **finished actions in sequence** – the main events of the story:

> *I **arrived** at 14:32. I **saw** a man near the car. He **ran** towards the park.*

### Past continuous – background, actions in progress

Form: **was / were + -ing**.
Use it for an action that was **in progress** at a moment, or as the **background** when something else happened:

> *At 14:30 I **was walking** along Elm Road.*
> *The victim **was standing** at the bus stop **when** the suspect **grabbed** her bag.*

**Long action (continuous) + short action that interrupts (simple).**

### Connectors

- **when** + past simple (the short event): *I was writing my notes **when** the radio **went** off.*
- **while / as** + past continuous (the background): ***While** I **was talking** to the witness, the suspect **left**.*

:::es
- **Past simple** (*arrived, saw, ran*): acciones **terminadas**, una detrás de otra → la "historia" principal.
- **Past continuous** (*was/were + -ing*): acción **en progreso** o **de fondo** cuando ocurre otra cosa.
- **Larga (continuous) + corta que la interrumpe (simple):** *She **was standing** at the bus stop **when** he **grabbed** her bag.*
- **when** + acción corta; **while/as** + acción larga.
:::

### Verbs we don't usually use in the continuous

State verbs: *know, believe, recognise, see (= notice), hear, want, own, seem*. → *I **recognised** him* (not *I was recognising*).

:::es
Verbos de **estado** (*know, believe, recognise, see, hear, want, own, seem*) no suelen ir en continuo: *I **recognised** him*, no *I was recognising him*.
:::

## Police scenarios

**Statement extract (model):**

> *At about 22:15 on Friday 6 November 2026 I **was walking** home along Albert Road, Southsea. **As** I **was passing** the Co-op, I **heard** shouting. I **turned** and **saw** two men. One **was holding** a glass bottle. He **hit** the other man on the head with it. The injured man **fell** to the ground. The man with the bottle **ran** off towards Victoria Road.*

:::es
Fíjate: el **fondo** (*was walking, was passing, was holding*) y los **hechos** en cadena (*heard, turned, saw, hit, fell, ran*).
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *I was seeing a man* → ✅ *I **saw** a man*.
- ❌ *When I arrived, the suspect run away* → ✅ *the suspect **ran** away*.
- ❌ *While he hit the victim…* (short action) → ✅ ***When** he hit the victim…* or *While he **was hitting** the victim…*
- Using the Spanish **imperfecto** logic for everything (*was going, was saying*) – in English the main events are **past simple**.

## Writing exercise ✍️

Write 5–6 sentences describing something you saw today (in the street, a shop, the gym) as if it were a statement.

**Self-check:**
- [ ] Time and place at the start (*At … on … I was …*).
- [ ] Background in past continuous.
- [ ] Main events in past simple, in order.
- [ ] At least one *when* and one *while/as*.
- [ ] No state verbs in the continuous.

## Summary

1. Past simple = completed actions, the main events.
2. Past continuous = background / in progress.
3. Long action (continuous) interrupted by short action (simple).
4. *when* + short event; *while/as* + long action.
5. State verbs (know, see, recognise) → simple.
