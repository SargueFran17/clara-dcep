## Objectives

- Use the core vocabulary of **evidence** and **investigation** correctly.
- Understand **exhibits, continuity and contamination** – words you'll use every day as a DC.
- Use typical **collocations**: *gather evidence, carry out enquiries, follow up a line of enquiry*.

## Explanation

### Evidence

- **evidence** is **uncountable**: *some evidence*, *a piece of evidence*, *the evidence shows…* (never *an evidence* / *evidences*).
- Types: **direct** (a witness saw it) and **circumstantial** (facts from which guilt may be inferred); **real** evidence (objects), **documentary**, **digital**, **forensic**, **hearsay**.
- **exhibit** – an item produced in court with a **reference** made from the initials of the person who found it + number: *JS/1*.
- **continuity** – a complete record of who had the exhibit and when, from seizure to court. **chain of custody** is the same idea.
- **contamination / cross-contamination** – evidence affected by contact with other material, people or scenes.
- **seize – retain – package – label – store – submit** (to the lab).

:::es
- **evidence** es **incontable**: *a piece of evidence*, nunca *an evidence*.
- **Exhibit** = pieza de convicción con referencia (iniciales + número: *JS/1*).
- **Continuity** = cadena de custodia: quién tuvo la prueba y cuándo.
- **Cross-contamination** = contaminación cruzada.
- Verbos: **seize** (incautar), **retain** (conservar), **package** (embalar), **label** (etiquetar), **submit** (enviar al laboratorio).
:::

### Investigation

| Collocation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **carry out / make enquiries** | investigate, ask questions, check things |
| **a line of enquiry** | something to check (*CCTV is a key line of enquiry*) |
| **follow up / pursue a lead** | check information that may help |
| **rule out / eliminate a suspect** | decide they were not involved |
| **trace a witness** | find them |
| **take a statement from** | record a witness's account |
| **gather / secure / preserve evidence** | collect and protect it |
| **identify a suspect** | find out who they are |
| **the golden hour** | the first period after a crime when evidence is easiest to secure |

:::es
**make enquiries** = hacer gestiones/averiguaciones; **line of enquiry** = línea de investigación; **rule out** = descartar; **trace** = localizar; **take a statement from** = tomar declaración a; **secure/preserve evidence** = asegurar/preservar pruebas; **golden hour** = la hora de oro.
:::

## Police scenarios

> *On arrival I **secured the scene** and started a **scene log**. I **seized** a kitchen knife from the hallway floor, **exhibit JS/1**, which I **packaged and labelled** and **booked into the property store**. **CCTV** from the shop opposite is an outstanding **line of enquiry**. House-to-house **enquiries** have **traced** one further witness.*

:::es
Observa cómo se encadenan: asegurar la escena → registro → incautar y etiquetar la prueba → almacén → líneas de investigación pendientes.
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *We have many evidences* → ✅ *We have a lot of evidence*.
- ❌ *I did investigations* → ✅ *I **made enquiries** / I **carried out** enquiries*.
- ❌ *prove* (noun) → the noun is **proof**; the verb is **prove**. *Prueba* (evidence) is usually **evidence**.
- ❌ *Discard a suspect* → ✅ *rule out / eliminate*.
- British spelling: **enquiries** (UK police use *enquiry*; *inquiry* is often used for a formal public inquiry).

## Summary

1. Evidence is uncountable.
2. Exhibit reference: initials + number (JS/1).
3. Continuity = who had it, when.
4. Make enquiries; follow up a line of enquiry; rule out.
5. Secure and preserve evidence in the golden hour.
