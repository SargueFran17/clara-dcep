## Objectives

- Understand the vocabulary of the **custody suite** and the people who work there.
- Know the words for a detainee's **rights and entitlements**.
- Use the language of **detention time limits and outcomes** (charge, bail, RUI, NFA).

## Explanation

### Places and people

- **custody suite** – the area where prisoners are held. **cell** – the room. **booking-in desk** – where the prisoner is registered.
- **custody officer** – usually a **sergeant**, responsible for the detainee's welfare and rights. **detention officer** – police staff who help.
- **detained person (DP) / detainee / prisoner** – the person in custody.
- **appropriate adult (AA)** – supports a child or vulnerable adult. **interpreter**. **solicitor / duty solicitor**. **healthcare professional (HCP)**.

:::es
**custody suite** = zona de custodia/calabozos; **cell** = celda; **custody officer** = sargento de custodia (responsable de derechos y bienestar). **detainee / DP** = detenido. **appropriate adult** = adulto que acompaña a menores o personas vulnerables. **duty solicitor** = abogado de oficio.
:::

### Rights and entitlements (PACE Code C – general idea)

When a person is booked in, the custody officer tells them about their **rights**:

1. to have **someone informed** of their arrest;
2. to **consult a solicitor** (free independent legal advice);
3. to **consult the Codes of Practice**.

Detainees also get a **notice of entitlements** (e.g. food, drink, medical help, an interpreter). Words: *to be **entitled** to*, *to **waive*** (give up) a right, *to **request***.

:::es
Derechos básicos al registrarse: que **avisen a alguien**, **hablar con un abogado** (gratis) y **consultar los Códigos de PACE**. Además, un **aviso de "entitlements"** (comida, bebida, médico, intérprete…). **waive a right** = renunciar a un derecho.
:::

### Time and outcomes

- **relevant time** – when the detention clock starts. **review** – an inspector checks detention is still necessary. **extension** – a superintendent can authorise more time (indictable offences). **warrant of further detention** – from magistrates.
- Outcomes: **charge** (formal accusation) · **bail** (release with a duty to return/attend court) · **released under investigation (RUI)** · **no further action (NFA)** · **caution** (an out-of-court disposal – *not* the same as the arrest caution!) · **remanded in custody**.

:::es
**relevant time** = inicio del cómputo; **review** = revisión (inspector); **extension** = prórroga (superintendente). Resultados: **charge** (acusación formal), **bail** (libertad con obligación de volver), **RUI** (libertad mientras se investiga), **NFA** (archivo), **caution** (amonestación formal – ojo, palabra distinta de la "caution" de la detención), **remand** (prisión preventiva).
:::

## Police scenarios

> I presented the detainee to the **custody officer**, Sgt PATEL, and gave the **circumstances of arrest**. Sgt PATEL **authorised detention** to secure and preserve evidence and to obtain evidence by questioning. The detainee **requested** a solicitor and **declined** to have anyone informed. A **risk assessment** was carried out and he was placed on **30-minute observations**.

:::es
Frases útiles: "**presented** the detainee to the custody officer", "**authorised detention** to…", "**requested** a solicitor", "**declined** to have anyone informed", "**30-minute observations**" (observación cada 30 minutos).
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *The custody* (as a place) → ✅ *the **custody suite*** / *in custody*.
- ❌ *He has right to a lawyer* → ✅ *He has **the** right to a solicitor / He is **entitled to** legal advice*.
- ❌ *He was put in liberty* → ✅ *He was **released** (on bail / under investigation)*.
- Confusing the two **cautions**: the *words said on arrest/interview* vs. the *formal out-of-court warning*.

## Summary

1. Custody suite, cell, custody officer (sergeant), detainee.
2. Three rights: someone informed, legal advice, consult the Codes.
3. *Entitled to*, *request*, *decline*, *waive*.
4. Relevant time → reviews → extensions.
5. Outcomes: charge, bail, RUI, NFA, caution, remand.
