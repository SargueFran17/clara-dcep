## Objectives

- Use the key words of an **arrest** correctly in speech and in writing.
- Tell apart verbs that look similar: **arrest / detain / stop / apprehend**.
- Describe an arrest in a **report** with the right collocations.

## Explanation

### Core words

| Word | Plain English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **to arrest** (someone **for** an offence) | to take someone into custody using a legal power | *I arrested him **for** theft.* |
| **to detain** | to keep someone, they are not free to go | *He was **detained** for the purpose of a search.* |
| **to stop** | to make someone or a vehicle stop | *I **stopped** the vehicle on the A27.* |
| **to apprehend** (formal) | to catch, often after a chase | *The suspect was **apprehended** in a garden.* |
| **grounds** (always plural here) | the reasons/facts behind a decision | *My **grounds** for arrest were…* |
| **necessity** | why the arrest was necessary (PACE Code G) | *The arrest was **necessary** to prevent…* |
| **to caution** someone | to say the caution words | *I **cautioned** him and he replied…* |
| **reply after caution** | what the person says after the caution | *His **reply** was "I didn't do it".* |
| **to resist / to struggle** | to fight against the arrest | *She **resisted** and was handcuffed.* |
| **to be compliant** | to cooperate | *He was **compliant** throughout.* |
| **to convey** | to transport (a prisoner) | *He was **conveyed** to Southampton custody.* |
| **to book in** | to register a prisoner in custody | *He was **booked in** at 15:20.* |

**Prepositions that Spanish speakers often get wrong:**

- arrest someone **for** an offence (not *by* / *of*): *arrested **for** burglary*.
- arrest someone **on suspicion of** an offence: *arrested **on suspicion of** assault*.
- **under** section 24 PACE: *arrested **under** section 24*.
- **in** handcuffs, **in** custody, **on** bail.

:::es
- **arrest** = detener formalmente (con un poder legal). **detain** = retener (no es libre de irse). **stop** = parar (persona o vehículo). **apprehend** = atrapar (formal).
- **grounds** = motivos / fundamentos (en plural). **necessity** = por qué la detención era *necesaria*.
- Preposiciones: arrest **for** (un delito), **on suspicion of** (por sospecha de), **under** section 24 (en virtud de), **in** custody, **on** bail.
- **convey** = trasladar al detenido; **book in** = registrarlo en custodia.
:::

### "Suspicion" family

- **suspect** (noun): *the suspect*. **suspect** (verb): *I suspected that he…*. **suspicion** (noun): *on suspicion of*. **suspicious** (adj.): *a suspicious vehicle*.
- Stress: the noun is **SUS**pect, the verb is sus**PECT**.

:::es
**SUS**pect (sustantivo: el sospechoso) vs. sus**PECT** (verbo: sospechar). **suspicion** = sospecha; **suspicious** = sospechoso (adjetivo: "a suspicious car").
:::

## Police scenarios

**Report extract (model):**

> At 14:32 on Monday 3 March I attended Elm Road, Southsea, following a report of a shoplifter. I saw a male matching the description. I **stopped** him and explained my grounds. I **arrested him for theft** at 14:40 because I had reasonable grounds to suspect he had stolen two bottles of whisky and the arrest was **necessary** to prevent the loss of evidence. I **cautioned** him and he made **no reply**. He was **compliant**, was **handcuffed** to the rear and was **conveyed** to Portsmouth custody, where he was **booked in** at 15:05.

:::es
Fíjate en el orden típico de un informe: **hora → lugar → motivo → lo que viste → detención (delito + motivos + necesidad) → caution y respuesta → actitud → esposas → traslado → registro en custodia**.
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *I detained him for theft* when you mean a formal arrest → ✅ *I **arrested** him **for** theft*.
- ❌ *He was arrested by robbery* → ✅ *arrested **for** robbery*.
- ❌ *The ground of the arrest* → ✅ *my **grounds** for arrest*.
- ❌ *He made resistance* → ✅ *he **resisted** / he **struggled***.
- ❌ *I carried him to the station* → ✅ *he was **conveyed** / **transported** to custody*.

## Summary

1. Arrest someone **for** an offence / **on suspicion of** an offence / **under** s. 24.
2. Arrest ≠ detain ≠ stop.
3. *Grounds* (plural) + *necessity* explain the arrest.
4. *Caution* → *reply after caution*.
5. *Convey* to custody → *book in*.
