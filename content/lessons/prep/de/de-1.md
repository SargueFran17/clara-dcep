## Objectives

- Give a **complete description** of a person in a fixed order, quickly, by radio or in writing.
- Use **UK units** for height (feet and inches) and the right words for **build, hair and face**.
- Avoid **subjective or offensive** words.

## Explanation

### The order (head to toe)

Use the same order every time. Controllers and colleagues expect it, and you won't forget anything:

1. **Sex** (male / female / unknown) and **approximate age** (*aged 25 to 30*).
2. **Height** – in **feet and inches**: *5 ft 8 in* ("five foot eight").
3. **Build** – *slim, medium, stocky, heavy, athletic, thin*.
4. **Ethnic appearance** – see lesson **DE3** (self-defined ethnicity vs IC codes). On the radio, many forces use **IC codes**.
5. **Hair** – colour, length, style: *short dark brown hair, shaved at the sides*, *long blonde hair in a ponytail*, *bald*, *receding*.
6. **Face** – *clean-shaven, stubble, beard, moustache, glasses, freckles*; eye colour if seen.
7. **Distinguishing marks** – *tattoos, scars, piercings, birthmarks* (where on the body and what they show).
8. **Clothing**, top to bottom (lesson **DE2**).
9. **Other** – accent, voice, gait (way of walking), what they carried, direction of travel.

:::es
**Orden fijo (de la cabeza a los pies):** sexo y edad aproximada → **altura en pies y pulgadas** → complexión → apariencia étnica (lección DE3) → pelo → cara → **señas particulares** (tatuajes, cicatrices, piercings) → ropa (DE2) → otros (acento, voz, forma de andar, qué llevaba, dirección de huida).
:::

### Height: metric → imperial

| cm | feet & inches |
|---|---|
| 152 | 5 ft 0 in |
| 160 | 5 ft 3 in |
| 165 | 5 ft 5 in |
| 170 | 5 ft 7 in |
| 175 | 5 ft 9 in |
| 180 | 5 ft 11 in |
| 185 | 6 ft 1 in |
| 190 | 6 ft 3 in |

Rule: **1 inch = 2.54 cm**, **1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm**. Quick trick: subtract 152 cm (= 5 ft) and divide the rest by 2.5 → inches over 5 ft.

:::es
**1 pulgada = 2,54 cm; 1 pie = 12 pulgadas ≈ 30,5 cm.** Truco: resta 152 cm (= 5 pies) y divide lo que queda entre 2,5 → pulgadas por encima de 5 pies. Ej.: 170 cm → 18 / 2,5 ≈ 7 → **5 ft 7 in**.
:::

### Neutral, factual words

- ✅ *slim / heavy build* · ❌ *skinny / fat*
- ✅ *appeared to be aged 30–35* · ❌ *old*
- ✅ describe what you **saw** · ❌ opinions (*ugly, dodgy, scruffy-looking*)

## Police scenarios

**Radio description (model):**

> "Male, aged 20 to 25, about 5 ft 10, slim build, [ethnic appearance/IC code], short dark hair, clean-shaven, tattoo of a rose on the left side of the neck. Wearing a black hooded top, hood up, grey tracksuit bottoms and white trainers. Carrying a blue rucksack. Last seen on foot towards Commercial Road."

:::es
Modelo para la radio: sexo, edad, altura, complexión, apariencia, pelo, cara, señas, ropa de arriba abajo, qué llevaba y **última dirección**.
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- Giving height in **centimetres** – the UK police use **feet and inches**.
- ❌ *He had 25 years* → ✅ *He was 25* / *aged 25*.
- ❌ *Thin hair* (when you mean short) → ✅ *short hair*; *thin(ning) hair* = poco pelo.
- ❌ *Moustaches* (one person) → ✅ *a moustache*.
- ❌ *Rubio* = *blond(e)*; *castaño* = *brown*; *moreno* (pelo) = *dark-haired*.

## Summary

1. Always the same order: head to toe.
2. Sex, age range, height (ft/in), build.
3. Hair, face, distinguishing marks.
4. Clothing top to bottom, items carried, direction.
5. Factual and neutral words only.
