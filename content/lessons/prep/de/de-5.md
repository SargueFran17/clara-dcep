## Objectives

- Write and say **UK addresses** in the correct order.
- Read **postcodes** and know the local ones (PO, SO, GU, RG, BH…).
- Describe **locations** precisely: roads, junctions, landmarks, directions of travel.

## Explanation

### UK address order

1. **Flat / building** (if any): *Flat 2*, *Ocean House*
2. **Number + street**: *14 Elm Road*
3. **Locality** (if needed): *Southsea*
4. **Town/city**: *Portsmouth*
5. **County** (optional): *Hampshire*
6. **Postcode**: *PO5 3AB*

> *Flat 2, 14 Elm Road, Southsea, Portsmouth, PO5 3AB*

Numbers go **before** the street (not "Elm Road 14"). Odd numbers usually on one side, even on the other.

:::es
Orden: **piso/edificio → número + calle → barrio → ciudad → condado (opcional) → código postal**. El número va **antes** de la calle (*14 Elm Road*, no *Elm Road 14*).
:::

### Postcodes

**PO5 3AB** = **outward code** (*PO5*: area + district) + **inward code** (*3AB*: sector + unit, a few addresses).

Local areas: **PO** Portsmouth (and IoW, e.g. PO30–PO41) · **SO** Southampton · **GU** Guildford (north-east Hampshire) · **RG** Reading (north Hampshire, e.g. Basingstoke) · **BH** Bournemouth · **SP** Salisbury (west Hampshire edge). (⚠ VERIFICAR distritos concretos.)

On the radio, spell postcodes with the **phonetic alphabet**: *"Papa Oscar five, three Alpha Bravo."*

:::es
**PO5 3AB** = parte de **salida** (área + distrito) + parte de **entrada** (sector + unidad: unas pocas casas). Locales: **PO** Portsmouth e Isla de Wight, **SO** Southampton, **GU** Guildford, **RG** Reading/Basingstoke, **BH** Bournemouth. En la radio, con alfabeto fonético.
:::

### Describing a location

- **Roads**: **M27** (motorway), **A3**, **B2177**; *the A27 **eastbound/westbound***; *junction 5 of the M27*.
- **Junctions**: *at the junction of X and Y*; *roundabout*; *slip road*; *lay-by*.
- **Landmarks**: *opposite the Co-op*, *next to the bus stop outside number 45*, *the alley behind the school*.
- **Directions**: *northbound*, *towards the city centre*, *away from the seafront*.
- Many forces also use **what3words** (three-word locations) and **grid references** (⚠ VERIFICAR uso en Hampshire).

:::es
**Carreteras**: M (autopista), A, B; *eastbound* (dirección este); *junction 5 of the M27*. **Cruces**: *at the junction of X and Y*, rotonda, *slip road* (incorporación), *lay-by* (apartadero). **Referencias**: *opposite* (enfrente), *next to*, *behind*. Muchas fuerzas usan **what3words**.
:::

## Police scenarios

> *"Control, 2-1-4, request a unit to the alley **behind** the Spar **on** Fawcett Road, **at the junction with** Albert Road, Southsea, **Papa Oscar four**. Suspect last seen **heading towards** the seafront."*

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- Putting the number after the street.
- Writing the postcode without the space (*PO53AB*) – keep the space: **PO5 3AB**.
- *"In the corner of"* → ✅ *on the corner of* (streets) / *at the junction of*.

## Summary

1. Flat → number + street → locality → town → (county) → postcode.
2. Postcode = outward (PO5) + inward (3AB).
3. Local: PO, SO, GU, RG, BH.
4. Roads: M/A/B; eastbound; junction numbers.
5. Landmarks + direction; what3words if used.
