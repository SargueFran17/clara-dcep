## Objectives

- Describe a vehicle in the standard order: **colour, make, model, type, registration**.
- Read and decode a **UK registration plate** (age identifier).
- Use vehicle vocabulary for damage and distinguishing features.

## Explanation

### Order of a vehicle description

**C-M-M-T-R** → **Colour – Make – Model – Type – Registration** (+ distinguishing features, direction of travel, occupants).

> *"**Silver Ford Focus hatchback**, registration **Hotel November two-six, Alpha Bravo Charlie**, damage to the nearside rear door, two occupants, last seen on the A27 eastbound."*

Types: **hatchback**, **saloon**, **estate**, **SUV/4x4**, **MPV/people carrier**, **coupé**, **convertible**, **van**, **pick-up**, **motorbike/moped**, **HGV/lorry**.

:::es
Orden **C-M-M-T-R**: **color, marca, modelo, tipo, matrícula** (+ señas, dirección y ocupantes). Tipos: *hatchback* (compacto con portón), *saloon* (berlina), *estate* (familiar), *SUV*, *MPV* (monovolumen), *van* (furgoneta), *lorry/HGV* (camión).
:::

### UK registration plates (current format since 2001)

Example: **HN26 ABC**

- **HN** – **memory tag**: the first letter is the **region** (H = Hampshire area/South), the second the **DVLA office** (⚠ VERIFICAR asignaciones exactas).
- **26** – **age identifier**: **March** registrations use the **year** (26 = March–August 2026); **September** registrations add **50** (76 = September 2026–February 2027).
- **ABC** – **random** letters.

**Front plate: white. Rear plate: yellow.** Private ("cherished") plates can hide the age. Foreign vehicles have their own plates (e.g. Spanish: 4 numbers + 3 letters).

:::es
Formato desde 2001: **HN26 ABC** → **HN** = región + oficina; **26** = identificador de edad (**marzo**: el año → 26 = marzo–agosto 2026; **septiembre**: año + 50 → 76 = septiembre 2026–febrero 2027); **ABC** = letras aleatorias. Placa **delantera blanca**, **trasera amarilla**. Las matrículas personalizadas ocultan la edad.
:::

### Sides and parts

- **nearside** = the **left** side (next to the kerb in the UK); **offside** = the **right** side (driver's side).
- **windscreen**, **bonnet** (US hood), **boot** (US trunk), **bumper**, **wing**, **wing mirror**, **number plate**, **tyre**, **alloy wheels**, **roof rack**, **tow bar**, **tinted windows**.
- Damage: **dented**, **scratched**, **smashed**, **missing** (*missing front number plate*).

:::es
**nearside** = lado **izquierdo** (el de la acera en UK); **offside** = **derecho** (lado del conductor). **bonnet** = capó, **boot** = maletero, **windscreen** = parabrisas, **wing mirror** = retrovisor exterior.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. A witness only remembers *"a white van, part of the number was 76"*. → September 2026 – February 2027 registration: useful for **ANPR** and **PNC** partial searches.
2. Radio: *"Black BMW 3 Series saloon, Kilo X-ray seven three, unknown rest, offside front wing damaged, heading north on the M27."*

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- Saying the number plate in Spanish letters under stress → use the **phonetic alphabet**.
- ❌ *matriculation* → ✅ *registration* / *reg* / *VRM*.
- Confusing **nearside/offside** – remember: in the UK the **driver** sits on the **offside** (right).

## Summary

1. Colour – Make – Model – Type – Registration.
2. Plate: memory tag + age identifier + random letters.
3. March = year (26); September = year + 50 (76).
4. Front white, rear yellow.
5. Nearside = left; offside = right (driver's side).
