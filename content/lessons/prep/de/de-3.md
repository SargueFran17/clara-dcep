> ⚠ Las categorías y su uso concreto pueden cambiar (el censo 2021 actualizó algunas). Sigue siempre la **política de Hampshire** y la formación de la academia.

## Objectives

- Understand **self-defined ethnicity (SDE)** and the **officer-defined** "IC codes" and why both exist.
- Know **when** each is used (e.g. stop and search records vs radio descriptions).
- Ask about ethnicity **sensitively and respectfully**.

## Explanation

### Self-defined ethnicity (SDE) – what the person says

The person **chooses** their ethnic group from a list based on the **census** categories (the "**18+1**" codes):

- **White**: W1 English/Welsh/Scottish/Northern Irish/British · W2 Irish · W3 Gypsy or Irish Traveller · W9 Any other White background
- **Mixed/Multiple**: M1 White and Black Caribbean · M2 White and Black African · M3 White and Asian · M9 Any other Mixed background
- **Asian/Asian British**: A1 Indian · A2 Pakistani · A3 Bangladeshi · A4 Chinese · A9 Any other Asian background
- **Black/Black British**: B1 Caribbean · B2 African · B9 Any other Black background
- **Other**: O1 Arab · O9 Any other ethnic group
- **NS** – Not stated (the "+1")

(⚠ VERIFICAR: el censo 2021 añadió, p. ej., *Roma* en el grupo White; algunas fuerzas ya lo usan.)

SDE is used when you **record information about a person** who can tell you, e.g. in **stop and search** records (PACE Code A requires the person's self-defined ethnicity to be recorded, and the officer may also record their own perception if different – ⚠ VERIFICAR la redacción vigente).

:::es
**SDE** = etnia que **elige la propia persona** de una lista basada en el **censo** (códigos **18+1**: W1–W9, M1–M9, A1–A9, B1–B9, O1–O9 y **NS** = no consta). Se usa cuando **la persona te lo puede decir**, p. ej. en el acta de **stop and search** (Code A).
:::

### IC codes – what the officer perceives

**IC (Identity Code)** codes describe how a person **appears** to the officer. They are short and used mainly for **radio descriptions** and when the person **cannot be asked** (e.g. a suspect who ran away):

| Code | Appearance |
|---|---|
| **IC1** | White – North European |
| **IC2** | White – South European |
| **IC3** | Black |
| **IC4** | Asian (Indian subcontinent) |
| **IC5** | Chinese, Japanese or other South-East Asian |
| **IC6** | Arab or North African |
| **IC0 / IC9** | Unknown (⚠ VERIFICAR qué código usa tu fuerza) |

IC codes are about **appearance only** – they don't tell you someone's nationality, religion or identity. Use them **only when relevant** to identify someone.

:::es
**IC codes** = cómo **percibe el agente** la apariencia (IC1 blanco norte de Europa, IC2 blanco sur de Europa –¡muchos españoles!–, IC3 negro, IC4 asiático del subcontinente indio, IC5 chino/japonés/sudeste asiático, IC6 árabe/norteafricano). Sobre todo para **descripciones por radio** y cuando **no puedes preguntar**. Solo **apariencia**, nunca nacionalidad o religión.
:::

### Asking sensitively

- Explain **why** you ask: *"We ask everyone we stop to describe their ethnic background. It helps make sure stop and search is used fairly."*
- Show or read the **list**, let them **choose**; they can **decline** ("Not stated").
- Don't argue with the person's answer.

:::es
Explica **por qué** preguntas (para garantizar un uso justo del poder), deja que **elijan** de la lista y acepta si no quieren contestar. **No discutas** su respuesta.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. After a stop and search, the person says they are "Black British – Caribbean". → Record **SDE B1**, even if your perception was different (record your perception separately only if your force's form requires it).
2. A robbery suspect runs off. → Radio description may include an **IC code** because the person can't be asked.

## Common mistakes & traps

- Using IC codes in a **conversation** with the public – they are internal shorthand.
- Recording **your** perception where the **SDE** is required.
- Confusing **ethnicity** with **nationality** (e.g. "Spanish" is a nationality).

## Summary

1. SDE = the person's own choice (census-based 18+1 codes).
2. IC codes = officer's perception of appearance (IC1–IC6).
3. SDE in records like stop and search; IC codes for radio/unknown persons.
4. Explain why you ask; let them choose; "not stated" is allowed.
5. Ethnicity ≠ nationality ≠ religion.
