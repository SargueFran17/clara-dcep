## Objectives

- Name **UK clothing** items accurately (including British vs American words).
- Describe **colour, pattern, logos and condition** so a colleague can recognise the person.
- Describe **tattoos, scars and piercings** precisely by location.

## Explanation

### Clothing, top to bottom

| Area | British words |
|---|---|
| Head | **cap** (baseball cap), **beanie**, **hat**, **hood up/down**, **balaclava**, **snood**/neck gaiter |
| Upper body | **T-shirt**, **shirt**, **jumper** (US *sweater*), **hoodie / hooded top**, **jacket**, **coat**, **gilet**, **puffer jacket**, **tracksuit top** |
| Lower body | **jeans**, **trousers** (US *pants* – in the UK *pants* = underwear!), **tracksuit bottoms / joggers**, **shorts**, **skirt** |
| Feet | **trainers** (US *sneakers*), **boots**, **shoes**, **sliders**, **sandals** |
| Accessories | **rucksack/backpack**, **bum bag** (US *fanny pack*), **holdall**, **gloves**, **glasses / sunglasses**, **jewellery**, **watch** |

For each item: **colour → pattern → brand/logo → condition**. *A **navy** North Face **puffer jacket** with a **white logo on the left chest**, **dirty**.*

Patterns: *plain, striped, checked/tartan, camouflage, floral, spotted*. Colours: *navy (dark blue), maroon/burgundy, khaki, beige, grey (UK spelling)*.

:::es
- **trousers** = pantalones (¡**pants** en UK = calzoncillos/bragas!).
- **jumper** = jersey; **trainers** = zapatillas deportivas; **joggers / tracksuit bottoms** = pantalón de chándal; **hoodie** = sudadera con capucha; **rucksack** = mochila; **bum bag** = riñonera.
- Orden para cada prenda: **color → estampado → marca/logo → estado**.
:::

### Distinguishing features

- **Tattoo** – what, where, colour: *a black tattoo of a spider web on the right elbow*; *tattoo of the name "MIA" across the knuckles of the left hand*.
- **Scar** – where and shape: *a 3 cm scar above the left eyebrow*.
- **Piercing** – *nose ring in the left nostril*, *eyebrow piercing*.
- Use **left/right from the person's point of view** (their left), and say so if unsure: *on **his** left forearm*.

:::es
Para **tatuajes, cicatrices y piercings**: **qué es, dónde (izquierda/derecha de la persona), tamaño y color**. *"a scar above **his left** eyebrow"* = sobre **su** ceja izquierda.
:::

## Police scenarios

> "Female, aged 30–35, about 5 ft 4, medium build, long dark hair in a high ponytail. Wearing a **grey hoodie** with a **pink Nike logo on the front**, **black leggings** and **white trainers**. **Small gold nose ring** in the **left** nostril. Carrying a **black holdall**."

:::es
Observa: cada prenda con **color + tipo + detalle**, y la seña particular con **lado** y **tamaño**.
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- ❌ *He wore black pants* (UK listeners hear "underwear") → ✅ *black trousers*.
- ❌ *A sweat-shirt with hat* → ✅ *a hooded top / hoodie*.
- ❌ *Sport shoes* → ✅ *trainers*.
- ❌ *Grey* and *gray*: in UK reports use **grey**; *colour*, not *color*.
- Forgetting **which side** (left/right) for marks.

## Summary

1. Top to bottom: head, upper, lower, feet, accessories.
2. Each item: colour → pattern → logo/brand → condition.
3. British words: trousers, jumper, trainers, rucksack.
4. Marks: what + where (their left/right) + size.
5. British spelling: colour, grey, jewellery.
