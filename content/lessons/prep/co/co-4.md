## Objectives

- Know the purpose and legal basis of the **MG11 witness statement** (Criminal Justice Act 1967, s. 9).
- Structure a statement so it is **complete, chronological and in the witness's own words**.
- Write and self-check your **own officer statement**.

## Explanation

### What the MG11 is

The **MG11** is the standard form for a **witness statement** (for civilians and police officers). Under **s. 9 Criminal Justice Act 1967**, a written statement can be used as evidence **instead of oral evidence** if the other side doesn't object. That's why it starts with a **declaration of truth**: the witness can be **prosecuted** if they wilfully state anything they know to be false or don't believe to be true.

:::es
El **MG11** es el formulario de **declaración de testigo** (civil o policía). Gracias a la **s. 9 CJA 1967**, la declaración escrita puede usarse **en lugar del testimonio oral** si la otra parte no se opone. Por eso empieza con una **declaración de veracidad**: mentir a sabiendas puede ser delito.
:::

### Structure (a practical order)

1. **Introduction** – who the witness is (name, occupation if relevant), and the date/time/place the statement is about.
2. **Background** – why they were there.
3. **The event**, **in chronological order** – what they **saw, heard, smelt, did**. First person: *"I saw…"*.
4. **Description of the suspect** (head to toe – lessons DE1–DE2) and the **ADVOKATE** points: how long they saw them, distance, light, obstructions, whether they knew them, any reason to remember, time lapse, errors.
5. **Injuries, damage, loss** – value of property, injuries suffered.
6. **Exhibits** produced by the witness (*"I produce a CCTV disc as exhibit SK/1"*).
7. **Victim's wishes / impact** (or a separate **Victim Personal Statement**).
8. **Signature** on every page and at the end.

For **officers**: your duty, your grounds, what you saw, **significant statements** word for word, **arrest** (time, offence, necessity), **caution and reply**, **searches** and **exhibits**, conveyance to custody.

:::es
Orden práctico: **presentación** → **contexto** → **hechos en orden cronológico** en **primera persona** → **descripción del sospechoso** + **ADVOKATE** → **lesiones/daños/pérdidas** → **pruebas aportadas** → **impacto/deseos** → **firma en cada página**. En tu declaración de agente: servicio, motivos, lo que viste, **manifestaciones literales**, detención (hora, delito, necesidad), **caution y respuesta**, registros, pruebas y traslado.
:::

### Style

- **The witness's own words**, not police jargon (*"he punched me"*, not *"the suspect assaulted the IP"*).
- **Facts**, not opinions or hearsay (*"I saw…"*, not *"my neighbour told me he saw…"*, unless needed and clearly marked).
- **Past simple** for events (GR3), **past perfect** for earlier events (GR4), **direct speech** for important words.
- **Times** in 24-hour format, **approximate** if unsure (*"at about 22:15"*).

:::es
Con **las palabras del testigo**, no jerga policial; **hechos**, no opiniones ni "me dijeron"; tiempos verbales correctos; **comillas** para palabras importantes; horas en formato 24 h ("about" si no es exacto).
:::

## Writing exercise ✍️

Write an MG11 for this witness: *Mrs Ana REID, 54, cleaner, was at the bus stop on Commercial Road at about 07:40 today. A man snatched the bag of a woman next to her and ran towards the Guildhall. She saw him for about 5 seconds, 3 metres away, in daylight. White man, 20s, ginger beard, green bomber jacket, black jeans. She didn't know him. The victim was crying.*

**Self-check:**
- [ ] Introduction: who, when, where.
- [ ] First person, past simple, chronological.
- [ ] Description head to toe.
- [ ] ADVOKATE: time (5 s), distance (3 m), visibility (daylight), known (no)…
- [ ] Her own words, no jargon.
- [ ] Ends with what happened next; signature reminder.

## Common mistakes

- Writing in police language instead of the witness's words.
- Leaving out **how** the witness could see (light, distance, time).
- Mixing opinions (*"he looked like a drug addict"*).
- Forgetting the witness must **sign each page**.

## Summary

1. MG11 = witness statement; s. 9 CJA 1967.
2. Declaration of truth at the top.
3. Who/when/where → events in order → description + ADVOKATE → harm → exhibits → signature.
4. Witness's own words, facts only.
5. Officers include grounds, arrest, caution & reply, exhibits.
