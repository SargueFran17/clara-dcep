> ⚠ Muchas fuerzas usan ya **libretas electrónicas** (PNB digital) en el móvil o tablet. Las reglas de fondo son las mismas; sigue la política de Hampshire.

## Objectives

- Know **what** to record in a pocket notebook (PNB) and **when**.
- Apply the **ELBOWS** rules so your notes are reliable in court.
- Correct mistakes the proper way.

## Explanation

### Why the notebook matters

Your notes are an **official record**. They help you write statements, can be disclosed to the defence, and in court you may ask to **refresh your memory** from notes made **at the time or as soon as practicable afterwards**. If they look altered or incomplete, the defence will attack them.

:::es
La libreta es un **registro oficial**: te sirve para escribir declaraciones, puede **revelarse a la defensa** y en el juicio puedes **refrescar memoria** con notas hechas **en el momento o lo antes posible**. Si parecen alteradas, la defensa lo usará.
:::

### What to record

- **Date, time and place** of each entry; your duty start/finish.
- **Incidents** you attend: what you saw, heard, did.
- **First descriptions** of suspects given by witnesses (exact words).
- **Significant statements** and **unsolicited comments** by suspects – in **direct speech**, word for word: *"I only took one, it was my mate's idea."*
- **Replies after caution**.
- **Searches**, items **seized**, **exhibit** references.
- **Times** of arrest, arrival at custody, etc.
- Names, dates of birth and addresses of people involved.

:::es
Anota: **fecha, hora y lugar**; lo que **viste, oíste e hiciste**; **primeras descripciones** de testigos; **manifestaciones relevantes** y **comentarios espontáneos** del sospechoso **palabra por palabra** (en estilo directo); **respuestas tras la caution**; registros, objetos incautados y referencias; horas; datos personales.
:::

### ELBOWS – the rules

| Letter | Rule |
|---|---|
| **E** | no **Erasures** (no rubbing out, no correction fluid) |
| **L** | no **Leaves torn out** |
| **B** | no **Blank spaces** (draw a line through unused space) |
| **O** | no **Overwriting** |
| **W** | no **Writing between the lines** |
| **S** | no **Spare pages** (⚠ some forces: *Statements in direct speech*) |

Also: write in **black ink**, in **chronological order**, **legibly**, and **sign** entries as required.

**Mistakes:** draw a **single line** through the error so it can **still be read**, **initial** it, and write the correct version next to it.

:::es
**ELBOWS**: sin **borrones**, sin **hojas arrancadas**, sin **espacios en blanco**, sin **sobrescribir**, sin **escribir entre líneas** y sin **páginas sueltas** (⚠ en algunas fuerzas la S es *Statements in direct speech*). Tinta **negra**, orden **cronológico**, letra legible, **firma**. **Errores**: una **sola línea** encima (que se pueda leer), tus **iniciales** y la corrección al lado.
:::

## Police scenarios

**Model entry:**

> *Tue 10/11/2026 · 14:32 · Elm Road, Southsea. Attended report of shoplifting at Co-op. Witness Sarah KHAN (DOB 03/04/1990) described suspect: "white man, about 30, red cap, black puffer jacket, ran towards the park". 14:40 stopped male matching description on Albert Road. Searched under s. 1 PACE (GOWISELY given). Found 2 bottles of whisky in jacket – seized, exhibit JS/1. 14:44 arrested for theft. Cautioned. Reply: "Fair enough, I was skint." 15:05 arrived Portsmouth custody.*

:::es
Observa: fecha, hora y lugar; descripción del testigo **entre comillas**; poder legal; prueba con referencia; detención; caution y **respuesta literal**; llegada a custodia.
:::

## Writing exercise ✍️

Write a notebook entry for this: *At 09:15 today you are called to a car with a smashed window on Victoria Road. The owner, Mr LEE, says he parked at 23:00 last night. A neighbour says she heard glass breaking at about 03:00 and saw "a tall man in a grey hoodie" walking away.*

**Self-check:**
- [ ] Date, time and place at the start.
- [ ] Names and what each person said – witness words in quotes.
- [ ] Chronological order.
- [ ] No blank spaces; mistakes crossed with one line + initials.
- [ ] 24-hour clock.

## Common mistakes

- Paraphrasing what the suspect said instead of **exact words**.
- Leaving gaps "to fill in later".
- Using correction fluid.
- Writing up notes hours later without saying **when** they were made.

## Summary

1. The PNB is an official record; it can be disclosed and used to refresh memory.
2. Record times, places, descriptions, significant statements word for word.
3. ELBOWS: no erasures, leaves torn out, blank spaces, overwriting, writing between lines, spare pages.
4. Mistakes: single line, readable, initialled.
5. Black ink, chronological, signed.
