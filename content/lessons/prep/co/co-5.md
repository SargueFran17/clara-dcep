## Objectives

- Write a short **incident report / crime report narrative** that a supervisor or CPS lawyer can understand quickly.
- Use a reliable **structure** and a **checklist** to self-check.
- Put together everything from CO1–CO4, GR1–GR7 and DE1–DE5.

## Explanation

### A simple structure: the "5 Ws + H" in time order

1. **When & where** – *At 22:15 on Friday 13 November 2026 at 45 Albert Road, Southsea…*
2. **Why you were there** – call, patrol, report.
3. **What you found / saw / heard** – facts, in order.
4. **Who** – victims, witnesses, suspects (names, DOB, descriptions).
5. **What you did and why** – powers used, grounds, actions (search, arrest, first aid, scene preservation).
6. **How it ended** – outcome, exhibits, enquiries outstanding, safeguarding.

:::es
Estructura: **cuándo y dónde → por qué estabas → qué encontraste/viste/oíste → quién → qué hiciste y por qué (poderes y motivos) → cómo terminó** (resultado, pruebas, gestiones pendientes, protección).
:::

### Style rules

- **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence.
- **Facts, not opinions.** *"He was shouting and swearing"* not *"he was aggressive and horrible"*.
- **Specific:** times, distances, quantities, exact words in quotes.
- **Active voice for your actions:** *"I arrested…"*.
- **No unexplained jargon/abbreviations** for external readers.
- **British spelling**: *offence, licence (noun), defence, colour, grey, tyre*.

:::es
Frases **cortas**, **hechos** (no opiniones), **datos concretos**, voz **activa** para lo que hiciste, sin siglas raras y con **ortografía británica** (*offence, licence, defence, colour, tyre*).
:::

## Writing exercise ✍️ (the main task of this lesson)

**Scenario:** At 16:40 today you are sent to *Tesco, Commercial Road, Portsmouth* for a shoplifter detained by security. The guard, Mr Tom HALL, says he saw a woman put 3 bottles of perfume (£45 each) in her handbag and leave without paying at 16:25; he stopped her outside. CCTV is available. The woman, Ms Kerry ADAMS (DOB 12/02/1995), says: *"I was going to pay, I forgot."* You arrest her for theft at 16:52 because you need to interview her and prevent the loss of evidence. She is compliant. You seize the perfume (exhibit and reference) and request the CCTV.

Write the report (150–250 words).

**Self-check:**
- [ ] Starts with time, date and place.
- [ ] Why you attended.
- [ ] Witness account in past simple, reported speech, with key words in quotes.
- [ ] Suspect's words **in direct speech, word for word**.
- [ ] Arrest: time, offence, grounds, **necessity**.
- [ ] Caution given and **reply** recorded.
- [ ] Exhibits with references; property value.
- [ ] Outstanding enquiries (CCTV).
- [ ] 24-hour clock; British spelling; no opinions.

:::es
Escribe el informe (150–250 palabras) y repasa la **checklist**. Si quieres, pégalo en tus apuntes del diario de academia o en el resumen de un capítulo para guardarlo.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Starting with background stories instead of **when/where**.
- Forgetting the **necessity** for arrest.
- Writing what the suspect said in your words.
- Long paragraphs without times.

## Summary

1. When/where → why → what → who → actions & grounds → outcome.
2. Short, factual, specific sentences.
3. Exact words in quotes for suspects.
4. Arrest: offence + grounds + necessity + caution + reply.
5. Self-check with the checklist every time.
