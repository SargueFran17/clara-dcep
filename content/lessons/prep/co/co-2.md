> ⚠ Cada fuerza tiene sus **procedimientos de radio** (indicativos, códigos de resultado, prioridades). Esto es la **estructura general**; en la academia te darán los de Hampshire.

## Objectives

- Structure a radio message so it is **short, clear and complete**.
- Use common **radio phrases** and understand what they mean.
- Know what **not** to say on the radio (personal data, opinions, jokes).

## Explanation

### The basic structure

1. **Who you are calling** → 2. **who you are** → 3. wait for **"go ahead"** → 4. **message** (most important first) → 5. confirmation.

> *"Control, 2-1-4."* — *"2-1-4, go ahead."* — *"2-1-4, I'm on scene at 12 Elm Road. One male detained. Request a van for one prisoner."* — *"Received, van being assigned."*

Keep messages **brief**: think before you press the button. Give **location first** if there is danger.

:::es
Estructura: **a quién llamas → quién eres → esperas "go ahead" → mensaje (lo importante primero) → confirmación**. Mensajes **cortos**; piensa antes de pulsar. Si hay peligro, **la ubicación primero**.
:::

### Common phrases

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Go ahead** | I'm listening, send your message |
| **Received** / **Copied** | I have understood |
| **Say again** | Repeat (don't say "repeat") |
| **Stand by** | Wait |
| **Show me…** | Record me as… (e.g. "show me on scene") |
| **ETA** | estimated time of arrival |
| **Urgent assistance** / **Assistance** | You need help now – this has priority (force-specific wording ⚠) |
| **Cancel / Stand down** | No longer needed |

:::es
**Go ahead** = adelante; **Received** = recibido; **Say again** = repite; **Stand by** = espera; **Show me on scene** = anótame en el lugar; **Urgent assistance** = necesito ayuda urgente (cada fuerza tiene su fórmula).
:::

### Urgent messages

If you need **urgent assistance**: say your call sign, the words your force uses for urgent help, and **WHERE you are** – even if you can't say anything else. Many radios have an **emergency button**.

:::es
**Ayuda urgente**: indicativo + fórmula de urgencia + **DÓNDE estás**. Muchas radios tienen **botón de emergencia**.
:::

### What not to say

- Avoid unnecessary **personal data** on open talk groups; follow force guidance.
- No **opinions, jokes or offensive language** – radio traffic can be **recorded** and disclosed in court.

:::es
Nada de opiniones, bromas ni lenguaje ofensivo: las comunicaciones pueden **grabarse** y llegar al **juicio**.
:::

## Police scenarios

**Circulating a description:**

> *"Control, 2-1-4, circulation for a suspect for robbery, last seen two minutes ago, on foot, Commercial Road towards the Guildhall. Male, 20 to 25, 5 ft 10, slim, black hoodie hood up, grey joggers, white trainers, carrying a blue rucksack."*

:::es
Para **circular una descripción**: qué delito, **cuándo** y **dónde** se le vio por última vez, **dirección**, y la descripción de la cabeza a los pies (lecciones DE1–DE2).
:::

## Common mistakes (Spanish speakers)

- Speaking too fast when nervous – slow down, short sentences.
- Saying *"Repeat"* instead of *"Say again"*.
- Starting with the story ("So, I was walking and…") instead of the **key information**.

## Summary

1. Who you call → who you are → go ahead → message.
2. Most important information first; location first if danger.
3. Say again / received / stand by / go ahead.
4. Urgent assistance: call sign + urgent words + location.
5. Radio is recorded: professional language only.
