## Explanation

**Glossary II** covers mainly:

- **Court**: magistrates' court, Crown Court, jury, plea, verdict, acquittal, conviction, sentence, remand, adjourn, special measures, examination-in-chief, cross-examination, burden and standard of proof, summary/either-way/indictable-only, indictment, allocation, precedent…
- **Offences**: actus reus, mens rea, intent, recklessness, negligence, strict liability, omission, causation, accessory, attempt, conspiracy, assault, battery, ABH, GBH, wounding, theft, robbery, burglary, handling, fraud, criminal damage…

(The exact split is automatic: the second third of `content/glossary.json`.)

### Technique: the "explain it to a 12-year-old" test

For each term, close the screen and say a **one-sentence plain-English explanation** out loud. Then check. If you used the word itself in the explanation, try again.

> *"Recklessness – when you know there's a risk and you take it anyway, and it wasn't reasonable to do that."*

:::es
**Técnica**: tapa la pantalla y explica cada término **en una frase en inglés sencillo** en voz alta, sin usar la misma palabra. Luego comprueba.
:::

### Words that look Spanish but aren't the same

| English | Means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **sentence** | punishment | *sentencia* (judgment) – that's **judgment/verdict** |
| **conviction** | finding of guilt | *convicción* (belief) |
| **trial** | court hearing to decide guilt | *trial* ≠ *prueba* |
| **jury** | 12 citizens deciding facts | *jurado* (✓ same) |
| **assault** | threatening/using force | *asalto* (robbery) – that's **robbery** |

:::es
Falsos amigos: **sentence** = pena (no "sentencia"); **conviction** = condena (no "convicción"); **assault** = agresión (no "asalto" = *robbery*).
:::

## Common mistakes

- Learning only the Spanish translation.
- Confusing **assault** (fear of force) and **battery** (actual force).
