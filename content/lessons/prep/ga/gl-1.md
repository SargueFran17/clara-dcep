## Objectives

- Learn the first third of the **glossary**: arrest, custody and evidence words.
- Use the three-step method: **English → plain English → Spanish**.
- Practise with flashcards and the **Glosario I** test.

## Explanation

The app's glossary has **3 columns** for every word:

1. **Term** – the word as it appears in law, the manual or police paperwork.
2. **Plain English** – what it means in simple English (this is what you should be able to say).
3. **Spanish** – only to check you understood.

The goal is to **think in plain English**, not to translate. In the exam and at the academy, nobody will translate for you.

:::es
Cada palabra del glosario tiene: **término → inglés sencillo → español**. El objetivo es que **pienses en inglés sencillo**, no que traduzcas: en la academia y en el examen nadie te va a traducir. Usa el español solo para comprobar.
:::

### Glossary I covers

- **Arrest**: arrest, lawful arrest, necessity criteria, caution, reasonable force, voluntary attendance, street bail, stop and search, reasonable grounds for suspicion…
- **Custody**: custody suite, custody officer, relevant time, review, superintendent's extension, warrant of further detention, appropriate adult, charge, bail, RUI, NFA, simple/conditional caution…
- **Evidence**: exhibit, continuity, scene log, cordon, cross-contamination, hearsay, admissible, significant statement, unused material, disclosure…

(The exact split is automatic: the first third of `content/glossary.json`.)

:::es
**Glosario I**: detención, custodia y pruebas. El reparto es automático (el primer tercio del archivo del glosario). Puedes añadir palabras editando `content/glossary.json`.
:::

## Police scenarios

> *"The **custody officer** authorised detention. The **relevant time** was 15:20. His **first review** is due at 21:20. Keep the **continuity** of the knife exhibit (**JS/1**) and update the **scene log**."*

:::es
Si entiendes esta frase sin traducir, vas bien: custodia, cómputo, revisión, cadena de custodia del cuchillo (prueba JS/1) y registro de la escena.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **caution** (arrest words) ≠ **caution** (out-of-court warning).
- **evidence** is uncountable: *a piece of evidence*, not *an evidence*.
- **charge** (formal accusation) ≠ *cargo* (job): *he was **charged with** burglary*.

## Summary

1. Term → plain English → Spanish.
2. Think in plain English.
3. Glossary I = arrest, custody, evidence.
4. Flashcards daily.
5. Test: "Test Glosario I".
