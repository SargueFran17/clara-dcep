## Objectives

- Learn the **40 essential acronyms** you will hear in your first weeks.
- Know both the **full form** and **what it means in plain English**.
- Practise them with **flashcards** and the **test** until they are automatic.

## Explanation

British policing uses **a lot** of acronyms. In your first weeks you will hear sentences like:

> *"The OIC wants the MG11s before the RUI date, and check PNC and PND before the MARAC."*

This lesson groups the 40 most important ones. The full list (with Spanish) is in **Más → Glosario y acrónimos → Acrónimos**.

**Organisations**: CID · CPS · IOPC · HMICFRS · PCC · NPCC · CoP · APP · NCA (Acrónimos II)
**Laws**: PACE · CPIA · RIPA · HRA · ECHR
**Decision-making and risk**: NDM · THRIVE · DASH · MARAC · MAPPA
**Interviews and witnesses**: PEACE · ABE · ADVOKATE
**Stop and search**: GOWISELY
**Outcomes**: RUI · NFA
**Systems**: PNC · PND · ANPR · BWV
**Roles**: OIC · SIO · PCSO · SC
**Training**: PIP · NIE · DCEP
**Paperwork**: MG11 · MG5 · ELBOWS
**Other**: CHIS · RTC

:::es
La lista completa con traducción está en **Más → Glosario y acrónimos**. Aquí están agrupados por temas para memorizarlos mejor: organizaciones, leyes, decisión y riesgo, entrevistas, registros, resultados, sistemas, roles, formación, papeleo.
:::

### How to memorise acronyms

1. **Say the full form out loud** three times, then the plain meaning.
2. Make a **sentence** with it: *"The **OIC** sent the file to the **CPS**."*
3. Group them by **topic** (above), not alphabetically.
4. Do the **flashcards every day** – the app spaces them for you.

:::es
Truco: **dilo en voz alta** (forma completa + significado), haz una **frase** con él, apréndelos **por temas** y repasa las **flashcards** cada día.
:::

## Police scenarios

- *"Do a **THRIVE** assessment on that missing person call."* → Threat, Harm, Risk, Investigation, Vulnerability, Engagement.
- *"Remember **GOWISELY** before you search him."* → Grounds, Object, Warrant card, Identity, Station, Entitlement to a copy, Legal power, You are detained.

## Common mistakes

- Pronouncing letter by letter vs as a word: **PACE** ("pace"), **PEACE** ("peace"), **MARAC** ("mar-ack"), **MAPPA** ("map-a"), but **C-P-S**, **P-N-C**, **O-I-C**.
- Confusing **PNC** (computer: people, vehicles, convictions) and **PND** (database: intelligence).

## Summary

1. 40 essential acronyms grouped by topic.
2. Learn full form + plain meaning.
3. Use them in sentences.
4. Daily flashcards.
5. Test: "Test Acrónimos I" in the glossary screen.
