## Objectives

- Learn the last third of the glossary: **investigation, thresholds, people and procedure**.
- Connect each term with the lesson where you studied it (VO6, VO7, ET2, ET3, SJ3…).
- Score at least 80 % in **Test Glosario III**.

## Explanation

**Glossary III** covers mainly:

- **Investigation**: OIC, SIO, lines of enquiry, reasonable lines of enquiry, golden hour, intelligence, first account, interview, prepared statement, adverse inference, special warning, covert/overt, surveillance…
- **Thresholds**: suspect, believe, know, reasonable grounds, necessary, proportionate, realistic prospect of conviction, threshold test, full code test, may/must/should…
- **People**: complainant, informant, vulnerable adult, appropriate adult, protected characteristic, self-defined ethnicity, IC code…
- **Procedure**: Codes of Practice, APP, Code of Ethics, Standards of Professional Behaviour, misconduct, duty to intervene, policing by consent, handover, briefing, graded response…

(The exact split is automatic: the last third of `content/glossary.json`.)

### Technique: link words to lessons

| Term | Lesson |
|---|---|
| reasonable grounds to suspect / believe | VO6 |
| realistic prospect of conviction | SJ3 |
| Standards of Professional Behaviour | ET3 |
| special measures, vulnerable witness | VO7 |
| policing by consent | SE1 |

:::es
Relaciona cada término con su lección: así lo recuerdas en contexto (VO6 umbrales, SJ3 CPS, ET3 normas de conducta, VO7 víctimas y testigos, SE1 policía por consentimiento).
:::

## Common mistakes

- Mixing **covert** (secret) and **overt** (open).
- Mixing **complainant** (person who reports) and **defendant**.

## Summary

1. Glossary III = investigation, thresholds, people, procedure.

3. Explain in one plain-English sentence.
4. Flashcards daily.
