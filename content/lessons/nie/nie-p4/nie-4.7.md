## Objectives

- Know the definitions of **prostitute** and **gain** (SOA 2003, s. 54).
- Know the offences of **causing/inciting** and **controlling** prostitution for gain (ss. 52–53), **paying for sexual services of a prostitute subjected to force** (s. 53A), **soliciting** (s. 51A) and **loitering/soliciting** (Street Offences Act 1959, s. 1).
- Know **brothel** offences and the offences of **sexual exploitation of children** (ss. 48–50).

## Explanation

### 1. Definitions (s. 54)

- **Prostitute**: a person (A) who, **on at least one occasion** and **whether or not compelled to do so**, **offers or provides sexual services** to another person **in return for payment or a promise of payment** to A or a third person.
- **Payment**: any **financial advantage**, including discharge of an obligation to pay or the provision of goods or services (incl. sexual services) **gratuitously or at a discount**.
- **Gain**: any **financial advantage** (incl. discharge of an obligation, goods or services at a discount) or the **goodwill** of any person which **appears likely** to bring financial advantage.

:::es
**Prostituta/o (s. 54)**: quien, **al menos una vez** y **aunque sea obligado**, **ofrece o presta servicios sexuales** a cambio de **pago o promesa de pago** (a sí o a un tercero). **Ganancia**: cualquier **ventaja económica** o el **favor** de alguien que probablemente la traiga.
:::

### 2. Exploitation offences

| Section | Offence | Max |
|---|---|---|
| s. 52 | **Causing or inciting** prostitution **for gain** (A's or a third person's) – anywhere in the world | 7 yrs |
| s. 53 | **Controlling** any of the activities of another person relating to that person's prostitution, **for gain** | 7 yrs |
| s. 53A | **Paying for sexual services** of a prostitute **subjected to force, threats, deception or other exploitative conduct** by a third party (for A's or another's gain) | summary, fine |

- s. 53A is **strict liability**: it doesn't matter whether A knew or ought to have known about the exploitation (⚠).
- "**Controlling**" needn't involve force – instructing, directing, setting prices (*R v Massey* 2007).

:::es
**s. 52** causar o **incitar** a la prostitución **con ánimo de lucro**; **s. 53** **controlar** la actividad de otra persona con ánimo de lucro (no hace falta violencia – *Massey*); máx. **7 años**. **s. 53A** **pagar** por servicios sexuales de alguien sometido a **fuerza, amenazas o engaño** por un tercero: **responsabilidad objetiva**, sumario, multa (⚠).
:::

### 3. Street offences

- **Loitering or soliciting** (Street Offences Act 1959, s. 1): a person **aged 18 or over** **persistently** loiters or solicits in a **street or public place** for the purpose of prostitution. **Persistently** = **two or more times in any period of three months** (⚠). Summary. Court can make an order to attend meetings (rehabilitation) instead of a fine.
- **Soliciting** (SOA 2003, s. 51A): a person in a **street or public place** **solicits another** for the purpose of **obtaining their sexual services as a prostitute** (the "**kerb-crawling**" buyer – in or near a vehicle or not). **No persistence** needed. Summary.
- **Street** includes bridges, roads, lanes, footways, subways, squares, courts, alleys and passages… and doorways/entrances of premises abutting a street (⚠).

:::es
**Merodear o solicitar (SOA 1959 s. 1)**: persona **18+** que **persistentemente** (≥2 veces en 3 meses ⚠) merodea o solicita en la **calle o lugar público** para prostituirse; sumario. **Solicitar servicios (SOA 2003 s. 51A)**: el **cliente** que solicita en la calle o lugar público ("**kerb-crawling**"); **no** hace falta persistencia; sumario.
:::

### 4. Brothels and adverts

- **Keeping a brothel used for prostitution** (SOA 1956, s. 33A): keeping, managing, acting/assisting in management – max **7 years**. A **brothel** is premises used by **more than one** person for prostitution (one person working alone is not a brothel).
- Placing **adverts** for prostitution in or near **public telephone boxes** (Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001, s. 46).
- **Closure orders** for premises used for prostitution/child sexual exploitation (⚠ VERIFICAR legislación vigente).

### 5. Children – sexual exploitation (ss. 48–50)

**Causing or inciting** (s. 48), **controlling** (s. 49), **arranging or facilitating** (s. 50) the **sexual exploitation of a child** (under **18**); A doesn't reasonably believe B is 18+ (or B under 13). **Sexual exploitation** includes recording indecent images. Max **14 years**. **Paying** for the sexual services of a child (s. 47) – penalties depend on age (life if under 13 and penetration ⚠). Children are **victims**, not offenders.

:::es
**Burdel (SOA 1956 s. 33A)**: local usado por **más de una** persona para la prostitución; máx. **7 años**. Anuncios en **cabinas telefónicas** (CJPA 2001 s. 46). **Menores (<18)**: **s. 47** pagar por servicios sexuales de un menor; **ss. 48–50** causar/incitar, controlar, organizar la **explotación sexual** infantil (máx. **14 años**). Los menores son **víctimas**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The phone.** A man takes 50% of a woman's earnings and decides her clients and prices. → **s. 53** – controlling for gain.
2. **The car.** A driver slows down and asks a woman on the pavement for sex for money. → **s. 51A** (no persistence needed).
3. **The trafficked woman.** A pays for sex with a woman controlled by a gang; he didn't know. → **s. 53A** – strict liability (⚠).
4. **Two flats.** Two women work together from one flat. → Potentially a **brothel**; consider who is keeping/managing it.
5. **The 16-year-old.** A 16-year-old is being sold for sex. → **ss. 48–50** – sexual exploitation of a child; safeguarding.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- Prostitute: **at least one occasion**, **whether or not compelled**.
- ss. 52–53 require **gain**; s. 53A doesn't require knowledge.
- SOA 1959 s. 1: aged **18+** and **persistently**; s. 51A: **no persistence**.
- Brothel = **more than one** person; child = **under 18** for exploitation offences.

## Summary

1. s. 54: prostitute (one occasion, compelled or not); payment; gain.
2. s. 52 causing/inciting, s. 53 controlling – for gain, 7 yrs.
3. s. 53A paying for services of an exploited prostitute – strict liability.
4. SOA 1959 s. 1 loitering/soliciting (18+, persistent); s. 51A soliciting (buyer).
5. Brothel (SOA 1956 s. 33A); child sexual exploitation ss. 47–50.
