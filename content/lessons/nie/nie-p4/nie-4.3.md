## Objectives

- Know **assault by penetration** (s. 2), **sexual assault** (s. 3) and **causing a person to engage in sexual activity without consent** (s. 4) of the Sexual Offences Act 2003.
- Know the equivalent offences against **children under 13** (ss. 6–8).
- Apply the definitions of **"sexual"** (s. 78) and **"touching"** (s. 79(8)).

## Explanation

### 1. Assault by penetration (s. 2)

A **intentionally penetrates** the **vagina or anus** of B with **a part of his/her body or anything else**; the penetration is **sexual**; B **does not consent**; A **does not reasonably believe** B consents. Maximum **life**.

- Can be committed by **anyone** (man or woman), with fingers, tongue, objects…
- The **mouth is not** included.

:::es
**Agresión por penetración (s. 2)**: penetrar la **vagina o el ano** con **una parte del cuerpo o cualquier objeto**, de forma **sexual**, sin consentimiento ni creencia razonable. **Cadena perpetua**. Puede cometerla **cualquier persona**. La **boca no** está incluida.
:::

### 2. Sexual assault (s. 3)

A **intentionally touches** B; the touching is **sexual**; B **does not consent**; A **does not reasonably believe** B consents. **Either-way**; maximum **10 years** on indictment.

**Touching** (s. 79(8)) includes touching **with any part of the body**, **with anything else**, **through anything** (e.g. clothing), and in particular **touching amounting to penetration**. *R v H* (2005): touching the victim's **clothing** is touching.

:::es
**Agresión sexual (s. 3)**: **tocar intencionadamente** a B de forma **sexual**, sin consentimiento ni creencia razonable. Delito *either-way*, máximo **10 años**. **Tocar** incluye con cualquier parte del cuerpo, con **cualquier objeto**, **a través de la ropa** (*R v H*: tocar la ropa ya es tocar).
:::

### 3. "Sexual" (s. 78)

Penetration, touching or any other activity is **sexual** if a reasonable person would consider that:
- (a) **whatever its circumstances or any person's purpose**, it is **because of its nature sexual**; or
- (b) **because of its nature it may be sexual** and **because of its circumstances or the purpose of any person** in relation to it, it **is** sexual.

Examples: (a) touching genitals; (b) a medical examination that becomes sexual because of the doctor's purpose. Something that **by its nature could not be sexual** is not caught by (b), whatever A's secret purpose (e.g. removing a shoe for a hidden fetish – compare *R v George* 1956 under the old law ⚠).

:::es
**"Sexual" (s. 78)**: (a) **sexual por naturaleza**, sea cual sea el contexto o el propósito; o (b) **puede ser sexual por naturaleza** y lo **es** por las **circunstancias o el propósito** de alguien (p. ej. un examen médico con fin sexual). Si por naturaleza **no puede** ser sexual, no entra en (b).
:::

### 4. Causing a person to engage in sexual activity without consent (s. 4)

A **intentionally causes** B to **engage in an activity**; the activity is **sexual**; B does not consent; no reasonable belief. Examples: forcing B to masturbate A or a third person, or to touch themselves.

- Maximum **life** if the activity involved **penetration** (of B's anus or vagina, of B's mouth with a penis, of someone else by B's penis or body part…); otherwise **10 years**.
- A woman who forces a man to penetrate her commits s. 4 (not rape).

:::es
**Causar actividad sexual sin consentimiento (s. 4)**: hacer que B realice una actividad **sexual** sin consentir (masturbar a alguien, tocarse…). **Perpetua** si hay **penetración**, si no **10 años**. Una mujer que obliga a un hombre a penetrarla comete **s. 4** (no violación).
:::

### 5. Children under 13 (ss. 6–8)

| Section | Offence | Consent |
|---|---|---|
| s. 5 | Rape of child under 13 | irrelevant |
| s. 6 | Assault of child under 13 by penetration | irrelevant |
| s. 7 | Sexual assault of child under 13 | irrelevant |
| s. 8 | Causing or inciting a child under 13 to engage in sexual activity | irrelevant |

**Strict liability as to age** – no defence of reasonable belief that the child was 13 or over. **Inciting** in s. 8 is complete even if the activity never takes place.

:::es
**Menores de 13 (ss. 5–8)**: violación, penetración, agresión sexual y causar/**incitar** actividad sexual. El **consentimiento es irrelevante** y la **edad es de responsabilidad objetiva**. La **incitación** se consuma aunque la actividad no ocurra.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **Nightclub.** A man grabs a woman's bottom over her jeans on the dance floor. → **s. 3**: touching through clothing; sexual by nature.
2. **Fingers.** A woman digitally penetrates another woman's vagina without consent. → **s. 2** (anyone can commit it).
3. **Forced act.** A threatens B unless B masturbates A's friend. → **s. 4** (no penetration → max 10 years).
4. **Medical exam.** A doctor performs an unnecessary breast examination for sexual gratification. → **s. 78(b)** – nature may be sexual; purpose makes it sexual → **s. 3**.
5. **Online incitement.** A messages a 12-year-old asking her to touch herself sexually; she doesn't. → **s. 8 – inciting**: complete.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- s. 2 covers **vagina or anus** only – not mouth.
- s. 2 can be with **anything**; rape (s. 1) only with the **penis**.
- s. 4: life only if **penetration** is involved.
- Touching **through clothing** counts.
- Under-13 offences: consent and belief as to age **irrelevant**.

## Summary

1. s. 2 assault by penetration: vagina/anus, any body part or object, sexual – life.
2. s. 3 sexual assault: sexual touching (incl. through clothes) – 10 years.
3. s. 4 causing sexual activity without consent: life if penetration, else 10 years.
4. s. 78: sexual by nature, or may be sexual + circumstances/purpose.
5. ss. 5–8: children under 13 – consent irrelevant, strict liability on age.
