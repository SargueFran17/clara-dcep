> ⚠ **Comprueba con tu edición 2027.** La Online Safety Act 2023 añadió nuevos delitos (ss. 66A–66D SOA 2003) desde 2024; verifica la redacción y las penas.

## Objectives

- Know **exposure** (s. 66) and **sending photographs or film of genitals** ("**cyber-flashing**", s. 66A).
- Know **voyeurism** (s. 67), **upskirting/downblousing** (s. 67A) and what a **private act** is (s. 68).
- Know **sharing or threatening to share intimate images** (s. 66B), and the offences of **sexual activity in a public lavatory**, **intercourse with an animal** and **sexual penetration of a corpse** (ss. 69–71).

## Explanation

### 1. Exposure (s. 66)

A **intentionally exposes his or her genitals**, and **intends that someone will see them and be caused alarm or distress**. Either-way; max **2 years**. Nobody needs to actually see them or be distressed – it's the **intention** that matters.

### 2. Cyber-flashing (s. 66A – Online Safety Act 2023)

A **intentionally sends or gives** a **photograph or film of any person's genitals** to B, and **either** intends B will **see** the genitals and be caused **alarm, distress or humiliation**, **or** sends it for the purpose of obtaining **sexual gratification** and is **reckless** as to whether B will be caused alarm, distress or humiliation. Max **2 years** (⚠).

:::es
**Exhibicionismo (s. 66)**: exponer intencionadamente los **genitales** con **intención** de que alguien los vea y sienta **alarma o angustia**; no hace falta que nadie los vea. Máx. **2 años**. **Cyber-flashing (s. 66A)**: **enviar una foto/vídeo de genitales** con intención de causar alarma, angustia o **humillación**, o para **gratificación sexual** siendo **imprudente** sobre ese efecto (⚠).
:::

### 3. Voyeurism (s. 67) and private acts (s. 68)

For the purpose of obtaining **sexual gratification**, A:
- (1) **observes** another person doing a **private act**, knowing that B does **not consent** to being observed for A's sexual gratification;
- (2) **operates equipment** to enable **another person** to observe B doing a private act (for that person's gratification);
- (3) **records** B doing a private act, intending that A or another will **look at the image** for sexual gratification;
- (4) **installs equipment** or **constructs/adapts a structure** (e.g. drills a peephole) with intent to enable A or another to commit (1).

**Private act** (s. 68): B is in a place which, in the circumstances, would **reasonably be expected to provide privacy**, and B's **genitals, buttocks or breasts are exposed or covered only with underwear**, **or** B is **using a lavatory**, **or** B is doing a **sexual act** not of a kind ordinarily done in public.

Max **2 years**.

:::es
**Voyeurismo (s. 67)**, para **gratificación sexual**: **observar** a alguien en un **acto privado** sin su consentimiento; **manejar un equipo** para que otro observe; **grabar**; o **instalar** equipos/agujeros. **Acto privado (s. 68)**: lugar donde se espera **privacidad** y **genitales, nalgas o pecho** expuestos o solo en **ropa interior**, **usando el baño**, o un **acto sexual** no habitual en público. Máx. **2 años**.
:::

### 4. Upskirting (s. 67A – Voyeurism (Offences) Act 2019)

A **operates equipment** or **records an image** **beneath the clothing** of B, to enable A or another to observe/look at B's **genitals or buttocks** (whether **exposed or covered with underwear**) or the underwear covering them, in circumstances where they would not otherwise be visible, **without B's consent** (and not reasonably believing B consents), for the purpose of **sexual gratification** **or** of **humiliating, alarming or distressing** B. Max **2 years**. Since 2024 also **breasts** ("downblousing") via the Online Safety Act ⚠.

### 5. Intimate image abuse (s. 66B – Online Safety Act 2023)

From **31 January 2024** (replacing CJCA 2015, s. 33 "revenge porn" ⚠):
- **Base offence**: intentionally **sharing** a photograph or film which **shows, or appears to show**, B in an **intimate state**, without B's **consent** and without reasonable belief in consent – **no specific intent required**; summary.
- **Aggravated**: with intent to cause **alarm, distress or humiliation**, or for **sexual gratification** – max **2 years**.
- **Threatening to share** an intimate image, intending B (or another) to fear it will be shared, or reckless – max **2 years**.
- Includes **deepfakes** ("appears to show").

:::es
**Upskirting (s. 67A)**: grabar u observar **debajo de la ropa** genitales o nalgas (desnudos o en ropa interior) sin consentimiento, para **gratificación sexual** o para **humillar, alarmar o angustiar**. Máx. **2 años** (desde 2024 también pecho ⚠). **Imágenes íntimas (s. 66B, desde 31/01/2024)**: **compartir** sin consentimiento una imagen íntima (incluye **deepfakes**) – delito base **sin intención** específica; agravado con intención de humillar o gratificación sexual; también **amenazar** con compartirla. Máx. **2 años** (⚠).
:::

### 6. Other offences (ss. 69–71)

- **Intercourse with an animal** (s. 69) – penile penetration of a living animal's vagina/anus, or causing/allowing one's own vagina/anus to be penetrated by an animal's penis; A **knows or is reckless**. Max **2 years**.
- **Sexual penetration of a corpse** (s. 70) – intentional sexual penetration with a body part or anything else, knowing/reckless as to it being a corpse. Max **2 years**.
- **Sexual activity in a public lavatory** (s. 71) – intentionally engages in sexual activity in a lavatory **to which the public or a section of the public has access**. **Summary**; max **6 months** ⚠.

### 7. Notification requirements ("sex offenders' register")

Offenders convicted of listed sexual offences must **notify** police of their details (name, address, etc.) within **3 days** and on changes (SOA 2003, Part 2 ⚠). Breach is an offence. Also **Sexual Harm Prevention Orders (SHPO)** and **Sexual Risk Orders (SRO)** (Sentencing Act 2020 / SOA 2003 ⚠).

:::es
**Otros**: **s. 69** relaciones con un **animal**; **s. 70** penetración sexual de un **cadáver**; **s. 71** actividad sexual en un **baño público** (sumario). **Registro de delincuentes sexuales**: comunicar sus datos a la policía en **3 días** y cada cambio (⚠); **SHPO** y **SRO**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The park.** A man exposes himself to joggers, hoping to shock them. → **s. 66** – intent to cause alarm/distress.
2. **AirDrop.** A man sends an unsolicited picture of his penis to strangers on a train for a laugh. → **s. 66A** – intent to cause alarm/distress/humiliation.
3. **The changing room.** A gym employee hides a camera in a changing room. → **s. 67(4)** installing; **s. 67(3)** if recording.
4. **The escalator.** A man films up a woman's skirt on an escalator. → **s. 67A** – upskirting.
5. **The ex.** After a breakup, A posts intimate photos of B online. → **s. 66B** (sharing intimate image; aggravated if to humiliate).

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- s. 66: **intent** that someone sees and is alarmed/distressed – no one need see.
- s. 67 requires **sexual gratification**; s. 67A: gratification **or** humiliation/alarm/distress.
- Private act: **reasonable expectation of privacy** + exposure/underwear, toilet, or sexual act.
- s. 66B base offence needs **no** intent to humiliate; deepfakes included.
- s. 71: lavatory to which **the public** has access; summary.

## Summary

1. s. 66 exposure; s. 66A cyber-flashing.
2. s. 67 voyeurism (observe, operate, record, install) for sexual gratification; s. 68 private act.
3. s. 67A upskirting (gratification or humiliation/alarm/distress).
4. s. 66B sharing/threatening to share intimate images (from 2024).
5. ss. 69–71 animal, corpse, public lavatory; notification requirements.
