## Objectives

- Know the **preparatory offences** of the SOA 2003: **administering a substance with intent** (s. 61), **committing an offence with intent** to commit a sexual offence (s. 62) and **trespass with intent** (s. 63).
- Identify the **intent** required and **when** it must exist.

## Explanation

### 1. Administering a substance with intent (s. 61)

A **intentionally administers** a substance to B, **or causes** a substance to be **taken by** B, **knowing that B does not consent**, and **with the intention of stupefying or overpowering B** so as to enable **any person** to engage in a **sexual activity** involving B. Max **10 years**.

- The sexual activity **need not take place**.
- "Spiking" a drink with alcohol or drugs is the classic example. The person intending the sexual activity can be **someone else**.

:::es
**Administrar una sustancia con intención (s. 61)**: administrar o hacer tomar a B una sustancia, **sabiendo que no consiente**, con **intención de aturdirla o dominarla** para que **cualquier persona** tenga actividad sexual con ella. La actividad sexual **no tiene que ocurrir**. Ej.: **echar droga/alcohol en la bebida**. Máx. **10 años**.
:::

### 2. Committing an offence with intent to commit a sexual offence (s. 62)

A commits **any offence** with the **intention of committing a relevant sexual offence**. Examples: **kidnapping** or **false imprisonment** to rape; assault to make B submit; burglary… Max **life** if the offence committed is **kidnapping or false imprisonment**; otherwise **10 years**.

### 3. Trespass with intent to commit a sexual offence (s. 63)

A is a **trespasser** on **any premises**, **knows or is reckless** as to being a trespasser, and **intends to commit a relevant sexual offence** on the premises. Max **10 years**.

- **Premises** includes a **structure or part of a structure** (building, room, **vehicle, vessel, tent** or other temporary/movable structure ⚠).
- The intention must exist **while A is a trespasser** – it can be formed **after** entering (unlike burglary, which needs intent at entry for s. 9(1)(a)).
- No need to commit the sexual offence.

:::es
**Cometer un delito con intención sexual (s. 62)**: **cualquier delito** (secuestro, detención ilegal, agresión…) con intención de cometer un delito sexual; **perpetua** si es secuestro o detención ilegal, si no **10 años**. **Allanamiento con intención (s. 63)**: estar como **intruso** en **cualquier local** (incluye **vehículos, barcos, tiendas de campaña**), sabiéndolo o con imprudencia, con **intención** de cometer allí un delito sexual. La intención puede formarse **después de entrar**. Máx. **10 años**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The spiked drink.** A man puts ketamine in a woman's drink so his friend can have sex with her; she notices and leaves. → **s. 61** – complete; the activity needn't happen; the intended actor can be **another person**.
2. **The van.** A drags a woman into his van intending to rape her; she escapes. → **s. 62** (kidnapping with intent – life) and attempted rape.
3. **The tent.** At a festival, A unzips a stranger's tent intending to sexually assault her. → **s. 63** (tent = premises ⚠).
4. **Change of mind inside.** A enters a house as a trespasser to steal, then decides to sexually assault the occupant. → **s. 63** – intent formed while trespassing is enough.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- s. 61: A must **know B does not consent** to taking the substance.
- s. 61: activity may be by **any person**, not just A.
- s. 62: **any** offence; life only for **kidnapping/false imprisonment**.
- s. 63: intent while a trespasser – **not only at entry**; premises include **vehicles and tents**.

## Summary

1. s. 61 administering a substance with intent to stupefy/overpower for sexual activity – 10 yrs.
2. s. 62 any offence with intent to commit a sexual offence – life if kidnapping/false imprisonment.
3. s. 63 trespass (knowing/reckless) with intent to commit a sexual offence on the premises – 10 yrs.
