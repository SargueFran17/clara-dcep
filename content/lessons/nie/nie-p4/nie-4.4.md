## Objectives

- Know the **child sex offences** (SOA 2003, ss. 9–15A): sexual activity with a child, causing/inciting, in the presence of a child, causing a child to watch, arranging/facilitating, **grooming** and **sexual communication**.
- Know **abuse of a position of trust** (ss. 16–24) and **familial** offences (ss. 25–26).
- Know the **indecent images of children** offences (PCA 1978, s. 1; CJA 1988, s. 160) and their defences.

## Explanation

### 1. Child sex offences – the age rules

For ss. 9–12 and 15: A is **18 or over**; B is **under 16**, and **either** A does **not reasonably believe** B is 16 or over, **or** B is **under 13** (then belief is irrelevant).

| Section | Offence | Max |
|---|---|---|
| s. 9 | **Sexual activity with a child** (intentional sexual touching) | 14 yrs |
| s. 10 | **Causing or inciting** a child to engage in sexual activity | 14 yrs |
| s. 11 | Engaging in sexual activity **in the presence of a child** (for sexual gratification, knowing/believing/intending the child is aware) | 10 yrs |
| s. 12 | **Causing a child to watch** a sexual act (incl. images), for sexual gratification | 10 yrs |
| s. 13 | ss. 9–12 committed by a person **under 18** | 5 yrs |
| s. 14 | **Arranging or facilitating** a child sex offence (anywhere in the world) | 14 yrs |

(⚠ VERIFICAR penas.)

:::es
**Delitos sexuales contra menores (ss. 9–12)**: A **18+**, B **menor de 16** y A no cree razonablemente que tenga 16+ (o B **menor de 13**: la creencia da igual). s. 9 actividad sexual; s. 10 causar o **incitar**; s. 11 actividad sexual **en presencia** de un menor; s. 12 hacer que un menor **mire** (incluye imágenes); s. 13 si el autor es **menor de 18** (pena menor); s. 14 **organizar o facilitar**.
:::

### 2. Grooming (s. 15) and sexual communication (s. 15A)

**Meeting a child following sexual grooming (s. 15)**: A (18+) has **met or communicated** with B **on at least one occasion** (reduced from two in 2015), and then:
- **intentionally meets** B, **travels** with the intention of meeting B, **arranges** to meet B, or **B travels** with the intention of meeting A;
- A **intends** to do anything to/in respect of B during or after the meeting that would be a **relevant offence**;
- B is **under 16** and A doesn't reasonably believe B is 16+.

The intended offence **need not occur**. Police decoys: if B is an **adult posing as a child**, the full offence isn't committed (no child) – charge an **attempt** (⚠).

**Sexual communication with a child (s. 15A)**: A (18+), **for the purpose of obtaining sexual gratification**, intentionally **communicates** with B (**under 16**, no reasonable belief 16+), and the communication is **sexual** or **intended to encourage B to make a sexual communication**. Max **2 years**.

:::es
**Grooming (s. 15)**: A (18+) se ha comunicado con B **al menos una vez**, y luego **queda, viaja, organiza** el encuentro (o **B viaja**), con **intención** de cometer un delito sexual. El delito **no tiene que ocurrir**. Si "B" es un adulto que se hace pasar por menor → **tentativa** (⚠). **Comunicación sexual con menor (s. 15A)**: para **gratificación sexual**, comunicación **sexual** o para animar a B a hacerla. Máx. **2 años**.
:::

### 3. Abuse of a position of trust (ss. 16–24)

A (**18+**) in a **position of trust** in relation to B (**under 18**) engages in sexual activity with B (s. 16), causes/incites (s. 17), in presence (s. 18), causes to watch (s. 19). Positions (s. 21) include those who **look after** under-18s in **schools/colleges**, **care homes**, **hospitals**, **youth offending**, and since **2022** **sports coaches** and **religious leaders** (s. 22A ⚠). Defence if A reasonably believed B was **18+**, or they were **lawfully married/civil partners** or in a sexual relationship **before** the position of trust began (ss. 23–24). Max **5 years**.

### 4. Familial child sex offences (ss. 25–26)

Sexual activity with, or inciting, a **child family member** **under 18** (s. 27 defines family relationships: parent, grandparent, sibling, aunt/uncle, step-parent, foster parent, and people living in the same household who have a caring role…). Max **14 years** (if A 18+).

:::es
**Abuso de posición de confianza (ss. 16–19)**: A **18+** en posición de confianza (colegio, centro de menores, hospital, y desde **2022** **entrenadores deportivos** y **líderes religiosos** ⚠) con B **menor de 18**. Defensas: creencia razonable de que tenía 18+, matrimonio o relación previa. Máx. **5 años**. **Familiares (ss. 25–26)**: B **menor de 18** de la familia (s. 27 lista quién cuenta). Máx. **14 años**.
:::

### 5. Indecent images of children

- **Protection of Children Act 1978, s. 1** – to **take, make** (includes **downloading/saving/opening an attachment** – *R v Bowden* 2000), **distribute or show**, **possess with a view to distribution or showing**, or **publish an advertisement** for indecent photographs/**pseudo-photographs** of a **child** (**under 18**). Max **10 years**.
- **CJA 1988, s. 160** – **possession** of an indecent photograph/pseudo-photograph of a child. Max **5 years**.
- **Defences**: **legitimate reason** (e.g. police/forensic work); **had not seen** it and **did not know or have cause to suspect** it was indecent; it was **sent unsolicited** and not kept for an unreasonable time (s. 160). **Marriage/partner** defence where the child was **16 or 17** and they were married/partners/living together as partners in an enduring relationship.
- Images are graded **Category A, B, C** (sentencing).
- **Prohibited images** (non-photographic, e.g. cartoons) – Coroners and Justice Act 2009, s. 62.

:::es
**Imágenes indecentes de menores (<18)**: **PCA 1978 s. 1** – **tomar, hacer** (incluye **descargar**, *Bowden*), **distribuir, mostrar, poseer para distribuir, anunciar**; máx. **10 años**. **CJA 1988 s. 160** – **posesión**; máx. **5 años**. **Defensas**: **motivo legítimo**; **no la vio** ni tenía motivos para sospechar; **recibida sin pedirla** y no guardada mucho tiempo; **matrimonio/pareja** si el menor tenía 16–17. Categorías **A, B, C**. Imágenes no fotográficas (dibujos): CJA 2009 s. 62.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The chat.** A 30-year-old chats online with a girl he thinks is 14, sends sexual messages and asks for a photo. → **s. 15A** (sexual communication); asking for images → also **s. 10** (inciting) ⚠.
2. **The train.** After one chat, he travels to meet her intending sexual activity. → **s. 15** – travelling with intent; no offence needs to occur.
3. **The decoy.** "She" is a police officer/adult volunteer. → **Attempted** s. 15 (⚠ check decoy issues).
4. **The coach.** A 25-year-old football coach has sex with a 17-year-old player. → Since 2022, **position of trust** (sports coach) – s. 16 ⚠.
5. **The downloads.** Images found in a deleted folder on a suspect's laptop. → **Making** (downloaded) and/or **possession**; consider whether he could still access them.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- ss. 9–12: A must be **18+**; if A is under 18 → **s. 13**.
- s. 15: **one** previous communication is enough; the meeting need not take place – **travelling** is enough.
- s. 15A: purpose of **sexual gratification**; B under **16**.
- Position of trust and familial offences: B **under 18**, not 16.
- Indecent images: child = **under 18**; **downloading = making**.

## Summary

1. ss. 9–12: A 18+, B under 16 (no reasonable belief) or under 13; s. 13 if A under 18; s. 14 arranging/facilitating.
2. s. 15 grooming: one prior communication + meeting/travel/arrangement + intent; s. 15A sexual communication.
3. ss. 16–19 position of trust (B under 18; sports coaches/religious leaders since 2022 ⚠); ss. 25–26 familial (B under 18).
4. PCA 1978 s. 1 (take, make, distribute…) – 10 yrs; CJA 1988 s. 160 possession – 5 yrs; defences.
