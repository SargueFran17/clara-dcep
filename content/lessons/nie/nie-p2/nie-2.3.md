## Objectives

- Know the definition of **firearm**, **imitation firearm**, **section 1 firearms**, **shotguns**, **air weapons** and **prohibited weapons** (s. 5).
- Know the main **offences of using/possessing firearms with intent** (ss. 16–18), **carrying in a public place** (s. 19), **trespassing** (s. 20) and **persons previously convicted** (s. 21).
- Know the **police powers** (s. 47) and the importance of officer safety.

## Explanation

### 1. Definitions (Firearms Act 1968, s. 57)

- **Firearm**: a **lethal barrelled weapon** of any description from which any **shot, bullet or other missile can be discharged** – including a **prohibited weapon**, any **component part** and any **accessory designed or adapted to diminish the noise or flash**.
- **Lethal barrelled weapon**: capable of discharging a missile with **kinetic energy of more than 1 joule** at the muzzle (⚠ VERIFICAR – Policing and Crime Act 2017).
- **Imitation firearm**: anything which **has the appearance** of a firearm, whether or not capable of discharging anything. (A finger under a coat is **not** a "thing" – *R v Bentham* 2005.)
- **Section 1 firearm**: any firearm **except** a **shotgun** (smooth-bore, barrel ≥ 24 inches, magazine ≤ 2 cartridges ⚠) and an **air weapon** that isn't "specially dangerous" (≤ **6 ft lb** pistol, ≤ **12 ft lb** rifle ⚠). Requires a **firearm certificate** (s. 1). Shotguns require a **shotgun certificate** (s. 2).
- **Prohibited weapons (s. 5)**: e.g. **automatic** weapons, **handguns** (since 1997), weapons designed for the discharge of **noxious liquid, gas or other thing** (e.g. **CS/pepper spray** and **stun guns** ⚠), disguised firearms, and some ammunition. Possession without Home Office authority: max **10 years**, with a **minimum sentence of 5 years** for many (s. 51A) ⚠.

:::es
**Arma de fuego (s. 57)**: arma **con cañón y letal** que puede disparar un proyectil (> **1 julio** ⚠); incluye **armas prohibidas**, **piezas** y **silenciadores**. **Imitación**: cualquier **cosa** que **parezca** un arma (un dedo bajo el abrigo **no** – *Bentham*). **Arma s. 1**: todas salvo **escopetas** y **armas de aire** de baja potencia; necesitan **licencia**. **Prohibidas (s. 5)**: automáticas, **pistolas** (desde 1997), **sprays CS**, **táseres**, armas camufladas; máx. **10 años**, pena mínima de **5** en muchos casos ⚠.
:::

### 2. Offences with intent (all max **life**, except s. 16A)

| Section | Offence |
|---|---|
| s. 16 | **Possession** of a firearm or ammunition **with intent to endanger life** (or enable another to) |
| s. 16A | **Possession** of a firearm **or imitation** with intent to **cause someone to believe unlawful violence will be used** – max **10 years** |
| s. 17(1) | **Using** a firearm **or imitation** with intent to **resist or prevent arrest** |
| s. 17(2) | **Possessing** a firearm **or imitation** at the time of **committing**, or **being arrested** for, a **Schedule 1 offence** (e.g. robbery, burglary, theft, wounding, rape…) |
| s. 18 | **Having** a firearm **or imitation** with intent to **commit an indictable offence** or **resist arrest** |

### 3. Carrying in a public place (s. 19)

A person commits an offence if, **without lawful authority or reasonable excuse** (proof on him), he has with him **in a public place**:
- a **loaded shotgun**;
- an **air weapon** (loaded or not);
- any **other firearm** (loaded or not) **together with ammunition** suitable for use in it;
- an **imitation firearm**.

Max **7 years** (12 months for air weapons and imitations ⚠).

### 4. Trespassing (s. 20) and previous convictions (s. 21)

- **s. 20**: entering or being in a **building** (max 7 yrs ⚠) or **on land** (summary) as a **trespasser** while having a firearm or imitation, without reasonable excuse.
- **s. 21**: a person sentenced to **3 years or more** custody must **never** possess a firearm or ammunition; **3 months to under 3 years** – not for **5 years** from release ⚠.

:::es
**Con intención (perpetua salvo s. 16A)**: **s. 16** poner en peligro la vida; **s. 16A** hacer temer violencia (10 años); **s. 17(1)** usarla para **resistirse a la detención**; **s. 17(2)** tenerla al cometer o ser detenido por un delito del **Anexo 1**; **s. 18** con intención de cometer un delito *indictable*. **s. 19** llevar en **lugar público** sin excusa: escopeta **cargada**, **arma de aire** (cargada o no), otra arma **con munición**, **imitación**. **s. 20** entrar armado como **intruso**. **s. 21** condenados a ≥ 3 años: **nunca**; 3 meses–3 años: **5 años** desde la salida.
:::

### 5. Police powers (s. 47) and safety

- **s. 47(1)**: a constable may require any person he has **reasonable cause to suspect** of having a firearm (with or without ammunition) **in a public place**, or committing/about to commit s. 18/s. 20 offences elsewhere, to **hand over** the firearm/ammunition for examination. Failing to hand over is an offence.
- **s. 47(3)–(4)**: to exercise this power, he may **search** the person and **detain** them for the search, and **stop and search a vehicle**.
- **s. 46**: search warrants for firearms.
- **Officer safety**: contain, don't approach armed suspects without authorised firearms officers (AFOs); preserve evidence (forensic, firearms residue), use **NABIS** for ballistic intelligence.

:::es
**Poderes (s. 47)**: con **sospecha razonable** de que alguien lleva un arma en **lugar público**, exigir que la entregue, **registrarle**, **retenerle** y registrar **vehículos**. **s. 46** órdenes de registro. **Seguridad**: no acercarse sin agentes armados (AFO); conservar pruebas; **NABIS**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The robbery.** D robs a shop waving a BB-gun replica. → **Robbery** + **s. 17(2)** (imitation at time of Sch. 1 offence) and/or **s. 18**.
2. **The park.** Youth in a park with an unloaded air rifle. → **s. 19** (air weapon, loaded or not) – unless reasonable excuse.
3. **The pepper spray.** Tourist with a CS/pepper spray in her bag. → **s. 5 prohibited weapon** (noxious substance).
4. **The finger.** Robber pretends his finger in his pocket is a gun. → **Not** an imitation firearm (*Bentham*) – still robbery.
5. **The ex-prisoner.** Released 2 years ago after a 4-year sentence, found with a shotgun. → **s. 21** – lifetime ban.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- **Imitation** counts for **ss. 16A, 17, 18, 19, 20** but **not** for **s. 16** (endanger life).
- s. 19: **air weapon** – loaded or not; **other firearm** – only with **ammunition**; **shotgun** – only if **loaded**.
- s. 17(2) needs a **Schedule 1** offence.
- s. 21: ≥ 3 years = **never**; 3 months–3 years = **5 years**.

## Summary

1. Firearm = lethal barrelled weapon (incl. parts/silencers); imitation = has the appearance.
2. s. 1 firearms (certificate), shotguns (s. 2), air weapons, prohibited weapons (s. 5).
3. ss. 16–18 intent offences (life; s. 16A 10 yrs); s. 19 public place; s. 20 trespass; s. 21 previous convictions.
4. s. 47 powers: require handover, search, detain, stop vehicles.
