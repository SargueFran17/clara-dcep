## Objectives

- Know the definition of **terrorism** (Terrorism Act 2000, s. 1).
- Know offences relating to **proscribed organisations** (ss. 11–13), **terrorist property** (ss. 15–18), **failure to disclose** (ss. 19, 38B), **collection of information** (s. 58) and **preparation** of terrorist acts (TA 2006, s. 5).
- Know the police **powers**: arrest (s. 41), stop and search (ss. 43, 43A, 47A), **Schedule 7**, and the **Prevent** duty.

## Explanation

### 1. Definition (TA 2000, s. 1)

**Terrorism** = the **use or threat of action** where:
- the action involves **serious violence** against a person, **serious damage to property**, **endangers a person's life** (other than the person committing it), creates a **serious risk to the health or safety of the public**, or is designed seriously to **interfere with or disrupt an electronic system**;
- the use or threat is **designed to influence the government** (or an international governmental organisation) **or to intimidate the public** or a section of it; **and**
- it is made **for the purpose of advancing a political, religious, racial or ideological cause**.

If it involves **firearms or explosives**, it's terrorism **even without** the "influence/intimidate" element. "Action" includes action **outside the UK**.

:::es
**Terrorismo (TA 2000 s. 1)**: usar o amenazar con **violencia grave**, **daños graves**, poner en peligro **vidas**, **riesgo grave** para la salud pública o **sabotaje informático**, para **influir en el gobierno o intimidar al público**, con una causa **política, religiosa, racial o ideológica**. Con **armas de fuego o explosivos** no hace falta lo de influir/intimidar. Incluye actos **fuera** del Reino Unido.
:::

### 2. Proscribed organisations (ss. 11–13)

- **s. 11 – membership**: belongs or professes to belong (defence: it wasn't proscribed when he joined and he hasn't taken part since) – max **14 years**.
- **s. 12 – support**: **invites support**, arranges/manages/addresses a **meeting** (3+ people) knowing it is to support or further the organisation's activities, or **expresses an opinion or belief supportive** of a proscribed organisation **being reckless** whether a person will be encouraged to support it (since 2019).
- **s. 13 – uniform and publication of images**: in a **public place**, **wears clothing** or **wears, carries or displays an article** in such a way as to arouse **reasonable suspicion** that he is a **member or supporter**; or **publishes an image** of such an item (2019). Summary; police may **seize** the item.

### 3. Terrorist property (ss. 15–18) and duties to disclose

- **s. 15** fund-raising; **s. 16** use and possession; **s. 17** funding arrangements; **s. 18** money laundering of terrorist property (max **14 years**).
- **s. 19** – **duty to disclose** (as soon as reasonably practicable) a belief or suspicion that another has committed a ss. 15–18 offence, based on information that came to his attention **in the course of a trade, profession, business or employment**.
- **s. 38B** – failure to disclose information which he knows or believes **might be of material assistance** in **preventing an act of terrorism** or securing the **apprehension/prosecution** of a person for terrorism – max **5 years**. (Applies to everyone – not only employment.)

### 4. Other offences

- **s. 58 TA 2000 – collection of information**: collects, makes a record of, possesses, or **views via the internet**, information **likely to be useful** to a person committing or preparing an act of terrorism. Defence: **reasonable excuse** (e.g. journalism, academic research). Max **15 years**.
- **TA 2006**: **s. 1** encouragement of terrorism; **s. 2** dissemination of terrorist publications; **s. 5** **preparation of terrorist acts** (any conduct in preparation, with intention to commit or assist acts of terrorism) – max **life**; **s. 6** training for terrorism.

:::es
**Organizaciones prohibidas**: **s. 11** ser **miembro** (14 años); **s. 12** **pedir apoyo**, organizar reuniones o **expresar apoyo** con imprudencia; **s. 13** llevar **ropa o símbolos** en público que hagan sospechar que es miembro o simpatizante, o publicar su imagen. **Bienes terroristas** (ss. 15–18). **s. 19** deber de revelar (por **trabajo**) sospechas de financiación; **s. 38B** no revelar información que podría **evitar un atentado** (5 años; cualquiera). **s. 58** recopilar o **ver en internet** información útil para terroristas. **TA 2006 s. 5**: **preparar** actos terroristas (perpetua).
:::

### 5. Police powers

- **s. 41 – arrest**: a constable may arrest **without warrant** a person whom he **reasonably suspects to be a terrorist** (s. 40: committed certain offences or is/has been concerned in the commission, preparation or instigation of acts of terrorism). Detention up to **48 hours** from arrest, then **warrants of further detention** up to **14 days** in total ⚠ (Schedule 8: review by an officer of at least **inspector** rank; after 24 h, **superintendent** ⚠).
- **s. 43** – stop and search a person **reasonably suspected** to be a terrorist (anything that may constitute evidence); **s. 43A** – vehicles.
- **s. 47A** – **no suspicion** needed: authorisation by an officer of at least **assistant chief constable** rank who **reasonably suspects** an act of terrorism **will take place** and considers the authorisation **necessary** to prevent it; area and duration **no greater than necessary**, max **14 days**; must be **confirmed by the Secretary of State within 48 hours** or it lapses. Constable in **uniform**; search for evidence that the person/vehicle is or has been used for terrorism.
- **Schedule 7** – **port and border** examinations: examining officers may stop, question and detain persons to determine whether they appear to be concerned in terrorism – **no suspicion required**; max examination **6 hours** ⚠; duty to answer questions; right to consult a solicitor.
- **Cordons** (s. 33; superintendent, or lower in urgency).

### 6. Prevent and public safety

- **Prevent duty** (Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015, s. 26): specified authorities (incl. police) must have **due regard to the need to prevent people from being drawn into terrorism**; **Channel** panels give multi-agency support to people at risk of radicalisation.
- **CONTEST** strategy: **Prevent, Pursue, Protect, Prepare**.
- Public advice for attacks: **Run, Hide, Tell**.

:::es
**Poderes**: **s. 41** detener sin orden a un sospechoso de terrorismo; detención hasta **48 h** y luego, con orden judicial, hasta **14 días** ⚠. **s. 43** registro con **sospecha razonable**; **s. 47A** registro **sin sospecha** autorizado por un **assistant chief constable** (máx. **14 días**, confirmación del **ministro en 48 h**). **Anexo 7**: controles en **puertos** sin sospecha (máx. 6 h ⚠). **Prevent** (CTSA 2015 s. 26), **Channel**; estrategia **CONTEST** (Prevent, Pursue, Protect, Prepare); **Run, Hide, Tell**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The flag.** At a march, a man carries a flag of a proscribed organisation. → **s. 13** (arouses reasonable suspicion of support); seize the flag.
2. **The bank clerk.** A bank employee suspects a customer's transfers fund a terrorist group. → **s. 19** duty to disclose (employment).
3. **The neighbour.** Someone knows their brother plans an attack next week but says nothing. → **s. 38B** failure to disclose.
4. **Bomb manual.** Suspect downloads and repeatedly views a bomb-making guide. → **s. 58** (viewing via internet) – unless reasonable excuse.
5. **Imminent threat.** Intelligence suggests an attack at a city event. → **s. 47A** authorisation (ACC; SoS within 48 h).

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- Firearms/explosives → no need to show intent to influence/intimidate.
- s. 19 relates to info obtained **in the course of employment** about **terrorist property** offences; s. 38B applies to **anyone** about **acts of terrorism**.
- s. 47A: **ACC** authorises; **14 days** max; **SoS confirms within 48 hours**; **no suspicion** for the stop.
- Schedule 7: **ports**, no suspicion needed.
- s. 41 detention: up to **14 days** with warrants ⚠.

## Summary

1. s. 1 TA 2000: action + designed to influence government/intimidate public + political, religious, racial or ideological cause.
2. Proscribed organisations: s. 11 membership, s. 12 support, s. 13 uniform/images.
3. Terrorist property ss. 15–18; disclosure duties s. 19 and s. 38B; s. 58 collection; TA 2006 s. 5 preparation.
4. Powers: s. 41 arrest, ss. 43/43A, s. 47A, Schedule 7, cordons.
5. Prevent duty, Channel, CONTEST.
