> ⚠ **Comprueba con tu edición 2027.** La PCSCA 2022 subió a **cadena perpetua** el causing death by dangerous driving y creó el **causing serious injury by careless driving**; confirma penas con el manual.

## Objectives

- Know the tests for **dangerous** (RTA 1988, s. 2A) and **careless/inconsiderate** driving (s. 3ZA).
- Know the offences **causing death or serious injury** by driving (ss. 1, 1A, 2B, 2C, 3A, 3ZB, 3ZC, 3ZD).
- Know the **Notice of Intended Prosecution** rules (RTOA 1988, ss. 1–2) and the **duty to stop and report** (RTA 1988, s. 170).

## Explanation

### 1. Dangerous driving (RTA 1988, s. 2 and s. 2A)

A person drives **dangerously** if:
- the way he drives **falls far below** what would be expected of a **competent and careful driver**, **and**
- it would be **obvious** to a competent and careful driver that driving in that way would be **dangerous**; **or**
- it would be obvious to a competent and careful driver that driving the vehicle **in its current state** would be dangerous (including anything attached or carried).

"Dangerous" = danger of **injury to any person** or of **serious damage to property**. The test is **objective** – D's skill or intentions don't matter, but circumstances **shown to be within D's knowledge** count. Applies on a **road or other public place**; **mechanically propelled vehicle**. s. 2 max **2 years**; disqualification obligatory.

### 2. Careless and inconsiderate driving (s. 3, s. 3ZA)

- **Careless**: the way he drives **falls below** what would be expected of a **competent and careful driver**.
- **Inconsiderate**: driving **without reasonable consideration for other persons** – only if those persons are **inconvenienced** by the driving (e.g. driving through a puddle to splash pedestrians).
- Summary; fine (unlimited ⚠) and penalty points.

:::es
**Conducción peligrosa (s. 2A)**: conducir **muy por debajo** de lo que haría un conductor **competente y cuidadoso**, y sería **obvio** para él que es **peligroso** (riesgo de **lesiones** o **daños graves**); también por el **estado del vehículo**. Test **objetivo**. **Conducción descuidada**: **por debajo** de ese estándar; **desconsiderada**: sin consideración por los demás, que se ven **perjudicados**. En **carretera u otro lugar público**.
:::

### 3. Causing death or serious injury (⚠ penalties)

| Section | Offence | Max |
|---|---|---|
| s. 1 | Causing **death** by **dangerous** driving | **life** (since 2022) |
| s. 1A | Causing **serious injury** by dangerous driving | 5 yrs |
| s. 2B | Causing death by **careless or inconsiderate** driving | 5 yrs |
| s. 2C | Causing **serious injury** by careless or inconsiderate driving (2022) | 2 yrs |
| s. 3A | Causing death by careless driving **when under the influence** of drink or drugs (or failing to provide a specimen) | **life** |
| s. 3ZB | Causing death by driving **unlicensed or uninsured** | 2 yrs |
| s. 3ZC / s. 3ZD | Causing **death / serious injury** by driving while **disqualified** | 10 / 4 yrs |

- **Serious injury** = physical harm amounting to **GBH**.
- **Causation**: D's driving must be **more than a minimal** cause; for **s. 3ZB**, there must be **some fault** in D's driving beyond simply being on the road (*R v Hughes* 2013, SC).

### 4. Notice of Intended Prosecution (RTOA 1988, s. 1)

For offences including **dangerous**, **careless/inconsiderate** driving and speeding, D shouldn't be convicted unless **one** of these happened:
- he was **warned at the time** of the offence that prosecution would be considered; **or**
- a **summons** (or written charge) was served within **14 days**; **or**
- a **NIP** was served within **14 days** on the **driver** or the **registered keeper**.

**Not required** (s. 2) where, at the time or immediately after, an **accident occurred owing to the presence of the vehicle** on a road (unless D was unaware of it), or for the "causing death/serious injury" offences.

### 5. Duty to stop and report (RTA 1988, s. 170)

Where, owing to the presence of a mechanically propelled vehicle on a **road or other public place**, an accident occurs causing **personal injury** to another person, **damage** to another vehicle, to an **animal** (horse, cattle, ass, mule, sheep, pig, goat or dog) not in that vehicle, or to **roadside property**, the driver must **stop** and, if required, give **name and address**, the owner's name/address and the vehicle's registration. If he doesn't give them at the scene, he must **report** the accident at a police station or to a constable **as soon as reasonably practicable** and in any case **within 24 hours** (in injury cases also produce insurance).

:::es
**Causar la muerte/lesiones graves**: por conducción **peligrosa** (s. 1 – **perpetua** desde 2022; s. 1A – 5 años), **descuidada** (s. 2B – 5; s. 2C – 2), **bajo los efectos** de alcohol/drogas (s. 3A – perpetua), **sin carné o seguro** (s. 3ZB – debe haber **culpa** en la conducción, *Hughes*), **inhabilitado** (ss. 3ZC/3ZD). **NIP**: advertencia en el momento, citación o aviso al conductor o titular en **14 días**; no hace falta si hubo **accidente**. **s. 170**: tras un accidente con heridos o daños, **parar** y dar datos; si no, **denunciarlo** lo antes posible y como mucho en **24 h**.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. **The race.** Two cars race at 90 mph in a 30 zone through a town centre. → **Dangerous** driving (far below; obviously dangerous).
2. **The phone.** A driver glances at a text and rear-ends a stationary car at low speed. → **Careless** driving (below the standard); no NIP needed – **accident**.
3. **The puddle.** A driver deliberately splashes a bus queue. → **Inconsiderate** driving.
4. **The bald tyres.** Car with completely bald tyres and no brakes in the wet. → Dangerous by **current state** of the vehicle.
5. **The uninsured driver.** Uninsured driver, driving perfectly, hits a pedestrian who ran out. → **Not s. 3ZB** without some fault in his driving (*Hughes*).

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- Dangerous = **far below** + **obvious** danger; careless = **below**.
- Inconsiderate driving needs **other persons inconvenienced**.
- NIP: **14 days**; not needed after an **accident** owing to the vehicle's presence.
- s. 170 report: **as soon as reasonably practicable** and **within 24 hours**; dogs are listed animals, cats are not.
- s. 3ZB needs **fault** (*Hughes*).

## Summary

1. s. 2A dangerous: far below + obvious; or vehicle's state.
2. s. 3ZA careless (below) / inconsiderate (others inconvenienced).
3. Causing death/serious injury offences – s. 1 and s. 3A now life ⚠.
4. NIP (RTOA s. 1): warning, summons or notice within 14 days; exceptions (s. 2).
5. s. 170: stop, give details, report within 24 hours.
