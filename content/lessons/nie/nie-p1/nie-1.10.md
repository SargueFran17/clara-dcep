## Objectives

- Know what must happen after an arrest **away from a police station** (PACE s. 30) and when the person can be **released** before reaching one.
- Understand **street bail** (s. 30A) and its **conditions** (s. 30B).
- Know the custody officer's duty to **release** when grounds for detention end (s. 34) and the release options: **bail**, **RUI**, **no further action**.

## Explanation

### 1. Arrest elsewhere than at a police station (s. 30)

- A person arrested away from a police station must be taken to a **police station** (normally a **designated** one if likely to be detained more than 6 hours ⚠) **as soon as practicable** after arrest.
- **Delay** is allowed if the person's presence **elsewhere** is necessary to carry out **investigations reasonably required immediately** (s. 30(10)) – e.g. to search premises under s. 18/s. 32 or to find a weapon; the **reasons** must be recorded on arrival.
- If, before arriving, the officer is satisfied there are **no grounds for keeping the person under arrest**, they **must release** them **without bail** (s. 30(7)) and record it.

:::es
**Tras detener fuera de comisaría (s. 30)**: llevar a la persona a una comisaría **lo antes posible**. Se puede **retrasar** si hace falta su presencia en otro sitio para **diligencias inmediatas** (s. 30(10)), registrando los motivos. Si antes de llegar desaparecen los motivos de la detención, **hay que liberarla sin fianza** (s. 30(7)).
:::

### 2. Street bail (ss. 30A–30D)

- Instead of taking the person to a station, an officer may **release them on bail** to attend a police station **later** (s. 30A).
- **Conditions** (s. 30B) can be imposed if necessary, e.g. to make sure they **surrender**, don't **commit an offence**, don't **interfere with witnesses**, or for their own **protection** – but **not**: a recognisance, a security, a surety, or a requirement to **reside in a bail hostel** (⚠ VERIFICAR lista).
- A **written notice** must be given (offence, grounds of arrest, time/place to attend, conditions).
- Failing to attend / breaching conditions → **arrest** (s. 30D).

:::es
**Street bail (s. 30A)**: en vez de ir a comisaría, se libera a la persona con obligación de **presentarse más tarde**. **Condiciones** (s. 30B) si son necesarias (comparecer, no delinquir, no interferir con testigos, protección), pero **no** fianza económica, avalista ni residir en un albergue. **Aviso por escrito**. Si no comparece o incumple → **detención** (s. 30D).
:::

### 3. Release from police detention

- **s. 34**: the custody officer must **order immediate release** if they become aware that the **grounds for detention have ceased** and there is no other lawful ground – **unless** the person was **unlawfully at large** when arrested (⚠).
- **s. 37**: if there's **sufficient evidence to charge**, charge or release (with or without bail) for a charging decision; if **not**, release (with or without bail) unless detention is necessary (1.7).
- **Pre-charge bail** must be considered where **necessary and proportionate** (e.g. to protect victims); otherwise **released under investigation (RUI)**. The periods and authorisation levels are in **1.4** (⚠ 3/6/9 months).
- **No further action (NFA)**: tell the person in writing when an investigation ends (⚠ VERIFICAR obligación).

:::es
**Liberación**: **s. 34** – si **desaparecen los motivos** de la detención, el custody officer debe **liberar de inmediato** (salvo que estuviera ilegalmente fugado). **s. 37** – con pruebas suficientes: acusar o liberar (con o sin fianza) para decisión de acusación; si no, liberar salvo necesidad. **Fianza previa** si es **necesaria y proporcionada**; si no, **RUI**. Plazos en el capítulo 1.4.
:::

### 4. Bail conditions and breach (pre-charge)

- Conditions possible on police bail (s. 47(1A)): e.g. no contact with the victim, residence, curfew, reporting.
- **s. 46A**: power to **arrest without warrant** a person who **fails to attend** a police station as required by bail, or where there are reasonable grounds to suspect they've **broken bail conditions**.
- Breach of pre-charge bail conditions is **not itself an offence**; failing to surrender to **police** bail without reasonable cause **is** an offence under the Bail Act (⚠ VERIFICAR alcance s. 6).

## Police scenarios

1. **The search on the way.** You arrest a burglar in the street and need to search his car (s. 32) before taking him in. → **Delay** under s. 30(10) – record the reason on arrival.
2. **Wrong person.** On the way to custody, CCTV clearly shows the person you arrested wasn't involved. → **Release without bail** (s. 30(7)) and record it.
3. **Street bail.** Minor shoplifting suspect, address verified, no risks. → **Street bail** to attend the station next week for interview.
4. **Domestic case.** Suspect released pending forensics; victim fears contact. → **Pre-charge bail** with a no-contact condition, not RUI.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- s. 30(10) delay must be for investigations **reasonably required immediately**.
- s. 30(7): release **without bail** if no grounds remain before arrival.
- **Street bail conditions** can't include a surety, security, recognisance or bail hostel residence.
- **s. 46A** arrest for failing to answer police bail / breach of conditions.
- **Necessary and proportionate** test for pre-charge bail.

## Summary

1. Arrest away from station → to a station ASAP (s. 30); delay for immediate investigations (s. 30(10)).
2. No grounds before arrival → release without bail (s. 30(7)).
3. Street bail (s. 30A) with limited conditions (s. 30B); arrest for failure/breach (s. 30D).
4. s. 34 release when grounds cease; s. 37 decisions; bail vs RUI (necessary & proportionate).
5. s. 46A arrest for failing to answer police bail or breaching conditions.
