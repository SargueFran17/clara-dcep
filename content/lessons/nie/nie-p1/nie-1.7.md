> ⚠ **Comprueba con tu edición 2027 y con el texto vigente de PACE Code C.** Es el capítulo más largo y con más **números** (horas, rangos, intervalos). Aquí tienes la estructura y las reglas principales; cada cifra, contrastada con el manual.

## Objectives

- Understand the **custody officer's** role, the decision to **authorise detention** (s. 37) and the **relevant time**.
- Master the **time limits**: **reviews** (s. 40), **24 hours** (s. 41), **superintendent's extension** (s. 42) and **warrants of further detention** (ss. 43–44).
- Know the detainee's **rights** (someone informed – s. 56; legal advice – s. 58), when they can be **delayed** and by whom, and the rules on **conditions, searches, vulnerable people and juveniles**.

## Explanation

### 1. Arrival: the custody officer and s. 37

The **custody officer** (normally at least a **sergeant**, independent of the investigation) decides whether there is **sufficient evidence to charge**. If **not**, the person must be **released** (on bail or without) **unless** the custody officer has **reasonable grounds for believing** that detention without charge is **necessary** to:

- **secure or preserve evidence** relating to the offence, or
- **obtain such evidence by questioning** the person (s. 37(2)).

The custody officer opens the **custody record**, carries out a **risk assessment**, and tells the detainee their **rights**.

:::es
El **custody officer** (normalmente **sargento**, independiente de la investigación) decide si hay **pruebas suficientes para acusar**. Si no, debe **liberar**, **salvo** que crea razonablemente que la detención es **necesaria** para **asegurar o preservar pruebas** u **obtenerlas mediante interrogatorio** (s. 37(2)). Abre el **custody record**, hace la **evaluación de riesgos** e informa de los **derechos**.
:::

### 2. The relevant time (s. 41(2)) – when the clock starts

Generally the **detention clock** starts at the **earlier** of:
- the time the person **arrives at the first police station** after arrest; or
- **24 hours after the arrest**.

Special rules (⚠ VERIFICAR detalles): a person who **attended voluntarily** and is then **arrested** at the station – relevant time = **time of arrest**; arrests in **another force area** or **outside England and Wales** have specific rules.

:::es
El **reloj** empieza normalmente con lo **primero** que ocurra: **llegada a la primera comisaría** tras la detención o **24 h después de la detención**. Si alguien vino **voluntariamente** y le detienen allí, cuenta desde **la detención**. Hay reglas especiales para detenciones en otra fuerza o fuera de Inglaterra y Gales (⚠).
:::

### 3. The time limits – the backbone of the chapter

| Stage | Rule (general) | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Reviews before charge** (s. 40) | **1st** review **not later than 6 hours** after detention was **first authorised**; then at intervals of **not more than 9 hours** | **Inspector** not directly involved in the investigation (the **review officer**) |
| **Basic limit** (s. 41) | **24 hours** from the **relevant time** | – |
| **Extension to 36 h** (s. 42) | for an **indictable** offence; detention necessary to secure/preserve/obtain evidence; investigation conducted **diligently and expeditiously**; given **before** the 24 hours expire (⚠ and normally after the second review) | **Superintendent** (or above) responsible for the station |
| **Warrant of further detention** (s. 43) | from a **magistrates' court**, up to **36 more hours** | magistrates |
| **Extension of warrant** (s. 44) | total **maximum 96 hours** from the relevant time | magistrates |

The detainee (or solicitor) can make **representations** at reviews and extensions. Reviews **after charge** are done by the **custody officer**.

:::es
**Plazos clave**:
- **Revisiones** (s. 40): la **1ª no más tarde de 6 h** desde que se **autorizó** la detención; después, cada **9 h como máximo**; las hace un **inspector no implicado**.
- **Límite básico** (s. 41): **24 h** desde el *relevant time*.
- **Prórroga a 36 h** (s. 42): **superintendente**, delito **indictable**, investigación **diligente y rápida**, antes de que venzan las 24 h.
- **Orden judicial** (s. 43): hasta **36 h más**; **ampliación** (s. 44): **máximo total 96 h**.
:::

### 4. Rights – and their delay

| Right | Delay possible? | Authorised by | Max delay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Have someone informed** of the arrest (s. 56) | yes, for an **indictable** offence on specified grounds | **Inspector** (or above) | **36 hours** from relevant time |
| **Consult a solicitor** privately and free (s. 58) | yes, for an **indictable** offence on specified grounds | **Superintendent** (or above) | **36 hours** from relevant time |
| **Consult the Codes of Practice** | – | – | – |

**Grounds** for delay (in summary): reasonable grounds for believing that exercising the right will lead to **interference with or harm to evidence** or **other people**, **alerting other suspects**, or **hindering the recovery of property** (plus some proceeds-of-crime grounds). Delay must end as soon as the reason stops. If legal advice has been **delayed**, the **restricted caution** applies in interview (no adverse inferences – SE4).

:::es
**Derechos**: **aviso a alguien** (s. 56) – se puede **retrasar** con autorización de **inspector**; **abogado** (s. 58) – retraso con autorización de **superintendente**; ambos solo en delitos **indictable**, por motivos tasados (perjudicar pruebas o personas, alertar a cómplices, dificultar recuperar bienes) y **máximo 36 h**. Si se retrasa el abogado, en la entrevista se usa la **caution restringida**.
:::

### 5. Conditions of detention (Code C, sections 8–9 – ⚠ VERIFICAR)

- Cells **adequately heated, cleaned, ventilated and lit**; normally **one detainee per cell** where practicable.
- **At least two light meals and one main meal** in any **24 hours**; **drinks** with meals and on reasonable request.
- In any 24 hours, a continuous period of at least **8 hours' rest**, free from questioning or travel, normally at night.
- **Visits**: detainees checked at least **every hour**; those who are **intoxicated** should be **visited and roused** at least **every half hour** (Annex H).
- **Health care**: call a **healthcare professional** if the detainee appears ill, injured or mentally disordered; **fitness to be interviewed** (Annex G).

:::es
**Condiciones** (⚠ verifica): celdas limpias, calientes, ventiladas e iluminadas; **2 comidas ligeras + 1 principal** cada 24 h; **8 h de descanso** seguido; **visitas cada hora**, y si está **ebrio**, **despertarle cada media hora**; **profesional sanitario** si está enfermo, herido o con trastorno mental; valorar si está **apto para la entrevista** (Annex G).
:::

### 6. Vulnerable people, juveniles, interpreters

- **Juvenile** = anyone who **appears to be under 18** (in the absence of clear evidence otherwise). They need an **appropriate adult** (AA), and the person responsible for their welfare must be informed.
- **Vulnerable** adults (e.g. mental disorder, difficulty understanding) also need an **AA**. The AA should be present for **rights, cautions, interviews, charging** and certain searches.
- **Interpreters** for people who don't speak/understand English or who are deaf/speech impaired.

### 7. Searches of detainees

- **s. 54**: the custody officer is responsible for **ascertaining** what a detainee has with them; a search can be authorised to the extent necessary. Items can be **seized** if they might be used to cause **harm**, **damage**, **interfere with evidence** or **help escape**, or may be **evidence**.
- **Strip search** (Code C Annex A): only if **necessary** to remove an article the detainee wouldn't be allowed to keep; authorised by the **custody officer**; by officers of the **same sex**; with privacy safeguards (⚠ VERIFICAR las reglas para personas trans).
- **Intimate search** (s. 55 – a search of **body orifices other than the mouth**): authorised by an **inspector** on reasonable grounds for believing the person has concealed (a) an article that could cause **physical injury**, or (b) a **Class A drug** with intent to **supply** or **export**. A **drug offence search** needs the detainee's **written consent** and is done by a **registered medical practitioner or registered nurse**, at a **hospital, surgery or other medical premises** (not a police station).
- **X-rays/ultrasound** (s. 55A) for Class A drugs: **inspector** authority + **written consent**.

:::es
- **s. 54**: el custody officer averigua qué lleva el detenido; puede **incautar** lo que sirva para **hacer daño, dañar, interferir con pruebas o escapar**, o que sea **prueba**.
- **Registro con desnudo** (Annex A): solo si es **necesario** para quitar algo que no puede tener; lo autoriza el **custody officer**; por personas del **mismo sexo** y con intimidad.
- **Registro íntimo** (s. 55, orificios **salvo la boca**): **inspector**, por objetos que puedan **causar lesiones** o **droga de Clase A** para **suministrar o exportar**. El de **drogas** requiere **consentimiento escrito** y lo hace un **médico o enfermero** en **centro sanitario**.
- **Rayos X / ecografía** (s. 55A): **inspector + consentimiento escrito**.
:::

### 8. Interviews and charge (link with 1.9)

- Interviews should normally take place **at a police station** (Code C 11.1) unless delay would lead to interference/harm to evidence or people, alerting others, or hindering recovery of property.
- **After charge**, a detainee should **not** be interviewed about that offence except in limited cases (e.g. to **prevent harm**, to **clear up an ambiguity**, or to put **new information** to them) (⚠ VERIFICAR Code C 16.5).

## Police scenarios

1. **The clock.** SAM is arrested at 10:00 in the street and arrives at the station at 10:40. Detention authorised at 10:50. → **Relevant time 10:40**; **first review by 16:50** (6 h after **authorisation**); 24 h limit at **10:40 next day**.
2. **The extension.** At hour 20, CID need more time for a GBH (indictable) investigation that has been diligent. → A **superintendent** can authorise up to **36 h** from the relevant time.
3. **Delaying legal advice.** A robbery suspect's solicitor is suspected of passing messages to accomplices. → Only a **superintendent** can authorise **delay** of s. 58 on the statutory grounds, max **36 h**; interview uses the **restricted caution**.
4. **The drunk detainee.** A man arrested for drunk and disorderly. → **Visit and rouse** at least **every 30 minutes**; healthcare professional if concerns.
5. **Drugs inside.** Intelligence suggests a suspect has concealed **wraps of crack cocaine for supply** inside his body. → **Intimate drug search**: **inspector** authority + **written consent** + **doctor/nurse** at a **medical** location.

:::es
1. Detenido 10:00, llega 10:40, autorizada 10:50 → *relevant time* **10:40**; 1ª revisión **antes de las 16:50**; límite 24 h: **10:40 del día siguiente**.
2. Prórroga a 36 h → **superintendente**, delito indictable, investigación diligente.
3. Retrasar al abogado → **superintendente**, máx. **36 h**, **caution restringida**.
4. Detenido ebrio → **despertar cada 30 min**.
5. Droga escondida en el cuerpo → registro íntimo: **inspector + consentimiento escrito + médico/enfermero en centro sanitario**.
:::

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- **s. 56 delay = inspector**; **s. 58 delay = superintendent**. Both only for **indictable** offences; both max **36 h**.
- **First review**: **6 h** after detention **first authorised** – **not** from the relevant time or the arrest.
- **Extension to 36 h** requires an **indictable** offence and a **superintendent**; beyond 36 h only **magistrates** (max **96 h**).
- **Juvenile** = **appears under 18**.
- **Intimate search** = orifices **other than the mouth**; drug search needs **consent**, done by **medical staff** at **medical premises**.
- **Drunk detainees**: rouse every **30 minutes**.

## Summary

1. s. 37: charge or release unless detention necessary to secure/preserve/obtain evidence.
2. Relevant time: arrival at station or 24 h after arrest (earlier); voluntary attendees: time of arrest.
3. Reviews: 6 h then 9 h (inspector); 24 h → 36 h (superintendent, indictable) → 96 h max (magistrates).
4. s. 56 delay: inspector; s. 58 delay: superintendent; indictable only; max 36 h.
5. Conditions (meals, 8 h rest, hourly checks, rouse drunk every 30 min); AA for juveniles/vulnerable; s. 54/55/55A searches.
