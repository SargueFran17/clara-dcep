## Objectives

- Use **your 10-step method** for every chapter with the app's checklist.
- Read the Blackstone's **actively**: definitions, points to prove, exceptions and time limits.
- Turn each chapter into **spaced reviews** until the final review.

## Explanation

### Your 10 steps (in the app: NIE → chapter → "Método de 10 pasos")

| # | Step | Suggested time | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3 questions without reading** | 10 min | Your brain looks for the answers while you read (pre-testing). |
| 2 | **1st reading of the manual** | 90 min | General picture. Don't underline everything. |
| 3 | **Workbook** | 45 min | Active practice. |
| 4 | **Recall questions** | 30 min | Remembering is what fixes memory. |
| 5 | **2nd reading** | 60 min | Now you notice the details (ages, times, ranks, exceptions). |
| 6 | **Repeat the 3 initial questions** | 10 min | Compare "before" and "after". |
| 7 | **Audios and videos** | 30 min | Same content through another channel. |
| 8 | **All possible questions** | 60 min | Exam-style practice + **error log**. |
| 9 | **Explain it out loud** | 15 min | Record yourself with the microphone and listen back. |
| 10 | **Write the topic** | 45 min | Your own summary (in the app's editor). |

When you tick step 10, the chapter becomes **"Estudiado"** and the **spaced reviews** start (+1 day, +3 days, +7 days, +1 month, then monthly) until **17 May**. From 17 to 31 May you do the **final review by groups**.

:::es
Cuando marcas el **paso 10**, el capítulo pasa a **Estudiado** y empiezan los **repasos espaciados** (+1d, +3d, +7d, +1 mes y mensual) hasta el **17 de mayo**. Del 17 al 31 de mayo: **repaso final por grupos**. Cada repaso aparece solo en **Hoy**.
:::

### How to read the Blackstone's

When you read a chapter, look for these **five things** and put them in your summary:

1. **The definition** – exact key words (e.g. *"intentionally or recklessly"*, *"knowing or believing"*).
2. **Points to prove** – the elements the prosecution must prove.
3. **Exceptions and defences** – "unless", "except", "it is a defence to show…".
4. **Numbers** – ages, time limits, ranks who authorise, maximum hours.
5. **Key cases** – one line each: *name + what happened + rule*.

:::es
Al leer, busca **cinco cosas**: 1) la **definición** con sus palabras clave; 2) los **puntos a probar**; 3) **excepciones y defensas** ("unless", "except"); 4) **números** (edades, plazos, rangos que autorizan, horas máximas); 5) **casos clave** en una línea.
:::

### Language tips for a Spanish speaker

- Make a **personal list** of legal words you don't know in each chapter (add them as flashcards in the app).
- Watch **modal verbs**: *may* (power, optional) ≠ *must/shall* (duty) ≠ *should* (expected).
- Watch **thresholds**: *suspect* < *believe* < *know*.

:::es
Haz tu **lista de palabras** por capítulo (créalas como flashcards). Ojo a los **verbos modales** (may/must/shall/should) y a los **umbrales** (suspect < believe < know): el examen juega mucho con ellos.
:::

## Police scenarios

1. You finish step 10 of 1.3 on 31/12. → Reviews appear on 01/01, 03/01, 07/01, 31/01, 28/02…
2. In step 8 you fail a question about *Jogee*. → Log the error as **"Concepto no sabido"** and write the rule in your own words.

## Common mistakes & exam traps

- Reading the chapter **three times** instead of **testing yourself** – recall beats re-reading.
- Skipping step 9 because it feels strange. Explaining out loud shows you exactly what you don't understand.
- Not writing the **error rule**: the error log only works if you write the correct rule.

## Summary

1. 10 steps per chapter, with a checklist in the app.
2. Step 10 starts the spaced reviews until 17 May.
3. Read for definitions, points to prove, exceptions, numbers and cases.
4. Make flashcards of new legal words.
5. Every wrong answer → error log + correct rule.
